# The document: sixteen A-CDM milestones, sentences numbered

## Milestone 1: ATC Flight Plan Activated

### Definition

[M1.definition.1] The ICAO flight plan is submitted to ATC.
[M1.definition.2] The Airport CDM Platform is initiated for this flight, and all available information is processed.
### Origin and priority

[M1.origin_and_priority.1] The ATC Flight Plan is submitted by the Aircraft Operator and distributed by the IFPS.
[M1.origin_and_priority.2] All involved ATC units receive the flight plan, including departure and destination aerodromes.
### Timing

[M1.timing.1] Normally this takes place 3 hours before EOBT, however it may be later.
[M1.timing.2] In some cases a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) has been submitted, covering daily or weekly flights.
### Data Quality

[M1.data_quality.1] The ATC Flight Plan corresponds to the airport slot programme.
### Effect

[M1.effect.1] One aircraft turn-round normally includes an arriving and a departing flight, meaning that it will have two related flight plans.
[M1.effect.2] For coordinated airports, the outbound flight is already known.
[M1.effect.3] The flight plan may be used to update certain information such as type of aircraft.
[M1.effect.4] For long distance flights, the ELDT may differ from the airport slot.
[M1.effect.5] For non coordinated airports, the flight plan is used to initiate the outbound flight.
[M1.effect.6] The flight is ready not later than 15 minutes after the planned EOBT.
[M1.effect.7] The DPI process commences the correct messaging with Network Operations (if implemented - see attachment 2 for details).
### Procedures

[M1.procedures.1] To check consistency between ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data and then confirm the flight to the Network Operations and allow further local processing of the flight.
[M1.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify the consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent.
[M1.procedures.3] The AO must provide correct information before this first E-DPI message, in order to feed Network Operations with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration, and first destination data, as early in time as possible.
[M1.procedures.4] The E-DPI message should not be sent if no or inconsistent information is provided.
[M1.procedures.5] This process is triggered by:
[M1.procedures.6] - The first activation of the ATC Flight Plan (earliest EOBT-3 hr), or
[M1.procedures.7] - New or late submissions of the ATC Flight Plan, after cancellation or revised EOBT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M1.operational_status.1] INITIATED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M1.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT and EIBT updated for an arrival EOBT and ETOT updated for a departure The DPI process commences (if implemented - see section 3.7.3 for details).
## Milestone 2: EOBT - 2 hr

### Definition

[M2.definition.1] At EOBT-2 hr most flights will be known in the Airport CDM Platform including if they are regulated or not.
[M2.definition.2] All regulated flights receive a CTOT from Network Operations.
### Origin and priority

[M2.origin_and_priority.1] The CTOT is issued by the Network Operations and is sent to relevant ATS units as well as the departure aerodrome.
[M2.origin_and_priority.2] CTOT flights usually have a priority over unregulated flights.
### Timing

[M2.timing.1] If the flight is regulated, a CTOT is issued at EOBT-2h.
### Data Quality

[M2.data_quality.1] Not applicable.
### Effect

[M2.effect.1] For inbound flights, ELDT is updated based on information provided by the FUM messages, taking into account the actual progress of the flight.
### Procedures

[M2.procedures.1] To check (before or after take off from outstation) whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan and to inform Network Operations about the updated take off time estimate, using a T-DPI Message.
[M2.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimated off block time at EOBT-2 hrs.
[M2.procedures.3] At EOBT-2 hrs Network Operations is informed through the first T-DPI message.
[M2.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT, if later than EOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.5] In the case of manual input of TOBT, this estimate will override the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.6] This procedure is triggered by
[M2.procedures.7] - a time stamp, at EOBT - 2h.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M2.operational_status.1] N. A.
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M2.action_on_cdm.1] ETOT/TTOT/CTOT Mark appropriate fields as REGULATED
## Milestone 3: Take Off from Outstation

### Definition

[M3.definition.1] The ATOT from the outstation (ADEP)
### Origin and priority

[M3.origin_and_priority.1] The outstation provides ATOT to the Network Operations and Aircraft Operator.
### Timing

[M3.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M3.data_quality.1] The accuracy of ATOT is +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M3.effect.1] If the departure airport is more than 3hrs flying time from the destination airport the ATOT is received from either the Network Operations FUM or via the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler.
[M3.effect.2] Using the ATOT an ELDT can be calculated by using the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL.
[M3.effect.3] If the flight is within 3hrs flying time of the destination airport the Network Operations monitors progress of the flight using the ETFMS and send a Flight Update Message (FUM) that provides updates of the flight's progress.
### Procedures

[M3.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH estimated landing time after take off from outstation are consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan, and when needed inform the Network Operations about the updated take off time estimates using a T-DPI-t Message.
[M3.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan at take off from outstation.
[M3.procedures.3] A TTOT tolerance of 5 minutes is respected before Network Operations is informed of the updated TTOT.
[M3.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.5] In case EOBT is later than EIBT+MTTT, TTOT equals EOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.6] In the case where TOBT is available this prediction will overrule the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.7] This process is triggered by
[M3.procedures.8] - the take off from outstation.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M3.operational_status.1] AIRBORNE
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M3.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 4: Local Radar Update

### Definition

[M4.definition.1] The flight enters the FIR (Flight Information Region) or the local airspace of the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M4.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from the Area Control Centre (ACC) or Approach Control Unit that is associated with an airport.
[M4.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system is able to detect a flight based upon the assigned SSR code when the flight crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary.
### Timing

[M4.timing.1] Dependent upon the position of the airport in relation to the FIR boundary.
### Data Quality

[M4.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M4.effect.1] Update of the ELDT can trigger a new TOBT to be entered by the AO/GH, or calculated automatically by the Airport CDM Platform.
[M4.effect.2] The accuracy of ELDT is particularly important at this stage since downstream decisions are taken, such as stand /gate / aircraft changes, preparation of arrival sequence, preparation of ground handling operations, decisions for connecting passengers.
[M4.effect.3] Uncertainty and ELDT non-accuracy at this stage significantly increase risks for bad and last minute decisions and internal disruptions.
[M4.effect.4] The objective to decrease the number of stand and gate changes in the last 30 minutes requires high accuracy regarding departure and arrival times.
[M4.effect.5] Therefore, taking into account the taxi-in time (EXIT), any change to a stand or gate is not preferred after ELDT-30'.
[M4.effect.6] The update of TOBT for the related departing flight takes place following this milestone.
[M4.effect.7] Decisions such as the turn-round period, connecting passengers etc are taken and need to be stable at this event.
[M4.effect.8] An estimated in-block time (EIBT) is computed using the ELDT and the estimated taxi-in time.
### Procedures

[M4.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M4.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M4.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M4.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M4.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M4.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M4.operational_status.1] FIR
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M4.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 5: Final Approach

### Definition

[M5.definition.1] The flight enters the Final Approach phase at the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M5.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from ATC.
[M5.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system detects a flight based upon the assigned SSR code and identifies when the flight crosses either a defined range / position or passes/leaves a predetermined level.
### Timing

[M5.timing.1] Dependent upon local parameters that are defined by ATC.
### Data Quality

[M5.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M5.effect.1] Update of the ELDT to determine a new TOBT.
[M5.effect.2] When a flight reaches this stage it is usually between 2 and 5 minutes from landing (depending on the parameter set by ATC).
[M5.effect.3] This is often the prompt for many partners to start moving resources connected with the flight, such as positioning a parking marshal and ground handling services.
### Procedures

[M5.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M5.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M5.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M5.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M5.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M5.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M5.operational_status.1] FINAL
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M5.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 6: Landed

### Definition

[M6.definition.1] ALDT - Actual Landing Time.
[M6.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft touches down on a runway.
[M6.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATA - Actual Time of Arrival landing, ACARS=ON).
### Origin and priority

[M6.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or by ACARS from equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M6.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M6.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M6.effect.1] The occurrence of ALDT triggers an update of downstream estimates: TOBT and TTOT are updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the defined turn-round period for the departing flight.
[M6.effect.2] The EIBT can be updated according to the ALDT +EXIT.
### Procedures

[M6.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M6.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M6.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M6.procedures.6] - Actual Landing Time: ALDT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M6.operational_status.1] LANDED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M6.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT changes to ALDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated

## Milestone 9: Final Confirmation of the TOBT

### Definition

[M9.definition.1] The time at which the Aircraft Operator or Ground Handler provide their most accurate TOBT taking into account the operational situation.
### Origin and priority

[M9.origin_and_priority.1] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler provides the information.
### Timing

[M9.timing.1] The information is provided t minutes before EOBT (t is a parameter time agreed locally).
### Data Quality

[M9.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M9.effect.1] The aim of the final TOBT is to give a timely, accurate and reliable assessment of the off-block time.
[M9.effect.2] It is recognised that main benefits of sharing the TOBT are expected in case of disruptions (internal or external).
[M9.effect.3] In such cases, the difference between EOBT (shared by ATC, Network Operations and Stand / Gate Management) and TOBT may be important.
[M9.effect.4] An accurate TOBT at [EOBT-t minutes] is a pre-requisite for ATC to establish a push back / pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.5] Emphasis is put on the need for the Aircraft Operator to integrate his own strategy to compute a TOBT related to the flight.
[M9.effect.6] Following the receipt of the TOBT, the ATC system will calculate and provide the Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) based on the predicted traffic load, gate / stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the Holding Position, etc. The flight is introduced into the pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.7] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M9.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M9.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M9.procedures.3] This check should be performed at a predefined time (local parameter) to confirm TOBT prior to TSAT issue and verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimates given the updated TOBT.
[M9.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M9.procedures.5] This Milestone Process is actually constantly applicable in the CDM Platform, as soon as a TOBT is available.
[M9.procedures.6] However the confirmed TOBT prior to TSAT has special status, where AO/GH check the quality of TOBT before TSAT issue.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M9.operational_status.1] IN-BLOCK
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M9.action_on_cdm.1] This process is triggered by - a new TOBT or TTOT update.
[M9.action_on_cdm.2] No need to confirm an existing TOBT if it has been manually modified before.

### Definition

[M10.definition.1] The time ATC issues the Target Start Up Approval Time
### Origin and priority

[M10.origin_and_priority.1] ATC
### Timing

[M10.timing.1] The information is provided t-minutes before EOBT, where t is a parameter agreed locally
### Data Quality

[M10.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M10.effect.1] The flight is stabilised into the pre-departure sequence.
[M10.effect.2] The Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M10.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant partners of the TSAT that has been allocated to the flight.
[M10.procedures.2] The Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.3] Second step: To check whether the number of TOBT updates exceeds a tolerance defined locally, after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.4] First: The TSAT will indicate to the partners the time when the start up approval can be expected.
[M10.procedures.5] Network Operations will be informed with a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.6] No check is performed.
[M10.procedures.7] Second: A check shall be performed to see the number of TOBT updates after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.8] In case the number of TOBT updates exceeds a threshold, then the TOBT input should be processed according to local procedure.
[M10.procedures.9] This process is triggered by
[M10.procedures.10] - A defined time (local parameter) before TOBT
[M10.procedures.11] - TOBT update after TSAT issue

[M11.definition.2] This is not to be confused with the time passengers are pre-called to the gate via flight information display systems (FIDS) or public address systems.
### Origin and priority

[M11.origin_and_priority.1] Automatic from airport system or manual input by Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M11.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M11.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M11.effect.1] When boarding commences it gives the Airport CDM Partners a good indication of whether the TOBT/TSAT will be respected.
### Procedures

[M11.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT).
[M11.procedures.2] Second step: To check whether boarding starts in time to respect TOBT and inform the AO/GH in case TOBT needs to be updated.
[M11.procedures.3] Inform of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) when it occurs.
[M11.procedures.4] At a certain time before TOBT (local variable e.g. corresponding to aircraft type) a check shall be performed to check the boarding status.
[M11.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M11.procedures.6] - a time variable <value> minutes before TOBT.
### Operational Status (changes to)

## Milestone 12: Aircraft Ready

### Definition

[M12.definition.1] The time when all doors are closed, boarding bridge removed, push back vehicle connected, ready to taxi immediately upon reception of TWR instructions (ARDT).
### Origin and priority

[M12.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by the Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M12.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M12.data_quality.1] Data is directly available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M12.effect.1] ATC refines the pre-departure sequence.
[M12.effect.2] The pilot requests start up just before TSAT, following coordination with the Ground Handler.
[M12.effect.3] (Dispatcher / Supervisor / Redcap).
### Procedures

[M12.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Ready Time (ARDT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft is ready for start up / push-back.
[M12.procedures.2] Second step: To inform the AO/GH that TOBT has passed and the Airport CDM Platform has not yet received ARDT or Ready Status (RDY).
[M12.procedures.3] Inform of ARDT or RDY confirming that the flight follows the indicated TOBT.
[M12.procedures.4] At TOBT + tolerance the AO/GH are informed that TOBT has passed and there has not been a ready status message yet.
[M12.procedures.5] This procedure is triggered by
[M12.procedures.6] - an input to the Airport CDM Platform.

## Milestone 15: Off-Block

### Definition

[M15.definition.1] AOBT - Actual Off-Block Time.
[M15.definition.2] The time the aircraft pushes back/vacates the parking position (Equivalent to Airline/Handler ATD - Actual Time of Departure ACARS=OUT).
### Origin and priority

[M15.origin_and_priority.1] ACARS equipped aircraft or automated docking systems or ATC systems (e.g. A-SMGCS) or by manual input.
### Timing

[M15.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M15.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M15.effect.1] TTOT updated considering the EXOT.
### Procedures

[M15.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Off-Block Time (AOBT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has commenced push-back / taxi from parking position.
[M15.procedures.2] Second step: To check if TTOT changes by more than the agreed tolerance and inform Network Operations.
[M15.procedures.3] Inform of AOBT when it occurs.
[M15.procedures.4] AOBT always triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations or in the case of remote holding at a defined time prior to TTOT.
[M15.procedures.5] After a first A-DPI is sent this check shall be performed to check TTOT updates against the TTOT tolerance before Network Operations is informed, with a new A-DPI, of the updated TTOT.
[M15.procedures.6] This process is triggered by AOBT detection.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M15.operational_status.1] OFF-BLOCK
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M15.action_on_cdm.1] AOBT recorded
## Milestone 16: Take Off

### Definition

[M16.definition.1] ATOT - Actual Take Off Time.
[M16.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft takes off from the runway.
[M16.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATD-Actual Time of Departure, ACARS = OFF).
### Origin and priority

[M16.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or from ACARS equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M16.timing.1] The information is directly available as soon as possible after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M16.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M16.effect.1] FSA and MVT messages are sent.
### Procedures

[M16.procedures.1] To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners about the actual take off.
[M16.procedures.2] An airborne message is generated and the flight is removed from the departure sequence.
[M16.procedures.3] This process is triggered by Tower FDPS, A-SMGCS / Radar detection or ACARS.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M16.operational_status.1] DEPARTED / TAKE OFF
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M16.action_on_cdm.1] ATOT recorded

# The model

class TurnRoundOperator (package Stakeholders): A joint role, referred to throughout the document as AO/GH, that manages the aircraft turn-round and provides TOBT-related updates to the Airport CDM process.
  attribute name: String; Name or identifier of the operator or handler organisation.
  operation provideTOBT(flight: Flight, time: DateTime): OffBlockTime; AO/GH provides or inserts a TOBT value, as referenced across milestones 4-9.
  operation updateTOBT(flight: Flight): void; TOBT is updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the defined or estimated turn-round period for the departing flight (milestones 6, 7, 8).
  operation confirmFinalTOBT(flight: Flight): OffBlockTime; The time at which the AO/GH provide their most accurate TOBT taking into account the operational situation, at t minutes before EOBT (milestone 9).
  operation manageTurnRoundProcess(flight: Flight): void; The AO/GH, in coordination with the aircrew, manages the turn-round process accordingly once TOBT/TSAT is stable (milestones 9, 10).
  operation recordActualCommenceGroundHandling(flight: Flight): GroundHandlingTime; AO/GH provides the Actual Commence of Ground Handling time (ACGT) (milestone 8).
  operation recordAircraftReady(flight: Flight): ReadyTime; AO/GH provides the Actual Ready Time (ARDT) when doors are closed and the aircraft is ready to taxi (milestone 12).

class AircraftOperator (package Stakeholders): The airline that submits the ATC Flight Plan and integrates its own strategy to compute the TOBT for a flight.
  operation submitATCFlightPlan(flightPlan: FlightPlan): void; The ATC Flight Plan is submitted by the Aircraft Operator (milestone 1).
  operation integrateOwnStrategyForTOBT(flight: Flight): void; Emphasis is put on the need for the Aircraft Operator to integrate his own strategy to compute a TOBT related to the flight (milestone 9).

class GroundHandler (package Stakeholders): The ground handling organisation that carries out turn-round operations for a flight, jointly referred to with the Aircraft Operator as AO/GH.

class AirTrafficControl (package Stakeholders): The air traffic control organisation, including ACC, Approach Control Unit and Tower units, that issues TSAT, approves start-up, and sequences departing flights.
  attribute unitType: String; The ATC unit involved, e.g. ACC, Approach Control Unit, or Tower, as named in the document for radar detection and sequencing.
  operation issueTSAT(flight: Flight): StartUpApprovalTime; ATC issues the Target Start Up Approval Time, t minutes before EOBT, a locally agreed parameter (milestone 10).
  operation approveStartUp(flight: Flight): StartUpApprovalTime; ATC provides the Actual start up Approval Time (ASAT), the time an aircraft receives its start up approval (milestone 14).
  operation confirmTSATToPilot(flight: Flight): void; ATC confirms TSAT to the pilot in order to maintain the aircraft in the pre-departure sequence (milestone 13).
  operation refinePreDepartureSequence(flight: Flight): void; ATC refines the pre-departure sequence once the pilot requests start up just before TSAT (milestone 12).
  operation resequenceFlight(flight: Flight): void; If start-up approval is not granted at TSAT+tolerance, all partners are informed and the flight will be re-sequenced (milestone 14).

class NetworkOperations (package Stakeholders): The network management function that issues CTOT for regulated flights, monitors flight progress, and receives DPI messages from the Airport CDM Platform.
  operation issueCTOT(flight: Flight): TakeOffTime; The CTOT is issued by Network Operations and sent to relevant ATS units and the departure aerodrome, at EOBT-2h if the flight is regulated (milestone 2).

class IFPS (package Stakeholders): The system/organisation that distributes the submitted ATC Flight Plan to all involved ATC units.
  operation distributeFlightPlan(flightPlan: FlightPlan): void; The ATC Flight Plan is distributed by the IFPS so that all involved ATC units, including departure and destination aerodromes, receive it (milestone 1).

class Pilot (package Stakeholders): The aircrew member who requests start-up approval from ATC ahead of TSAT.
  operation requestStartUp(flight: Flight): void; The pilot requests start up just before TSAT, following coordination with the Ground Handler (milestone 12), triggering ATC's start-up request handling (milestone 13).

class Flight (package Flights): A single arrival or departure operation of an aircraft, tracked through the sixteen milestones of the Airport CDM process.
  attribute aircraftRegistration: String; The aircraft registration associated with the flight, fed to Network Operations via the first E-DPI (milestone 1).
  attribute aircraftType: String; The type of aircraft, which the flight plan may be used to update (milestone 1).
  attribute direction: FlightDirection; Whether this leg is the arrival or departure flight of a turn-round (milestone 1).
  attribute status: FlightStatus; The current operational status of the flight, changed by each milestone as it occurs.
  attribute regulated: Boolean; Whether the flight has received a CTOT and is therefore regulated; regulated fields are marked REGULATED (milestone 2).

class TurnRound (package Flights): An aircraft turn-round normally comprising a related arriving and departing flight pair.
  attribute turnRoundId: String; Identifier of the turn-round grouping an arrival and a departure flight.

class Aircraft (package Flights): The physical aircraft that operates a flight, identified by registration and type.
  attribute registration: String; The aircraft registration.
  attribute type: String; The type of aircraft, as may be updated from the flight plan (milestone 1).

class FlightPlan (package Flights): The ICAO ATC Flight Plan submitted for a flight, possibly a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) covering daily or weekly flights.
  attribute repetitive: Boolean; Whether this is a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) covering daily or weekly flights (milestone 1).
  attribute submissionTime: DateTime; Time the ATC Flight Plan was submitted, normally 3 hours before EOBT though it may be later (milestone 1).
  attribute departureAerodrome: String; The departure aerodrome named in the flight plan (milestone 1).
  attribute destinationAerodrome: String; The destination aerodrome named in the flight plan (milestone 1).

class AirportSlot (package Flights): The airport slot allocated to a flight under the airport slot programme, against which the ATC Flight Plan must be consistent.
  attribute slotTime: DateTime; The scheduled time associated with the airport slot.
  attribute aerodrome: String; The aerodrome to which the slot applies.

class Airport (package Flights): An aerodrome involved in a flight, either as departure (including as an outstation) or destination, which may or may not be schedule-coordinated.
  attribute icaoCode: String; ICAO identifier of the aerodrome.
  attribute coordinated: Boolean; Whether the airport is schedule-coordinated; for coordinated airports the outbound flight is already known, for non-coordinated airports the flight plan is used to initiate the outbound flight (milestone 1).
  operation provideActualTakeOffTime(flight: Flight): TakeOffTime; The outstation (ADEP) provides ATOT to the Network Operations and Aircraft Operator (milestone 3).

class TimeQuantity (package Timings): A time value describing a flight's progress, named by a prefix (Scheduled, Estimated, Target, Actual, Calculated) and an event, keeping every successive value with its originator and time of update.
  attribute prefix: TimePrefix; The prefix identifying the nature of the value: Scheduled, Estimated, Target, Actual, or Calculated.
  attribute value: DateTime; The time value itself.
  attribute originator: String; Who or what provided or updated this value, e.g. AO, GH, ATC, Network Operations, ACARS, radar.
  attribute accuracy: String; The stated accuracy of the value where the document gives one, e.g. +/- 1 minute.
  attribute recordedAt: DateTime; The time this value was recorded; updates append a new value rather than overwrite so a turn-round can be analysed afterwards.

class OffBlockTime (package Timings): The off-block time quantity of a flight, covering SOBT, EOBT, TOBT and AOBT values distinguished by prefix.

class InBlockTime (package Timings): The in-block time quantity of a flight, covering EIBT and AIBT values distinguished by prefix.

class LandingTime (package Timings): The landing time quantity of a flight, covering ELDT and ALDT values distinguished by prefix.

class TakeOffTime (package Timings): The take-off time quantity of a flight, covering ETOT, TTOT, CTOT and ATOT values distinguished by prefix.

class StartUpApprovalTime (package Timings): The start-up approval time quantity of a flight, covering TSAT and ASAT values distinguished by prefix.

class TaxiTime (package Timings): An estimated taxi time quantity of a flight, covering EXOT (taxi-out) and EXIT (taxi-in), distinguished by direction.
  attribute direction: TaxiDirection; Whether this taxi time is Out (EXOT) or In (EXIT).

class MinimumTurnRoundTime (package Timings): The minimum/estimated turn-round time (MTTT) used to calculate downstream TOBT and TTOT for a departing flight.

class GroundHandlingTime (package Timings): The Actual Commence of Ground Handling time (ACGT), specific to flights that are the first operation of the day or have been long-term parked.

class BoardingTime (package Timings): The Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT), the time the gate opens for passengers to physically start boarding.

class ReadyTime (package Timings): The Actual Ready Time (ARDT), the time all doors are closed and the aircraft is ready to taxi upon TWR instruction.

class StartUpRequestTime (package Timings): The Actual Start-up Request Time (ASRT), the time start-up is requested.

class ElapsedTime (package Timings): The Estimated Elapsed Time given on the ATC Flight Plan, used with ATOT to calculate ELDT.

class Milestone (package Milestones): One of the sixteen defined events of the Airport CDM process, each with its own trigger, timing rule, effect, and procedure.
  attribute milestoneNumber: Integer; The number (1-16) identifying the milestone.
  attribute name: String; The name of the milestone, e.g. 'ATC Flight Plan Activated'.
  attribute definition: String; The document's definition of the milestone.
  attribute trigger: String; What triggers this milestone process, as stated in the document.
  attribute dataQualityAccuracy: String; The stated data quality/accuracy for the milestone's information, where given.
  attribute resultingStatus: FlightStatus; The operational status the flight changes to when this milestone occurs.
  operation changeOperationalStatus(flight: Flight): void; Sets the flight's status to the milestone's resultingStatus, as stated in each milestone's 'Operational Status (changes to)' field.

class FlightPlanActivatedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 1: the ICAO flight plan is submitted to ATC and the Airport CDM Platform is initiated for the flight.
  operation checkConsistency(flightPlan: FlightPlan, airportSlot: AirportSlot, flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent.
  operation confirmFlightToNetworkOperations(flight: Flight): void; Confirms the flight to Network Operations and allows further local processing of the flight.
  operation sendFirstEDPI(flight: Flight): DPIMessage; Sends the first E-DPI with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration and first destination data; not sent if information is missing or inconsistent, triggered by first activation of the flight plan (earliest EOBT-3hr) or new/late submissions after cancellation or revised EOBT.
  operation checkFlightReadyDeadline(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks that the flight is ready not later than 15 minutes after the planned EOBT.

class EOBTMinus2HourMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 2: at EOBT-2hr regulated flights receive a CTOT and Network Operations is informed of the updated take-off estimate.
  operation checkFlightEstimatesConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks, before or after take-off from outstation, whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan, verifying feasibility at EOBT-2hrs.
  operation sendTDPI(flight: Flight): DPIMessage; Informs Network Operations at EOBT-2hrs about the updated take-off time estimate using a T-DPI message.
  operation calculateTTOT(eibt: InBlockTime, mttt: MinimumTurnRoundTime, exot: TaxiTime, eobt: OffBlockTime, tobt: OffBlockTime): TakeOffTime; Calculation basis for TTOT takes into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT if later than EOBT+EXOT; if TOBT is manually input it overrides the EIBT+MTTT estimate, so TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
  operation updateELDTFromFUM(flight: Flight, fum: FlightUpdateMessage): LandingTime; For inbound flights, ELDT is updated based on information provided by FUM messages, taking into account the actual progress of the flight.

class TakeOffFromOutstationMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 3: the ATOT from the outstation (ADEP) is recorded and used to update landing and take-off estimates.
  attribute ttotTolerance: Duration; A TTOT tolerance of 5 minutes is respected before Network Operations is informed of the updated TTOT.
  operation checkLandingEstimateConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH estimated landing time after take-off from outstation is consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan.
  operation sendTDPIt(flight: Flight): DPIMessage; Informs Network Operations about the updated take-off time estimates using a T-DPI-t message, respecting the 5-minute TTOT tolerance.
  operation calculateTTOT(eibt: InBlockTime, mttt: MinimumTurnRoundTime, exot: TaxiTime, eobt: OffBlockTime, tobt: OffBlockTime): TakeOffTime; TTOT takes into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT; if EOBT is later than EIBT+MTTT, TTOT equals EOBT+EXOT; if TOBT is available it overrules the EIBT+MTTT estimate, so TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
  operation calculateELDT(atot: TakeOffTime, elapsedTime: ElapsedTime): LandingTime; If the departure airport is more than 3hrs flying time from the destination, ELDT is calculated using the ATOT and the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL.

class LocalRadarUpdateMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 4: the flight enters the FIR or local airspace of the destination airport, updating ELDT and commencing the TOBT process.
  attribute standGateChangeCutoff: Duration; Any change to a stand or gate is not preferred after ELDT-30 minutes, taking into account the taxi-in time (EXIT).
  operation commenceTOBTProcess(flight: Flight): void; Commences the TOBT process and checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Verifies feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT.
  operation notifyNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
  operation calculateEIBT(eldt: LandingTime, exit: TaxiTime): InBlockTime; An estimated in-block time (EIBT) is computed using the ELDT and the estimated taxi-in time.
  operation checkStandGateChangeAllowed(eldt: LandingTime, currentTime: DateTime): Boolean; Checks that any change to a stand or gate is not made after ELDT-30 minutes, taking into account the taxi-in time (EXIT).

class FinalApproachMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 5: the flight enters the Final Approach phase at the destination airport, updating ELDT and commencing the TOBT process.
  operation commenceTOBTProcess(flight: Flight): void; Commences the TOBT process and checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Verifies feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT.
  operation notifyNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.

class LandedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 6: ALDT, the actual landing time, triggers an update of downstream estimates.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan, verifying feasibility given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
  operation notifyNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
  operation updateTOBTAndTTOT(flight: Flight): void; TOBT and TTOT are updated automatically or inserted manually by the AO/GH, calculated on the basis of the defined turn-round period for the departing flight.
  operation calculateEIBT(aldt: LandingTime, exit: TaxiTime): InBlockTime; The EIBT can be updated according to the ALDT+EXIT.

class InBlockMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 7: AIBT, the actual in-block time, triggers an update of downstream estimates.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan, verifying feasibility given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
  operation notifyNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
  operation updateTOBTAndTTOT(flight: Flight): void; TOBT and TTOT are updated automatically or inserted manually by the AO/GH, calculated on the basis of the estimated turn-round period for the departing flight.

class GroundHandlingStartedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 8: commencement of ground handling operations (ACGT) triggers an update of downstream estimates, specific to first-of-day or long-parked flights.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan, verifying feasibility given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
  operation notifyNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
  operation updateTOBT(flight: Flight): void; TOBT is updated automatically or inserted manually by the AO/GH, calculated on the basis of the estimated turn-round period for the departing flight.

class FinalTOBTConfirmationMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 9: the time at which the AO/GH provide their most accurate TOBT taking into account the operational situation, t minutes before EOBT.
  attribute confirmationOffsetMinutes: Duration; TOBT is confirmed t minutes before EOBT, t being a parameter agreed locally.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
  operation notifyNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
  operation confirmTOBT(flight: Flight): Boolean; Performed at a predefined local-parameter time to confirm TOBT prior to TSAT issue and verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimates; no need to confirm an existing TOBT if it has been manually modified before.
  operation introduceFlightIntoPreDepartureSequence(flight: Flight): void; The flight is introduced into the pre-departure sequence once TOBT is confirmed.

class TSATIssuedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 10: the time ATC issues the Target Start Up Approval Time.
  attribute issueOffsetMinutes: Duration; TSAT is provided t-minutes before EOBT, t being a locally agreed parameter.
  operation informPartnersOfTSAT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant partners of the TSAT that has been allocated to the flight; no check is performed for this step.
  operation sendTDPIs(flight: Flight): DPIMessage; Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non-regulated flights.
  operation checkTOBTUpdateCount(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the number of TOBT updates after TSAT has been issued exceeds a locally defined tolerance; if exceeded, the TOBT input is processed according to local procedure.

class BoardingStartsMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 11: the gate is open for passengers to physically start boarding.
  attribute checkOffsetMinutes: Duration; A local time variable, e.g. corresponding to aircraft type, before TOBT at which the boarding status check is triggered.
  operation informPartnersOfASBT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of the Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) when it occurs.
  operation checkBoardingStatus(flight: Flight): Boolean; At a certain local-variable time before TOBT, checks whether boarding starts in time to respect TOBT, and informs AO/GH in case TOBT needs to be updated.

class AircraftReadyMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 12: the time when all doors are closed, boarding bridge removed, push-back vehicle connected, ready to taxi upon TWR instructions (ARDT).
  attribute readyTolerance: Duration; At TOBT+tolerance the AO/GH are informed that TOBT has passed and there has not been a ready status message yet.
  operation informPartnersOfARDT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of ARDT in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft is ready for start-up/push-back.
  operation checkReadyStatusReceived(flight: Flight): Boolean; At TOBT+tolerance, checks and informs the AO/GH that TOBT has passed and the Airport CDM Platform has not yet received ARDT or Ready Status (RDY).

class StartUpRequestedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 13: the time that start-up is requested (ASRT), based on pilot request to ATC.
  attribute tsatTolerance: Duration; The locally agreed TSAT tolerance window inside which a start-up request is expected.
  operation informPartnersOfASRT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of the Actual Start-up Request Time (ASRT) in the Airport CDM Platform when it occurs.
  operation alertIfNoStartUpRequested(flight: Flight): void; If the start-up request is not made by TSAT+tolerance, alerts all relevant Airport CDM Partners and informs the AO/GH that no start-up has been requested and TOBT should be updated.

class StartUpApprovedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 14: ASAT, the actual time an aircraft receives its start-up approval.
  attribute startUpTolerance: Duration; In case start-up approval is not granted at TSAT+tolerance, all relevant partners are informed.
  operation informPartnersOfASAT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of ASAT in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has received start-up approval/push-back clearance.
  operation checkASATAgainstTSAT(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks if ASAT is in accordance with TSAT and alerts all relevant Airport CDM Partners when no start-up has been granted at TSAT+tolerance; the flight will be re-sequenced.

class OffBlockMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 15: AOBT, the actual time the aircraft pushes back/vacates the parking position.

  operation sendADPI(flight: Flight): DPIMessage; AOBT always triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations, or in the case of remote holding at a defined time prior to TTOT.
  operation checkTTOTChangeAndNotify(flight: Flight): void; After the first A-DPI is sent, checks TTOT updates against the TTOT tolerance before Network Operations is informed, with a new A-DPI, of the updated TTOT.

class TakeOffMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 16: ATOT, the actual time the aircraft takes off from the runway.
  operation informPartnersOfATOT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners about the actual take-off.
  operation generateAirborneMessage(flight: Flight): AirborneMessage; An airborne message is generated, triggered by Tower FDPS, A-SMGCS/Radar detection or ACARS.
  operation removeFromDepartureSequence(flight: Flight): void; The flight is removed from the departure sequence.
  operation sendFSAAndMVT(flight: Flight): void; FSA and MVT messages are sent.

class AirportCDMPlatform (package Systems): The Airport CDM Platform that is initiated for each flight, processes all available flight information, and performs the ACISP actions defined for each milestone.
  operation initiatePlatformForFlight(flight: Flight): void; The Airport CDM Platform is initiated for the flight and all available information is processed (milestone 1).
  operation updateArrivalEstimates(flight: Flight): void; ELDT and EIBT updated for an arrival (milestone 1 ACISP action).
  operation updateDepartureEstimates(flight: Flight): void; EOBT and ETOT updated for a departure (milestone 1 ACISP action).
  operation commenceDPIProcess(flight: Flight): void; The DPI process commences the correct messaging with Network Operations, if implemented (milestone 1).
  operation updateETOTTTOTCTOT(flight: Flight): void; ETOT/TTOT/CTOT updated (milestone 2 ACISP action).

class ATCSystem (package Systems): The ATC technical system (including radar and A-SMGCS) that detects a flight's position and calculates the Estimated Taxi-Out Time.

class ETFMS (package Systems): The Network Operations system used to monitor a flight's progress and send Flight Update Messages.
