# The document: sixteen A-CDM milestones, sentences numbered

## Milestone 1: ATC Flight Plan Activated

### Definition

[M1.definition.1] The ICAO flight plan is submitted to ATC.
[M1.definition.2] The Airport CDM Platform is initiated for this flight, and all available information is processed.
### Origin and priority

[M1.origin_and_priority.1] The ATC Flight Plan is submitted by the Aircraft Operator and distributed by the IFPS.
[M1.origin_and_priority.2] All involved ATC units receive the flight plan, including departure and destination aerodromes.
### Timing

[M1.timing.1] Normally this takes place 3 hours before EOBT, however it may be later.
[M1.timing.2] In some cases a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) has been submitted, covering daily or weekly flights.
### Data Quality

[M1.data_quality.1] The ATC Flight Plan corresponds to the airport slot programme.
### Effect

[M1.effect.1] One aircraft turn-round normally includes an arriving and a departing flight, meaning that it will have two related flight plans.
[M1.effect.2] For coordinated airports, the outbound flight is already known.
[M1.effect.3] The flight plan may be used to update certain information such as type of aircraft.
[M1.effect.4] For long distance flights, the ELDT may differ from the airport slot.
[M1.effect.5] For non coordinated airports, the flight plan is used to initiate the outbound flight.
[M1.effect.6] The flight is ready not later than 15 minutes after the planned EOBT.
[M1.effect.7] The DPI process commences the correct messaging with Network Operations (if implemented - see attachment 2 for details).
### Procedures

[M1.procedures.1] To check consistency between ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data and then confirm the flight to the Network Operations and allow further local processing of the flight.
[M1.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify the consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent.
[M1.procedures.3] The AO must provide correct information before this first E-DPI message, in order to feed Network Operations with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration, and first destination data, as early in time as possible.
[M1.procedures.4] The E-DPI message should not be sent if no or inconsistent information is provided.
[M1.procedures.5] This process is triggered by:
[M1.procedures.6] - The first activation of the ATC Flight Plan (earliest EOBT-3 hr), or
[M1.procedures.7] - New or late submissions of the ATC Flight Plan, after cancellation or revised EOBT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M1.operational_status.1] INITIATED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M1.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT and EIBT updated for an arrival EOBT and ETOT updated for a departure The DPI process commences (if implemented - see section 3.7.3 for details).
## Milestone 2: EOBT - 2 hr

### Definition

[M2.definition.1] At EOBT-2 hr most flights will be known in the Airport CDM Platform including if they are regulated or not.
[M2.definition.2] All regulated flights receive a CTOT from Network Operations.
### Origin and priority

[M2.origin_and_priority.1] The CTOT is issued by the Network Operations and is sent to relevant ATS units as well as the departure aerodrome.
[M2.origin_and_priority.2] CTOT flights usually have a priority over unregulated flights.
### Timing

[M2.timing.1] If the flight is regulated, a CTOT is issued at EOBT-2h.
### Data Quality

[M2.data_quality.1] Not applicable.
### Effect

[M2.effect.1] For inbound flights, ELDT is updated based on information provided by the FUM messages, taking into account the actual progress of the flight.
### Procedures

[M2.procedures.1] To check (before or after take off from outstation) whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan and to inform Network Operations about the updated take off time estimate, using a T-DPI Message.
[M2.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimated off block time at EOBT-2 hrs.
[M2.procedures.3] At EOBT-2 hrs Network Operations is informed through the first T-DPI message.
[M2.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT, if later than EOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.5] In the case of manual input of TOBT, this estimate will override the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.6] This procedure is triggered by
[M2.procedures.7] - a time stamp, at EOBT - 2h.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M2.operational_status.1] N. A.
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M2.action_on_cdm.1] ETOT/TTOT/CTOT Mark appropriate fields as REGULATED
## Milestone 3: Take Off from Outstation

### Definition

[M3.definition.1] The ATOT from the outstation (ADEP)
### Origin and priority

[M3.origin_and_priority.1] The outstation provides ATOT to the Network Operations and Aircraft Operator.
### Timing

[M3.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M3.data_quality.1] The accuracy of ATOT is +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M3.effect.1] If the departure airport is more than 3hrs flying time from the destination airport the ATOT is received from either the Network Operations FUM or via the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler.
[M3.effect.2] Using the ATOT an ELDT can be calculated by using the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL.
[M3.effect.3] If the flight is within 3hrs flying time of the destination airport the Network Operations monitors progress of the flight using the ETFMS and send a Flight Update Message (FUM) that provides updates of the flight's progress.
### Procedures

[M3.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH estimated landing time after take off from outstation are consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan, and when needed inform the Network Operations about the updated take off time estimates using a T-DPI-t Message.
[M3.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan at take off from outstation.
[M3.procedures.3] A TTOT tolerance of 5 minutes is respected before Network Operations is informed of the updated TTOT.
[M3.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.5] In case EOBT is later than EIBT+MTTT, TTOT equals EOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.6] In the case where TOBT is available this prediction will overrule the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.7] This process is triggered by
[M3.procedures.8] - the take off from outstation.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M3.operational_status.1] AIRBORNE
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M3.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 4: Local Radar Update

### Definition

[M4.definition.1] The flight enters the FIR (Flight Information Region) or the local airspace of the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M4.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from the Area Control Centre (ACC) or Approach Control Unit that is associated with an airport.
[M4.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system is able to detect a flight based upon the assigned SSR code when the flight crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary.
### Timing

[M4.timing.1] Dependent upon the position of the airport in relation to the FIR boundary.
### Data Quality

[M4.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M4.effect.1] Update of the ELDT can trigger a new TOBT to be entered by the AO/GH, or calculated automatically by the Airport CDM Platform.
[M4.effect.2] The accuracy of ELDT is particularly important at this stage since downstream decisions are taken, such as stand /gate / aircraft changes, preparation of arrival sequence, preparation of ground handling operations, decisions for connecting passengers.
[M4.effect.3] Uncertainty and ELDT non-accuracy at this stage significantly increase risks for bad and last minute decisions and internal disruptions.
[M4.effect.4] The objective to decrease the number of stand and gate changes in the last 30 minutes requires high accuracy regarding departure and arrival times.
[M4.effect.5] Therefore, taking into account the taxi-in time (EXIT), any change to a stand or gate is not preferred after ELDT-30'.
[M4.effect.6] The update of TOBT for the related departing flight takes place following this milestone.
[M4.effect.7] Decisions such as the turn-round period, connecting passengers etc are taken and need to be stable at this event.
[M4.effect.8] An estimated in-block time (EIBT) is computed using the ELDT and the estimated taxi-in time.
### Procedures

[M4.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M4.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M4.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M4.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M4.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M4.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M4.operational_status.1] FIR
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M4.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 5: Final Approach

### Definition

[M5.definition.1] The flight enters the Final Approach phase at the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M5.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from ATC.
[M5.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system detects a flight based upon the assigned SSR code and identifies when the flight crosses either a defined range / position or passes/leaves a predetermined level.
### Timing

[M5.timing.1] Dependent upon local parameters that are defined by ATC.
### Data Quality

[M5.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M5.effect.1] Update of the ELDT to determine a new TOBT.
[M5.effect.2] When a flight reaches this stage it is usually between 2 and 5 minutes from landing (depending on the parameter set by ATC).
[M5.effect.3] This is often the prompt for many partners to start moving resources connected with the flight, such as positioning a parking marshal and ground handling services.
### Procedures

[M5.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M5.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M5.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M5.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M5.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M5.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M5.operational_status.1] FINAL
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M5.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 6: Landed

### Definition

[M6.definition.1] ALDT - Actual Landing Time.
[M6.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft touches down on a runway.
[M6.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATA - Actual Time of Arrival landing, ACARS=ON).
### Origin and priority

[M6.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or by ACARS from equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M6.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M6.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M6.effect.1] The occurrence of ALDT triggers an update of downstream estimates: TOBT and TTOT are updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the defined turn-round period for the departing flight.
[M6.effect.2] The EIBT can be updated according to the ALDT +EXIT.
### Procedures

[M6.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M6.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M6.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M6.procedures.6] - Actual Landing Time: ALDT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M6.operational_status.1] LANDED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M6.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT changes to ALDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated

## Milestone 9: Final Confirmation of the TOBT

### Definition

[M9.definition.1] The time at which the Aircraft Operator or Ground Handler provide their most accurate TOBT taking into account the operational situation.
### Origin and priority

[M9.origin_and_priority.1] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler provides the information.
### Timing

[M9.timing.1] The information is provided t minutes before EOBT (t is a parameter time agreed locally).
### Data Quality

[M9.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M9.effect.1] The aim of the final TOBT is to give a timely, accurate and reliable assessment of the off-block time.
[M9.effect.2] It is recognised that main benefits of sharing the TOBT are expected in case of disruptions (internal or external).
[M9.effect.3] In such cases, the difference between EOBT (shared by ATC, Network Operations and Stand / Gate Management) and TOBT may be important.
[M9.effect.4] An accurate TOBT at [EOBT-t minutes] is a pre-requisite for ATC to establish a push back / pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.5] Emphasis is put on the need for the Aircraft Operator to integrate his own strategy to compute a TOBT related to the flight.
[M9.effect.6] Following the receipt of the TOBT, the ATC system will calculate and provide the Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) based on the predicted traffic load, gate / stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the Holding Position, etc. The flight is introduced into the pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.7] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M9.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M9.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M9.procedures.3] This check should be performed at a predefined time (local parameter) to confirm TOBT prior to TSAT issue and verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimates given the updated TOBT.
[M9.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M9.procedures.5] This Milestone Process is actually constantly applicable in the CDM Platform, as soon as a TOBT is available.
[M9.procedures.6] However the confirmed TOBT prior to TSAT has special status, where AO/GH check the quality of TOBT before TSAT issue.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M9.operational_status.1] IN-BLOCK
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M9.action_on_cdm.1] This process is triggered by - a new TOBT or TTOT update.
[M9.action_on_cdm.2] No need to confirm an existing TOBT if it has been manually modified before.

### Definition

[M10.definition.1] The time ATC issues the Target Start Up Approval Time
### Origin and priority

[M10.origin_and_priority.1] ATC
### Timing

[M10.timing.1] The information is provided t-minutes before EOBT, where t is a parameter agreed locally
### Data Quality

[M10.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M10.effect.1] The flight is stabilised into the pre-departure sequence.
[M10.effect.2] The Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M10.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant partners of the TSAT that has been allocated to the flight.
[M10.procedures.2] The Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.3] Second step: To check whether the number of TOBT updates exceeds a tolerance defined locally, after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.4] First: The TSAT will indicate to the partners the time when the start up approval can be expected.
[M10.procedures.5] Network Operations will be informed with a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.6] No check is performed.
[M10.procedures.7] Second: A check shall be performed to see the number of TOBT updates after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.8] In case the number of TOBT updates exceeds a threshold, then the TOBT input should be processed according to local procedure.
[M10.procedures.9] This process is triggered by
[M10.procedures.10] - A defined time (local parameter) before TOBT
[M10.procedures.11] - TOBT update after TSAT issue

[M11.definition.2] This is not to be confused with the time passengers are pre-called to the gate via flight information display systems (FIDS) or public address systems.
### Origin and priority

[M11.origin_and_priority.1] Automatic from airport system or manual input by Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M11.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M11.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M11.effect.1] When boarding commences it gives the Airport CDM Partners a good indication of whether the TOBT/TSAT will be respected.
### Procedures

[M11.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT).
[M11.procedures.2] Second step: To check whether boarding starts in time to respect TOBT and inform the AO/GH in case TOBT needs to be updated.
[M11.procedures.3] Inform of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) when it occurs.
[M11.procedures.4] At a certain time before TOBT (local variable e.g. corresponding to aircraft type) a check shall be performed to check the boarding status.
[M11.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M11.procedures.6] - a time variable <value> minutes before TOBT.
### Operational Status (changes to)

## Milestone 12: Aircraft Ready

### Definition

[M12.definition.1] The time when all doors are closed, boarding bridge removed, push back vehicle connected, ready to taxi immediately upon reception of TWR instructions (ARDT).
### Origin and priority

[M12.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by the Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M12.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M12.data_quality.1] Data is directly available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M12.effect.1] ATC refines the pre-departure sequence.
[M12.effect.2] The pilot requests start up just before TSAT, following coordination with the Ground Handler.
[M12.effect.3] (Dispatcher / Supervisor / Redcap).
### Procedures

[M12.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Ready Time (ARDT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft is ready for start up / push-back.
[M12.procedures.2] Second step: To inform the AO/GH that TOBT has passed and the Airport CDM Platform has not yet received ARDT or Ready Status (RDY).
[M12.procedures.3] Inform of ARDT or RDY confirming that the flight follows the indicated TOBT.
[M12.procedures.4] At TOBT + tolerance the AO/GH are informed that TOBT has passed and there has not been a ready status message yet.
[M12.procedures.5] This procedure is triggered by
[M12.procedures.6] - an input to the Airport CDM Platform.

## Milestone 15: Off-Block

### Definition

[M15.definition.1] AOBT - Actual Off-Block Time.
[M15.definition.2] The time the aircraft pushes back/vacates the parking position (Equivalent to Airline/Handler ATD - Actual Time of Departure ACARS=OUT).
### Origin and priority

[M15.origin_and_priority.1] ACARS equipped aircraft or automated docking systems or ATC systems (e.g. A-SMGCS) or by manual input.
### Timing

[M15.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M15.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M15.effect.1] TTOT updated considering the EXOT.
### Procedures

[M15.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Off-Block Time (AOBT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has commenced push-back / taxi from parking position.
[M15.procedures.2] Second step: To check if TTOT changes by more than the agreed tolerance and inform Network Operations.

## Milestone 16: Take Off

### Definition

[M16.definition.1] ATOT - Actual Take Off Time.
[M16.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft takes off from the runway.
[M16.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATD-Actual Time of Departure, ACARS = OFF).
### Origin and priority

[M16.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or from ACARS equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M16.timing.1] The information is directly available as soon as possible after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M16.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M16.effect.1] FSA and MVT messages are sent.
### Procedures

[M16.procedures.1] To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners about the actual take off.
[M16.procedures.2] An airborne message is generated and the flight is removed from the departure sequence.
[M16.procedures.3] This process is triggered by Tower FDPS, A-SMGCS / Radar detection or ACARS.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M16.operational_status.1] DEPARTED / TAKE OFF
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M16.action_on_cdm.1] ATOT recorded

# The model

class Stakeholder (package Stakeholders): A party that takes part in the CDM process and originates or receives flight or timing information.
  attribute name: String; Name or identifier of the stakeholder.
  attribute role: String; Role played in the CDM process.

class AircraftOperator (package Stakeholders): The Aircraft Operator (AO), who submits the ATC Flight Plan and provides TOBT, ready status, and other flight information.

class GroundHandler (package Stakeholders): The Ground Handler (GH), who together with the Aircraft Operator provides TOBT, ready status, and manages the turn-round process.

class AirTrafficControl (package Stakeholders): ATC, including Area Control Centre, Approach Control, and Tower units, which issues TSAT, ASAT, and detects milestones via radar.

class NetworkOperations (package Stakeholders): Network Operations, which issues CTOT, monitors flight progress via ETFMS, and receives DPI messages.

class Pilot (package Stakeholders): The flight crew who request start-up and coordinate with the Ground Handler around TSAT.

class Aircraft (package Flights): The physical aircraft operating a flight, identified by registration and type, which may be updated from the flight plan.
  attribute registration: String; Aircraft registration.
  attribute type: String; Type of aircraft, which the flight plan may be used to update.

class Flight (package Flights): An arriving or departing flight tracked through the CDM process from ATC Flight Plan activation to take-off.
  attribute flightId: String; Identifier of the flight.
  attribute direction: String; Whether the flight is an arrival or a departure within its turn-round.
  attribute operationalStatus: FlightOperationalStatus; Current operational status, changed at each milestone (e.g. INITIATED, AIRBORNE, FIR, FINAL, LANDED, IN-BLOCK, SEQUENCED, BOARDING, READY, OFF-BLOCK, DEPARTED/TAKE OFF).
  attribute regulated: Boolean; Whether the flight is regulated and has received a CTOT; appropriate fields are marked REGULATED at EOBT-2hr.
  operation isReadyWithinTolerance(): Boolean; The flight is ready not later than 15 minutes after the planned EOBT (Milestone 1 effect).

class TurnRound (package Flights): The turn-round of an aircraft, normally comprising a related arriving flight and departing flight.
  attribute turnRoundPeriod: Duration; The defined or estimated turn-round period used as a basis for calculating TOBT for the departing flight.

class ATCFlightPlan (package Flights): The ICAO ATC Flight Plan submitted by the Aircraft Operator and distributed by the IFPS to all involved ATC units.
  attribute departureAerodrome: String; The departure aerodrome named in the flight plan.
  attribute destinationAerodrome: String; The destination aerodrome named in the flight plan.
  attribute sobt: DateTime; Scheduled off-block time, fed to Network Operations with aircraft registration and first destination data before the first E-DPI.
  attribute estimatedElapsedTime: Duration; The Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL, used together with ATOT to calculate ELDT.
  attribute aircraftRegistration: String; Aircraft registration fed to Network Operations before the first E-DPI.

class RepetitiveFlightPlan (package Flights): A repetitive flight plan (RFPL), a flight plan covering daily or weekly flights, submitted in some cases instead of a one-off flight plan.
  attribute frequency: String; Whether the RFPL covers daily or weekly flights.

class AirportSlot (package Flights): The airport slot allocated to a flight, against which the ATC Flight Plan and airport flight data must be consistent.
  attribute slotTime: DateTime; The scheduled time associated with the airport slot programme.
  attribute coordinated: Boolean; Whether the airport is coordinated, which determines whether the outbound flight is already known or must be initiated from the flight plan.

class TimeQuantity (package Timings): A named time quantity (prefixed Scheduled, Estimated, Target, Actual, or Calculated, for an event such as Off-Block, In-Block, Landing, or Take-Off) that describes a flight's progress, with its origin, accuracy, and tolerance; every successive value is kept, an update appends rather than overwrites.
  attribute type: TimeQuantityType; Which named time quantity this is (e.g. TOBT, EOBT, AOBT).
  attribute value: DateTime; The time value, or duration for quantities such as EXOT, EXIT, MTTT.
  attribute originator: String; Who or what provided this value, e.g. ATC, AO/GH, ACARS, radar system.
  attribute accuracy: String; Stated accuracy, e.g. +/- 1 minute for ATOT, ALDT, AIBT, ACGT, ASBT, ARDT, ASRT, ASAT, AOBT.
  attribute tolerance: String; Locally agreed tolerance applied before partners are informed of a change, e.g. the TTOT tolerance or 5-minute TTOT tolerance at outstation take-off.
  attribute recordedAt: DateTime; Time at which this value was recorded, so a turn-round can be analysed afterwards.
  operation calculateTTOT(eibt: DateTime, mttt: Duration, exot: Duration, eobt: DateTime, tobt: DateTime): DateTime; Calculation basis for TTOT takes into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT if later than EOBT+EXOT; if TOBT is manually input it overrides EIBT+MTTT, so TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
  operation calculateELDT(atot: DateTime, estimatedElapsedTime: Duration): DateTime; ELDT is calculated using ATOT and the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL.
  operation calculateEIBT(eldt: DateTime, exit: Duration): DateTime; EIBT is computed using ELDT (or ALDT) and the estimated/actual taxi-in time (EXIT).
  operation exceedsTolerance(newValue: DateTime): Boolean; Checks whether a new value changes by more than the agreed tolerance, triggering notification of Network Operations or Airport CDM Partners.

class MilestoneEvent (package Milestones): One of the sixteen defined events in the CDM process, each with its own trigger, timing rule, effect, and procedure.
  attribute milestoneNumber: Integer; Number of the milestone (1-16).
  attribute name: String; Name of the milestone.
  attribute definition: String; The document's definition of the milestone.
  attribute trigger: String; What triggers this milestone's procedure.
  attribute timingRule: String; The timing rule stated for this milestone.
  attribute effect: String; The effect the milestone has on flight data and downstream decisions.
  attribute operationalStatusChange: FlightOperationalStatus; The operational status the flight changes to at this milestone.

class FlightPlanActivationMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 1: the ICAO flight plan is submitted to ATC and the Airport CDM Platform is initiated for the flight.
  operation checkConsistency(flightPlan: ATCFlightPlan, airportSlot: AirportSlot): Boolean; Checks consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot, and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent.
  operation sendEDPI(): void; Confirms the flight to Network Operations with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration, and first destination data; the E-DPI should not be sent if no or inconsistent information is provided.

class EOBTMinus2HourMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 2: at EOBT-2hr most flights are known and all regulated flights receive a CTOT from Network Operations.
  operation checkEstimatesConsistency(): Boolean; Checks whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan at EOBT-2hrs.
  operation sendFirstTDPI(): void; Informs Network Operations at EOBT-2hrs through the first T-DPI message of the updated take-off time estimate.

class TakeOffFromOutstationMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 3: the ATOT from the outstation (ADEP) is provided to Network Operations and the Aircraft Operator.
  operation checkLandingEstimateConsistency(): Boolean; Checks whether AO/GH estimated landing times after take off from outstation are consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan.
  operation sendTDPIt(): void; Informs Network Operations about updated take off time estimates using a T-DPI-t message, respecting a 5-minute TTOT tolerance.

class LocalRadarUpdateMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 4: the flight enters the FIR or local airspace of the destination airport, detected by radar via the assigned SSR code.
  operation commenceTOBTProcess(): void; Commences the TOBT process and checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
  operation informNetworkOperationsIfTTOTExceedsTolerance(): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.

class FinalApproachMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 5: the flight enters the Final Approach phase at the destination airport, usually 2 to 5 minutes from landing.
  operation commenceTOBTProcess(): void; Commences the TOBT process and checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
  operation informNetworkOperationsIfTTOTExceedsTolerance(): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.

class LandedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 6: ALDT, the time an aircraft touches down on a runway.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
  operation informNetworkOperationsIfTTOTExceedsTolerance(): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.

class InBlockMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 7: AIBT, the time an aircraft arrives in-blocks; ACGT is considered to commence at AIBT.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
  operation informNetworkOperationsIfTTOTExceedsTolerance(): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.

class GroundHandlingStartedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 8: the commencement of ground handling operations (ACGT), specific to first flights of the day or long-parked aircraft.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
  operation informNetworkOperationsIfTTOTExceedsTolerance(): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.

class FinalTOBTConfirmationMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 9: the time the Aircraft Operator or Ground Handler provides its most accurate TOBT, taking into account the operational situation, t minutes before EOBT.
  operation confirmTOBT(): void; Performed at a predefined local time to confirm TOBT prior to TSAT issue and verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimates; no need to confirm an existing TOBT if it has been manually modified before.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan; informs Network Operations when TTOT changes by more than the agreed tolerance.

class TSATIssuedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 10: the time ATC issues the Target Start Up Approval Time (TSAT), t minutes before EOBT.
  operation informPartnersOfTSAT(): void; Informs all relevant partners of the TSAT allocated to the flight; Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non-regulated flights; no check is performed for this step.
  operation checkTOBTUpdateCount(): Boolean; Checks whether the number of TOBT updates after TSAT has been issued exceeds a locally defined tolerance; if exceeded, the TOBT input is processed according to local procedure.

class BoardingStartsMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 11: the gate is opened for passengers to physically start boarding.
  operation informASBT(): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of the Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) when it occurs.
  operation checkBoardingStatus(): void; At a certain time before TOBT (local variable, e.g. by aircraft type) checks the boarding status and informs the AO/GH in case TOBT needs to be updated.

class AircraftReadyMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 12: the time all doors are closed, boarding bridge removed, push-back vehicle connected, ready to taxi (ARDT).
  operation informReady(): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Ready Time (ARDT) or Ready Status (RDY), confirming the flight follows the indicated TOBT.
  operation alertIfNoReadyStatus(): void; At TOBT plus tolerance, informs the AO/GH that TOBT has passed and no ready status message has been received yet.

class StartUpRequestedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 13: the time start-up is requested by the pilot to ATC (ASRT).
  operation informASRT(): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start-up Request Time (ASRT) when it occurs.
  operation alertIfNoStartUpRequested(): void; If no start-up request is made by TSAT plus the locally agreed tolerance window, informs the AO/GH that no start-up has been requested and that TOBT should be updated.

class StartUpApprovedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 14: ASAT, the time an aircraft receives its start-up approval from ATC.
  operation informASAT(): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start-up Approval Time (ASAT) when it occurs, indicating start-up/push-back clearance.
  operation checkASATAgainstTSAT(): Boolean; Checks if ASAT is in accordance with TSAT; if start-up approval is not granted at TSAT plus tolerance, all relevant partners are informed and the flight is re-sequenced.

class OffBlockMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 15: AOBT, the time an aircraft pushes back/vacates the parking position.
  operation informAOBT(): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Off-Block Time (AOBT) when it occurs; AOBT always triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations, or at a defined time prior to TTOT in the case of remote holding.
  operation checkTTOTAfterAOBT(): Boolean; After the first A-DPI is sent, checks TTOT updates against the TTOT tolerance before Network Operations is informed, with a new A-DPI, of the updated TTOT.

class TakeOffMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 16: ATOT, the time an aircraft takes off from the runway.
  operation informActualTakeOff(): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners about the actual take off.
  operation generateAirborneMessage(): void; Generates an airborne message and removes the flight from the departure sequence; FSA and MVT messages are sent.

class AirportCDMPlatform (package Systems): The Airport CDM Platform, initiated for each flight, which processes available information and performs the CDM operation actions (ACISP) recorded at each milestone.
  attribute status: String; Current status held by the platform for the flight, e.g. INITIATED.
  operation initiateForFlight(flight: Flight): void; Initiates the Airport CDM Platform for the flight and processes all available information (Milestone 1).
  operation updateArrivalAndDepartureEstimates(flight: Flight): void; ELDT and EIBT updated for an arrival, EOBT and ETOT updated for a departure (Milestone 1 ACISP).
  operation markRegulated(flight: Flight): void; Updates ETOT/TTOT/CTOT and marks appropriate fields as REGULATED (Milestone 2 ACISP).
  operation updateTimingsAfterOutstationTakeOff(flight: Flight): void; ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated after take off from outstation (Milestone 3 ACISP).
  operation updateTimingsAfterRadarOrApproach(flight: Flight): void; ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated at Local Radar Update and Final Approach (Milestones 4 and 5 ACISP).
  operation changeELDTToALDT(flight: Flight): void; ELDT changes to ALDT and EIBT, TOBT, TTOT updated on landing (Milestone 6 ACISP).
  operation changeEIBTToAIBT(flight: Flight): void; EIBT changes to AIBT and TOBT, TTOT updated on in-block (Milestone 7 ACISP).
  operation updateAfterGroundHandlingStart(flight: Flight): void; ETTT/TOBT and TTOT updated when ground handling starts (Milestone 8 ACISP).
  operation updateTTOTAfterTSAT(flight: Flight): void; TTOT updated after TSAT is issued (Milestone 10 ACISP).
  operation recordAOBT(flight: Flight, aobt: DateTime): void; Records AOBT on off-block (Milestone 15 ACISP).
  operation recordATOT(flight: Flight, atot: DateTime): void; Records ATOT on take off (Milestone 16 ACISP).
  operation commenceDPIProcess(flight: Flight): void; The DPI process commences the correct messaging with Network Operations, if implemented (Milestone 1).

class ATCSystem (package Systems): The ATC ground system (e.g. A-SMGCS, Tower FDPS) that detects milestones and calculates taxi time estimates.
  attribute accuracy: String; Accuracy of the ATC system, which the accuracy of radar-detected milestones must equal.
  operation calculateEXOT(trafficLoad: String, gateLocation: String, runwayInUse: String, holdingWaitTime: Duration): Duration; Following receipt of TOBT, the ATC system calculates and provides the Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) based on predicted traffic load, gate/stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the Holding Position.

class RadarSystem (package Systems): The radar system associated with an airport that detects a flight based on its assigned SSR code.
  operation detectFlight(ssrCode: String): Boolean; Detects a flight based upon the assigned SSR code when it crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary, range/position, or passes/leaves a predetermined level.

class ACARS (package Systems): The ACARS aircraft reporting system that can provide actual times such as ATOT, ALDT, AIBT, and AOBT for equipped aircraft.
  operation reportActualTime(event: String): DateTime; Reports an actual time (ACARS=ON for landing, IN for in-block, OUT for off-block, OFF for take-off).

class ETFMS (package Systems): The system used by Network Operations to monitor the progress of a flight within 3 hours flying time of the destination airport.
  operation monitorFlightProgress(flight: Flight): void; Monitors progress of the flight and provides the basis for sending Flight Update Messages (FUM).

class IFPS (package Systems): The Integrated Initial Flight Plan Processing System that distributes the ATC Flight Plan to all involved ATC units.
  operation distributeFlightPlan(flightPlan: ATCFlightPlan): void; Distributes the submitted ATC Flight Plan to all involved ATC units, including departure and destination aerodromes.

class DPIMessage (package Messages): A Departure Planning Information message exchanged with Network Operations at defined points in the process.
  attribute type: DPIType; The kind of DPI message: E-DPI, T-DPI, T-DPI-t, T-DPI-s, or A-DPI.
  attribute sentTo: String; Recipient of the message, normally Network Operations.
  attribute timestamp: DateTime; Time the message was sent.

class FlightUpdateMessage (package Messages): A Flight Update Message (FUM) sent by Network Operations that provides updates of the flight's progress and is used to update ELDT.
  attribute updatedELDT: DateTime; The updated estimated landing time carried by the message.

class MovementMessage (package Messages): A movement (MVT) message sent on actual take off, along with an FSA message and an airborne message.
  attribute actualTakeOffTime: DateTime; The ATOT carried by the message.
