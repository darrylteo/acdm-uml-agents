# The document: sixteen A-CDM milestones, sentences numbered

## Milestone 1: ATC Flight Plan Activated

### Definition

[M1.definition.1] The ICAO flight plan is submitted to ATC.
[M1.definition.2] The Airport CDM Platform is initiated for this flight, and all available information is processed.
### Origin and priority

[M1.origin_and_priority.1] The ATC Flight Plan is submitted by the Aircraft Operator and distributed by the IFPS.
[M1.origin_and_priority.2] All involved ATC units receive the flight plan, including departure and destination aerodromes.
### Timing

[M1.timing.1] Normally this takes place 3 hours before EOBT, however it may be later.
[M1.timing.2] In some cases a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) has been submitted, covering daily or weekly flights.
### Data Quality

[M1.data_quality.1] The ATC Flight Plan corresponds to the airport slot programme.
### Effect

[M1.effect.1] One aircraft turn-round normally includes an arriving and a departing flight, meaning that it will have two related flight plans.
[M1.effect.2] For coordinated airports, the outbound flight is already known.
[M1.effect.3] The flight plan may be used to update certain information such as type of aircraft.
[M1.effect.4] For long distance flights, the ELDT may differ from the airport slot.
[M1.effect.5] For non coordinated airports, the flight plan is used to initiate the outbound flight.
[M1.effect.6] The flight is ready not later than 15 minutes after the planned EOBT.
[M1.effect.7] The DPI process commences the correct messaging with Network Operations (if implemented - see attachment 2 for details).
### Procedures

[M1.procedures.1] To check consistency between ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data and then confirm the flight to the Network Operations and allow further local processing of the flight.
[M1.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify the consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent.
[M1.procedures.3] The AO must provide correct information before this first E-DPI message, in order to feed Network Operations with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration, and first destination data, as early in time as possible.
[M1.procedures.4] The E-DPI message should not be sent if no or inconsistent information is provided.
[M1.procedures.5] This process is triggered by:
[M1.procedures.6] - The first activation of the ATC Flight Plan (earliest EOBT-3 hr), or
[M1.procedures.7] - New or late submissions of the ATC Flight Plan, after cancellation or revised EOBT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M1.operational_status.1] INITIATED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M1.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT and EIBT updated for an arrival EOBT and ETOT updated for a departure The DPI process commences (if implemented - see section 3.7.3 for details).
## Milestone 2: EOBT - 2 hr

### Definition

[M2.definition.1] At EOBT-2 hr most flights will be known in the Airport CDM Platform including if they are regulated or not.
[M2.definition.2] All regulated flights receive a CTOT from Network Operations.
### Origin and priority

[M2.origin_and_priority.1] The CTOT is issued by the Network Operations and is sent to relevant ATS units as well as the departure aerodrome.
[M2.origin_and_priority.2] CTOT flights usually have a priority over unregulated flights.
### Timing

[M2.timing.1] If the flight is regulated, a CTOT is issued at EOBT-2h.
### Data Quality

[M2.data_quality.1] Not applicable.
### Effect

[M2.effect.1] For inbound flights, ELDT is updated based on information provided by the FUM messages, taking into account the actual progress of the flight.
### Procedures

[M2.procedures.1] To check (before or after take off from outstation) whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan and to inform Network Operations about the updated take off time estimate, using a T-DPI Message.
[M2.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimated off block time at EOBT-2 hrs.
[M2.procedures.3] At EOBT-2 hrs Network Operations is informed through the first T-DPI message.
[M2.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT, if later than EOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.5] In the case of manual input of TOBT, this estimate will override the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.6] This procedure is triggered by
[M2.procedures.7] - a time stamp, at EOBT - 2h.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M2.operational_status.1] N. A.
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M2.action_on_cdm.1] ETOT/TTOT/CTOT Mark appropriate fields as REGULATED
## Milestone 3: Take Off from Outstation

### Definition

[M3.definition.1] The ATOT from the outstation (ADEP)
### Origin and priority

[M3.origin_and_priority.1] The outstation provides ATOT to the Network Operations and Aircraft Operator.
### Timing

[M3.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M3.data_quality.1] The accuracy of ATOT is +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M3.effect.1] If the departure airport is more than 3hrs flying time from the destination airport the ATOT is received from either the Network Operations FUM or via the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler.
[M3.effect.2] Using the ATOT an ELDT can be calculated by using the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL.
[M3.effect.3] If the flight is within 3hrs flying time of the destination airport the Network Operations monitors progress of the flight using the ETFMS and send a Flight Update Message (FUM) that provides updates of the flight's progress.
### Procedures

[M3.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH estimated landing time after take off from outstation are consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan, and when needed inform the Network Operations about the updated take off time estimates using a T-DPI-t Message.
[M3.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan at take off from outstation.
[M3.procedures.3] A TTOT tolerance of 5 minutes is respected before Network Operations is informed of the updated TTOT.
[M3.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.5] In case EOBT is later than EIBT+MTTT, TTOT equals EOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.6] In the case where TOBT is available this prediction will overrule the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.7] This process is triggered by
[M3.procedures.8] - the take off from outstation.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M3.operational_status.1] AIRBORNE
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M3.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 4: Local Radar Update

### Definition

[M4.definition.1] The flight enters the FIR (Flight Information Region) or the local airspace of the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M4.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from the Area Control Centre (ACC) or Approach Control Unit that is associated with an airport.
[M4.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system is able to detect a flight based upon the assigned SSR code when the flight crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary.
### Timing

[M4.timing.1] Dependent upon the position of the airport in relation to the FIR boundary.
### Data Quality

[M4.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M4.effect.1] Update of the ELDT can trigger a new TOBT to be entered by the AO/GH, or calculated automatically by the Airport CDM Platform.
[M4.effect.2] The accuracy of ELDT is particularly important at this stage since downstream decisions are taken, such as stand /gate / aircraft changes, preparation of arrival sequence, preparation of ground handling operations, decisions for connecting passengers.
[M4.effect.3] Uncertainty and ELDT non-accuracy at this stage significantly increase risks for bad and last minute decisions and internal disruptions.
[M4.effect.4] The objective to decrease the number of stand and gate changes in the last 30 minutes requires high accuracy regarding departure and arrival times.
[M4.effect.5] Therefore, taking into account the taxi-in time (EXIT), any change to a stand or gate is not preferred after ELDT-30'.
[M4.effect.6] The update of TOBT for the related departing flight takes place following this milestone.
[M4.effect.7] Decisions such as the turn-round period, connecting passengers etc are taken and need to be stable at this event.
[M4.effect.8] An estimated in-block time (EIBT) is computed using the ELDT and the estimated taxi-in time.
### Procedures

[M4.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M4.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M4.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M4.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M4.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M4.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M4.operational_status.1] FIR
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M4.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 5: Final Approach

### Definition

[M5.definition.1] The flight enters the Final Approach phase at the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M5.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from ATC.
[M5.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system detects a flight based upon the assigned SSR code and identifies when the flight crosses either a defined range / position or passes/leaves a predetermined level.
### Timing

[M5.timing.1] Dependent upon local parameters that are defined by ATC.
### Data Quality

[M5.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M5.effect.1] Update of the ELDT to determine a new TOBT.
[M5.effect.2] When a flight reaches this stage it is usually between 2 and 5 minutes from landing (depending on the parameter set by ATC).
[M5.effect.3] This is often the prompt for many partners to start moving resources connected with the flight, such as positioning a parking marshal and ground handling services.
### Procedures

[M5.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M5.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M5.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M5.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M5.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M5.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M5.operational_status.1] FINAL
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M5.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 6: Landed

### Definition

[M6.definition.1] ALDT - Actual Landing Time.
[M6.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft touches down on a runway.
[M6.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATA - Actual Time of Arrival landing, ACARS=ON).
### Origin and priority

[M6.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or by ACARS from equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M6.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M6.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M6.effect.1] The occurrence of ALDT triggers an update of downstream estimates: TOBT and TTOT are updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the defined turn-round period for the departing flight.
[M6.effect.2] The EIBT can be updated according to the ALDT +EXIT.
### Procedures

[M6.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M6.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M6.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M6.procedures.6] - Actual Landing Time: ALDT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M6.operational_status.1] LANDED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M6.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT changes to ALDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated

## Milestone 9: Final Confirmation of the TOBT

### Definition

[M9.definition.1] The time at which the Aircraft Operator or Ground Handler provide their most accurate TOBT taking into account the operational situation.
### Origin and priority

[M9.origin_and_priority.1] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler provides the information.
### Timing

[M9.timing.1] The information is provided t minutes before EOBT (t is a parameter time agreed locally).
### Data Quality

[M9.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M9.effect.1] The aim of the final TOBT is to give a timely, accurate and reliable assessment of the off-block time.
[M9.effect.2] It is recognised that main benefits of sharing the TOBT are expected in case of disruptions (internal or external).
[M9.effect.3] In such cases, the difference between EOBT (shared by ATC, Network Operations and Stand / Gate Management) and TOBT may be important.
[M9.effect.4] An accurate TOBT at [EOBT-t minutes] is a pre-requisite for ATC to establish a push back / pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.5] Emphasis is put on the need for the Aircraft Operator to integrate his own strategy to compute a TOBT related to the flight.
[M9.effect.6] Following the receipt of the TOBT, the ATC system will calculate and provide the Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) based on the predicted traffic load, gate / stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the Holding Position, etc. The flight is introduced into the pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.7] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M9.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M9.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M9.procedures.3] This check should be performed at a predefined time (local parameter) to confirm TOBT prior to TSAT issue and verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimates given the updated TOBT.
[M9.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M9.procedures.5] This Milestone Process is actually constantly applicable in the CDM Platform, as soon as a TOBT is available.
[M9.procedures.6] However the confirmed TOBT prior to TSAT has special status, where AO/GH check the quality of TOBT before TSAT issue.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M9.operational_status.1] IN-BLOCK
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M9.action_on_cdm.1] This process is triggered by - a new TOBT or TTOT update.
[M9.action_on_cdm.2] No need to confirm an existing TOBT if it has been manually modified before.

### Definition

[M10.definition.1] The time ATC issues the Target Start Up Approval Time
### Origin and priority

[M10.origin_and_priority.1] ATC
### Timing

[M10.timing.1] The information is provided t-minutes before EOBT, where t is a parameter agreed locally
### Data Quality

[M10.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M10.effect.1] The flight is stabilised into the pre-departure sequence.
[M10.effect.2] The Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M10.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant partners of the TSAT that has been allocated to the flight.
[M10.procedures.2] The Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.3] Second step: To check whether the number of TOBT updates exceeds a tolerance defined locally, after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.4] First: The TSAT will indicate to the partners the time when the start up approval can be expected.
[M10.procedures.5] Network Operations will be informed with a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.6] No check is performed.
[M10.procedures.7] Second: A check shall be performed to see the number of TOBT updates after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.8] In case the number of TOBT updates exceeds a threshold, then the TOBT input should be processed according to local procedure.
[M10.procedures.9] This process is triggered by
[M10.procedures.10] - A defined time (local parameter) before TOBT
[M10.procedures.11] - TOBT update after TSAT issue

[M11.definition.2] This is not to be confused with the time passengers are pre-called to the gate via flight information display systems (FIDS) or public address systems.
### Origin and priority

[M11.origin_and_priority.1] Automatic from airport system or manual input by Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M11.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M11.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M11.effect.1] When boarding commences it gives the Airport CDM Partners a good indication of whether the TOBT/TSAT will be respected.
### Procedures

[M11.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT).
[M11.procedures.2] Second step: To check whether boarding starts in time to respect TOBT and inform the AO/GH in case TOBT needs to be updated.
[M11.procedures.3] Inform of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) when it occurs.
[M11.procedures.4] At a certain time before TOBT (local variable e.g. corresponding to aircraft type) a check shall be performed to check the boarding status.
[M11.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M11.procedures.6] - a time variable <value> minutes before TOBT.
### Operational Status (changes to)

## Milestone 12: Aircraft Ready

### Definition

[M12.definition.1] The time when all doors are closed, boarding bridge removed, push back vehicle connected, ready to taxi immediately upon reception of TWR instructions (ARDT).
### Origin and priority

[M12.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by the Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M12.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M12.data_quality.1] Data is directly available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M12.effect.1] ATC refines the pre-departure sequence.
[M12.effect.2] The pilot requests start up just before TSAT, following coordination with the Ground Handler.
[M12.effect.3] (Dispatcher / Supervisor / Redcap).
### Procedures

[M12.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Ready Time (ARDT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft is ready for start up / push-back.
[M12.procedures.2] Second step: To inform the AO/GH that TOBT has passed and the Airport CDM Platform has not yet received ARDT or Ready Status (RDY).
[M12.procedures.3] Inform of ARDT or RDY confirming that the flight follows the indicated TOBT.
[M12.procedures.4] At TOBT + tolerance the AO/GH are informed that TOBT has passed and there has not been a ready status message yet.
[M12.procedures.5] This procedure is triggered by
[M12.procedures.6] - an input to the Airport CDM Platform.

## Milestone 15: Off-Block

### Definition

[M15.definition.1] AOBT - Actual Off-Block Time.
[M15.definition.2] The time the aircraft pushes back/vacates the parking position (Equivalent to Airline/Handler ATD - Actual Time of Departure ACARS=OUT).
### Origin and priority

[M15.origin_and_priority.1] ACARS equipped aircraft or automated docking systems or ATC systems (e.g. A-SMGCS) or by manual input.
### Timing

[M15.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M15.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M15.effect.1] TTOT updated considering the EXOT.
### Procedures

[M15.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Off-Block Time (AOBT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has commenced push-back / taxi from parking position.
[M15.procedures.2] Second step: To check if TTOT changes by more than the agreed tolerance and inform Network Operations.
[M15.procedures.3] Inform of AOBT when it occurs.
[M15.procedures.4] AOBT always triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations or in the case of remote holding at a defined time prior to TTOT.
[M15.procedures.5] After a first A-DPI is sent this check shall be performed to check TTOT updates against the TTOT tolerance before Network Operations is informed, with a new A-DPI, of the updated TTOT.
[M15.procedures.6] This process is triggered by AOBT detection.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M15.operational_status.1] OFF-BLOCK
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M15.action_on_cdm.1] AOBT recorded
## Milestone 16: Take Off

### Definition

[M16.definition.1] ATOT - Actual Take Off Time.
[M16.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft takes off from the runway.
[M16.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATD-Actual Time of Departure, ACARS = OFF).
### Origin and priority

[M16.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or from ACARS equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M16.timing.1] The information is directly available as soon as possible after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M16.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M16.effect.1] FSA and MVT messages are sent.
### Procedures

[M16.procedures.1] To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners about the actual take off.
[M16.procedures.2] An airborne message is generated and the flight is removed from the departure sequence.
[M16.procedures.3] This process is triggered by Tower FDPS, A-SMGCS / Radar detection or ACARS.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M16.operational_status.1] DEPARTED / TAKE OFF
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M16.action_on_cdm.1] ATOT recorded

# The model

class AirportCDMPartner (package Stakeholders): An abstract party that takes part in the Airport CDM process and is informed of, or provides, milestone information.
  attribute name: String; Name identifying the partner.

class TurnRoundPartner (package Stakeholders): The Aircraft Operator or Ground Handler (AO/GH), referred to jointly by the document, who together manage the turn-round and provide TOBT and related operational times.
  operation updateTOBT(flight: Flight, turnRoundPeriod: Duration): OffBlockTime; TOBT is updated automatically or inserted manually, calculated on the basis of the defined/estimated turn-round period for the departing flight (M3,6,7,8); manual input overrides the EIBT+MTTT estimate so TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT (M2,3).
  operation confirmFinalTOBT(flight: Flight, t: Duration): OffBlockTime; AO/GH provide their most accurate TOBT taking into account the operational situation, t minutes before EOBT, a locally agreed parameter (M9).
  operation reportGroundHandlingStart(flight: Flight): DateTime; Provides ACGT, the actual commencement of ground handling operations, for flights that are the first operation of the day or long-term parked (M8).
  operation reportAircraftReady(flight: Flight): ReadyTime; Provides ARDT: all doors closed, boarding bridge removed, push back vehicle connected, ready to taxi immediately upon reception of TWR instructions (M12).
  operation manageTurnRound(flight: Flight): void; In coordination with the aircrew, manages the turn-round process accordingly following TOBT confirmation and TSAT issue (M9,10).

class AircraftOperator (package Stakeholders): The airline operating the flight, responsible for submitting the ATC Flight Plan and, jointly with the Ground Handler, providing TOBT and turn-round data.
  operation submitAtcFlightPlan(flightPlan: AtcFlightPlan): void; Submits the ICAO ATC Flight Plan, which is distributed by IFPS so all involved ATC units, including departure and destination aerodromes, receive it (M1).
  operation provideCorrectInformation(sobt: OffBlockTime, registration: String, firstDestination: String): void; Must provide correct information before the first E-DPI message, feeding Network Operations with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration and first destination data as early in time as possible (M1).

class GroundHandler (package Stakeholders): The ground handling company that carries out turn-round operations for the flight.

class ATCUnit (package Stakeholders): An air traffic control unit (ACC, Approach Control, Tower, or ATS unit) that receives the flight plan, detects the flight, calculates EXOT, and issues TSAT/ASAT.
  attribute unitRole: String; The role of the ATC unit, e.g. Area Control Centre (ACC), Approach Control Unit, or Tower (M4,5,16).
  operation calculateEXOT(trafficLoad: String, gateStandLocation: String, runwayInUse: String, holdingPositionWait: Duration): TaxiOutTime; The ATC system calculates and provides the Estimated Taxi-Out Time based on the predicted traffic load, gate/stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the Holding Position, following receipt of TOBT (M9).
  operation issueTSAT(flight: Flight, t: Duration): StartUpApprovalTime; ATC issues the Target Start-Up Approval Time, t minutes before EOBT, a locally agreed parameter (M10).
  operation approveStartUp(flight: Flight): StartUpApprovalTime; ATC gives start-up approval (ASAT) following a pilot's request, on receipt of which the aircraft will start up, push back and taxi (M14).
  operation confirmTSATToPilot(flight: Flight): void; ATC confirms TSAT to the pilot to maintain the aircraft in the pre-departure sequence and assure that a regulated flight can respect its CTOT (M13).
  operation detectFlight(flight: Flight): void; Detects the flight based upon its assigned SSR code, when it crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary (M4) or a defined range/position/level on final approach (M5).

class NetworkOperations (package Stakeholders): The network management function that issues CTOT, monitors flight progress via ETFMS, and is kept informed by DPI messages so ATC Flight Plan estimates stay consistent.
  operation issueCTOT(flight: Flight): TakeOffTime; Issues the CTOT, sent to relevant ATS units and the departure aerodrome, for regulated flights at EOBT-2h (M2).
  operation monitorFlightProgress(flight: Flight): void; Monitors progress of the flight using the ETFMS and sends a Flight Update Message providing updates of the flight's progress, when within 3 hrs flying time of the destination (M3).
  operation receiveDPI(message: DPIMessage): void; Receives DPI messages informing it of updated take-off time estimates (M1,2,3,10,15).

class StandGateManagement (package Stakeholders): The party managing stand and gate allocation, which shares EOBT with ATC and Network Operations and is affected by stand/gate change decisions.

class Aircrew (package Stakeholders): The flight crew (pilot) who requests start-up and coordinates with the Ground Handler during the turn-round.
  operation requestStartUp(flight: Flight): DateTime; The pilot requests start-up just before TSAT, following coordination with the Ground Handler, producing the Actual Start-up Request Time (M12,13).

class Flight (package Flights): A single arrival or departure flight tracked through the CDM process from ATC Flight Plan activation to take-off, carrying its operational status and successive time estimates.
  attribute flightId: String; Identifier of the flight.
  attribute direction: String; Whether the flight is an arrival or a departure; one turn-round normally includes an arriving and a departing flight (M1).
  attribute aircraftType: String; Type of aircraft, which the flight plan may be used to update (M1).
  attribute registration: String; Aircraft registration, fed to Network Operations before the first E-DPI (M1).
  attribute operationalStatus: OperationalStatus; The current operational status of the flight, changed at each milestone (M1-16).
  attribute isRegulated: Boolean; Whether the flight is regulated and has received a CTOT; regulated flights usually have priority and have fields marked REGULATED (M2).
  attribute offBlockTime: OffBlockTime; Latest off-block time value (SOBT/EOBT/TOBT/AOBT); every successive value is kept (M1-15).
  attribute inBlockTime: InBlockTime; Latest in-block time value (EIBT/AIBT) (M1,3-7).
  attribute landingTime: LandingTime; Latest landing time value (ELDT/ALDT) (M1-6).
  attribute takeOffTime: TakeOffTime; Latest take-off time value (ETOT/TTOT/CTOT/ATOT) (M1-10,15,16).
  attribute taxiOutTime: TaxiOutTime; Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) (M2,3,9,15).
  attribute taxiInTime: TaxiInTime; Estimated taxi-in time (EXIT) (M4,6).
  attribute startUpApprovalTime: StartUpApprovalTime; Target/Actual start-up approval time (TSAT/ASAT) (M9,10,12-14).
  attribute boardingTime: BoardingTime; Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) (M11).
  attribute readyTime: ReadyTime; Actual Ready Time (ARDT) (M12,13).
  attribute startUpRequestTime: StartUpRequestTime; Actual Start-up Request Time (ASRT) (M13,14).
  operation isReadyWithin15MinOfEOBT(): Boolean; The flight is ready not later than 15 minutes after the planned EOBT (M1).

class TurnRound (package Flights): The aircraft turn-round, normally comprising an arriving flight and a related departing flight, analysed using the minimum turn-round time.
  attribute minimumTurnRoundTime: MinimumTurnRoundTime; MTTT, the minimum/estimated turn-round period used to calculate TOBT and TTOT for the departing flight (M2,3,6,7,8).

class Aircraft (package Flights): The physical aircraft operating a flight, identified by type and registration.
  attribute registration: String; Aircraft registration (M1).
  attribute aircraftType: String; Type of aircraft (M1).

class AtcFlightPlan (package Flights): The ICAO flight plan submitted to ATC for a flight, distributed by IFPS, which may be a one-off submission or a repetitive flight plan (RFPL).
  attribute isRepetitive: Boolean; Whether this is a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) covering daily or weekly flights (M1).
  attribute sobt: DateTime; Scheduled Off-Block Time carried on the flight plan, fed to Network Operations before the first E-DPI (M1).
  attribute destinationAerodrome: String; Destination aerodrome named on the flight plan (M1).
  attribute departureAerodrome: String; Departure aerodrome named on the flight plan (M1).
  attribute submissionTime: DateTime; Time the flight plan is submitted, normally 3 hours before EOBT, though it may be later (M1).
  operation checkConsistency(slot: AirportSlot, flightData: Flight): Boolean; Checks consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent (M1).

class AirportSlot (package Flights): The airport slot allocated to the flight under the airport slot programme, with which the ATC Flight Plan must correspond.
  attribute slotTime: DateTime; The scheduled slot time under the airport slot programme (M1).

class TimeQuantity (package Timings): A time quantity describing a flight's progress, named by a prefix (Scheduled, Estimated, Target, Actual, Calculated) and an event, with its origin, accuracy and tolerance; every successive value is kept.
  attribute prefix: PrefixType; Whether the value is Scheduled, Estimated, Target, Actual or Calculated.
  attribute value: DateTime; The time value itself.
  attribute accuracy: String; The stated accuracy of the value, e.g. +/- 1 minute (M3,6,7,8,11,12,13,14,15,16).
  attribute tolerance: Duration; The tolerance agreed, locally or otherwise, before a change is reported, e.g. the TTOT tolerance or TSAT tolerance window (M3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10,12,13,14,15).
  attribute originator: String; Who provided or updated this value, e.g. AO/GH, ATC, ACARS, Network Operations.
  attribute timeOfUpdate: DateTime; The time at which this value was recorded, so the turn-round can be analysed afterwards.
  operation recordValue(value: DateTime, originator: String): void; Appends a new successive value with its originator and time of update rather than overwriting the previous value.

class OffBlockTime (package Timings): The off-block time for the flight, held as SOBT, EOBT, TOBT or AOBT depending on its prefix.

class InBlockTime (package Timings): The in-block time for the flight, held as EIBT or AIBT depending on its prefix.
  operation calculateEIBT(landingTime: LandingTime, exit: TaxiInTime): DateTime; EIBT is computed using the ELDT/ALDT and the estimated/actual taxi-in time EXIT (M4,6).

class LandingTime (package Timings): The landing time for the flight, held as ELDT or ALDT depending on its prefix.
  operation calculateELDT(atot: TakeOffTime, estimatedElapsedTime: Duration): DateTime; ELDT is calculated using the ATOT and the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL (M3).

class TakeOffTime (package Timings): The take-off time for the flight, held as ETOT, TTOT, CTOT or ATOT depending on its prefix.
  operation calculateTTOT(eibt: InBlockTime, mttt: MinimumTurnRoundTime, exot: TaxiOutTime, eobt: OffBlockTime, tobt: OffBlockTime): DateTime; Calculation basis for TTOT is EIBT+MTTT+EXOT, if later than EOBT+EXOT (M2); if EOBT is later than EIBT+MTTT, TTOT equals EOBT+EXOT (M3); if TOBT is available it overrules the EIBT+MTTT estimate, so TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT (M2,3).

class TaxiOutTime (package Timings): The Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT), calculated by the ATC system.

class TaxiInTime (package Timings): The estimated taxi-in time (EXIT), used with ELDT/ALDT to compute EIBT.

class MinimumTurnRoundTime (package Timings): The minimum/estimated turn-round period (MTTT) used as the basis for calculating TOBT and TTOT for a departing flight.

class StartUpApprovalTime (package Timings): The start-up approval time for the flight, held as TSAT or ASAT depending on its prefix.

class BoardingTime (package Timings): The Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT), when the gate opens for passengers to physically start boarding.

class ReadyTime (package Timings): The Actual Ready Time (ARDT), when the aircraft's doors are closed, boarding bridge removed, and push-back vehicle connected.

class StartUpRequestTime (package Timings): The Actual Start-Up Request Time (ASRT), when start-up is requested by the pilot.

class Milestone (package Milestones): One of the sixteen defined events of the Airport CDM process, each with its trigger, timing rule, effect on operational status, and procedure.
  attribute name: String; The name of the milestone.
  attribute trigger: String; What triggers this milestone process, as stated by the document.
  attribute timingRule: String; The timing rule stated for when this milestone normally occurs.
  attribute effect: String; The effect the milestone has on estimates and decisions, as stated by the document.
  attribute operationalStatusChange: OperationalStatus; The operational status the flight changes to at this milestone.

class M1FlightPlanActivated (package Milestones): The milestone at which the ICAO flight plan is submitted to ATC and the Airport CDM Platform is initiated for the flight.
  operation checkConsistency(flightPlan: AtcFlightPlan, slot: AirportSlot, flightData: Flight): Boolean; Checks consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent, to confirm the flight to Network Operations and allow further local processing (M1).
  operation sendFirstEDPI(flight: Flight): DPIMessage; Sends the first E-DPI message once correct SOBT, registration and first destination data are provided; should not be sent if information is missing or inconsistent; triggered by first activation of the flight plan (earliest EOBT-3hr) or new/late submissions after cancellation or revised EOBT (M1).
  operation updateArrivalEstimates(flight: Flight): void; For an arrival, ELDT and EIBT are updated (M1).
  operation updateDepartureEstimates(flight: Flight): void; For a departure, EOBT and ETOT are updated (M1).
  operation initiateOutboundFlight(flight: Flight): void; For non-coordinated airports the flight plan is used to initiate the outbound flight; for coordinated airports the outbound flight is already known (M1).

class M2EOBTMinus2h (package Milestones): The milestone at EOBT-2h at which most flights are known, regulated flights receive a CTOT, and Network Operations is informed of the updated take-off time estimate.
  operation checkFlightEstimatesConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks, before or after take-off from outstation, whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan, verifying feasibility of EOBT at EOBT-2hrs (M2).
  operation sendFirstTDPI(flight: Flight): DPIMessage; Informs Network Operations of the updated take-off time estimate via a first T-DPI message at EOBT-2hrs (M2).
  operation markRegulated(flight: Flight): void; Marks appropriate fields as REGULATED for a flight that has received a CTOT (M2).
  operation updateInboundELDT(flight: Flight, fum: FlightUpdateMessage): void; For inbound flights, ELDT is updated based on information provided by FUM messages, taking into account the actual progress of the flight (M2).

class M3TakeOffFromOutstation (package Milestones): The milestone at which the ATOT from the outstation is recorded and used to check and update downstream estimates.
  operation checkLandingEstimateConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH estimated landing time after take-off from outstation is consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan (M3).
  operation sendTDPIt(flight: Flight): DPIMessage; Informs Network Operations about updated take-off time estimates using a T-DPI-t message when needed, respecting a TTOT tolerance of 5 minutes before informing (M3).
  operation receiveOutstationATOT(flight: Flight): TakeOffTime; Records ATOT, received from either the Network Operations FUM or via the AO/GH, if the destination is more than 3 hrs flying time away (M3).

class M4LocalRadarUpdate (package Milestones): The milestone at which the flight enters the FIR or local airspace of the destination airport, detected by radar via the assigned SSR code.
  operation commenceTOBTProcess(flight: Flight): void; Commences the TOBT process and checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan, given the updated ELDT (M4).
  operation informNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance (M4).
  operation preventStandGateChangeAfterELDTMinus30(flight: Flight): Boolean; Any change to a stand or gate is not preferred after ELDT-30', taking into account the taxi-in time EXIT, to decrease last-minute stand/gate changes (M4).

class M5FinalApproach (package Milestones): The milestone at which the flight enters the Final Approach phase, detected by radar crossing a defined range/position or level.
  operation commenceTOBTProcess(flight: Flight): void; Commences the TOBT process and checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan, given the updated ELDT (M5).
  operation informNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance (M5).

class M6Landed (package Milestones): The milestone at which ALDT, the actual landing time, is recorded.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan (M6).
  operation informNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance (M6).
  operation updateELDTtoALDT(flight: Flight): void; ELDT changes to ALDT on landing, triggering an update of downstream estimates TOBT and TTOT, and EIBT may be updated as ALDT+EXIT (M6).

class M7InBlock (package Milestones): The milestone at which AIBT, the actual in-block time, is recorded.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan (M7).
  operation informNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance (M7).
  operation updateEIBTtoAIBT(flight: Flight): void; EIBT changes to AIBT, triggering an update of downstream estimates TOBT and TTOT, calculated on the basis of the estimated turn-round period for the departing flight (M7).

class M8GroundHandlingStarted (package Milestones): The milestone at which ground handling operations commence (ACGT), specific to flights that are the first operation of the day or have been long-term parked.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan (M8).
  operation informNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance (M8).

class M9FinalConfirmationTOBT (package Milestones): The milestone at which the AO/GH provides its most accurate TOBT, t minutes before EOBT, prior to TSAT issue.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; At a predefined local time, checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan, to confirm TOBT prior to TSAT issue (M9).
  operation informNetworkOperationsIfTTOTChange(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; Informs Network Operations when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance (M9).
  operation confirmTOBT(flight: Flight): Boolean; Triggered by a new TOBT or TTOT update; no need to confirm an existing TOBT if it has already been manually modified before (M9).

class M10TSATIssued (package Milestones): The milestone at which ATC issues the Target Start-Up Approval Time (TSAT), t minutes before EOBT.
  operation informPartnersOfTSAT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant partners of the TSAT allocated to the flight; Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non-regulated flights (M10).
  operation checkTOBTUpdateCount(flight: Flight, threshold: Integer): Boolean; Checks whether the number of TOBT updates after TSAT has been issued exceeds a locally defined tolerance; if exceeded the TOBT input is processed according to local procedure (M10).

class M11BoardingStarts (package Milestones): The milestone at which the gate opens for passengers to physically start boarding (ASBT).
  operation informPartnersOfASBT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of the Actual Start Boarding Time when it occurs (M11).
  operation checkBoardingStatus(flight: Flight, timeBeforeTOBT: Duration): Boolean; At a locally defined time before TOBT (e.g. corresponding to aircraft type), checks the boarding status and informs the AO/GH if TOBT needs updating (M11).

class M12AircraftReady (package Milestones): The milestone at which the aircraft is ready for start-up/push-back (ARDT).
  operation informPartnersOfARDT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of ARDT or Ready Status (RDY) in the Airport CDM Platform, confirming that the flight follows the indicated TOBT (M12).
  operation alertIfNoReadyStatus(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; At TOBT + tolerance, informs the AO/GH that TOBT has passed and no ready status message has yet been received (M12).

class M13StartUpRequested (package Milestones): The milestone at which start-up is requested (ASRT), based on the pilot's request to ATC.
  operation informPartnersOfASRT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of the Actual Start-up Request Time when it occurs (M13).
  operation alertIfNoStartUpRequest(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; If the start-up request is not made by TSAT + tolerance, informs the AO/GH that no start-up has been requested and that TOBT should be updated (M13).

class M14StartUpApproved (package Milestones): The milestone at which the aircraft receives its start-up approval (ASAT).
  operation informPartnersOfASAT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of ASAT in the Airport CDM Platform, confirming that the aircraft has received start-up approval/push-back clearance (M14).
  operation checkASATAgainstTSAT(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): Boolean; Checks if ASAT is in accordance with TSAT; if start-up approval is not granted at TSAT + tolerance, all relevant partners are informed and the flight is re-sequenced (M14).

class M15OffBlock (package Milestones): The milestone at which the aircraft pushes back/vacates the parking position (AOBT).
  operation informPartnersOfAOBT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of AOBT in the Airport CDM Platform, that the aircraft has commenced push-back/taxi from the parking position (M15).
  operation sendADPI(flight: Flight): DPIMessage; AOBT always triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations, or in the case of remote holding, at a defined time prior to TTOT (M15).
  operation checkTTOTChangeAndInform(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; After the first A-DPI is sent, checks TTOT updates against the TTOT tolerance before Network Operations is informed, with a new A-DPI, of the updated TTOT (M15).

class M16TakeOff (package Milestones): The milestone at which the aircraft takes off from the runway (ATOT).
  operation informPartnersOfATOT(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners about the actual take-off (M16).
  operation generateAirborneMessageAndRemoveFromSequence(flight: Flight): void; Generates an airborne message and removes the flight from the departure sequence, triggered by Tower FDPS, A-SMGCS/Radar detection or ACARS (M16).
  operation sendFSAandMVT(flight: Flight): void; Sends FSA and MVT messages (M16).

class AirportCDMPlatform (package Systems): The Airport CDM Platform, the technical system initiated for each flight that processes available information, computes estimates, and records the Actions on CDM Operation (ACISP) at each milestone.
  attribute initiatedFlight: Flight; The flight for which the platform has been initiated (M1).
  operation initiateForFlight(flight: Flight): void; The Airport CDM Platform is initiated for this flight and all available information is processed (M1).
  operation computeTOBTAutomatically(flight: Flight): OffBlockTime; TOBT is calculated automatically by the Airport CDM Platform, following an ELDT update (M4).
  operation recordAOBT(flight: Flight, aobt: DateTime): void; Records AOBT (M15).
  operation recordATOT(flight: Flight, atot: DateTime): void; Records ATOT (M16).

class IFPS (package Systems): The system that distributes the submitted ATC Flight Plan to all involved ATC units.
  operation distributeFlightPlan(flightPlan: AtcFlightPlan): void; Distributes the ATC Flight Plan submitted by the Aircraft Operator so that all involved ATC units, including departure and destination aerodromes, receive it (M1).

class ETFMS (package Systems): The system Network Operations uses to monitor progress of a flight within 3 hrs flying time of the destination airport.
  operation monitorProgress(flight: Flight): void; Monitors progress of the flight so that Network Operations can send a Flight Update Message providing updates of the flight's progress (M3).

class ACARS (package Systems): The aircraft system that can automatically report actual times equivalent to ALDT, AIBT, AOBT and ATOT.
  operation reportEvent(flight: Flight, eventType: String): DateTime; Reports an actual time (ACARS=ON at landing M6, IN at in-block M7, OUT at off-block M15, OFF at take-off M16), with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.

class AirportSurveillanceSystem (package Systems): The radar, A-SMGCS or Tower FDPS system that detects a flight by its assigned SSR code crossing the FIR/ATC boundary, entering final approach, or taking off.
  operation detectFIRCrossing(flight: Flight): void; Detects a flight based upon the assigned SSR code when it crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary (M4).
  operation detectFinalApproach(flight: Flight): void; Detects when the flight crosses a defined range/position or passes/leaves a predetermined level on final approach (M5).
  operation detectTakeOff(flight: Flight): void; Detects take-off via Tower FDPS, A-SMGCS or Radar (M16).

class DPIMessage (package Messages): A Departure Planning Information message (E-DPI, T-DPI, T-DPI-t, T-DPI-s or A-DPI) sent to Network Operations to inform of updated take-off time estimates.
  attribute type: DPIType; The kind of DPI message sent, as named by the document (E-DPI M1, T-DPI M2, T-DPI-t M3, T-DPI-s M10, A-DPI M15).
  attribute sentAt: DateTime; The time the message was sent.
  operation send(flight: Flight): void; Sends the message to Network Operations.

class FlightUpdateMessage (package Messages): The FUM sent by Network Operations, via ETFMS, that provides updates of a flight's progress and is used to update ELDT.
  attribute progressData: String; The progress data the FUM provides (M2,3).

class FSAMessage (package Messages): The FSA message sent when the aircraft takes off.

class MVTMessage (package Messages): The MVT message sent when the aircraft takes off.

class AirborneMessage (package Messages): The airborne message generated when the aircraft takes off, after which the flight is removed from the departure sequence.

class ReadyStatusMessage (package Messages): The Ready Status (RDY) message that may be sent instead of ARDT, confirming the flight follows the indicated TOBT.
