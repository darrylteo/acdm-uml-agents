# The document: sixteen A-CDM milestones, sentences numbered

## Milestone 1: ATC Flight Plan Activated

### Definition

[M1.definition.1] The ICAO flight plan is submitted to ATC.
[M1.definition.2] The Airport CDM Platform is initiated for this flight, and all available information is processed.
### Origin and priority

[M1.origin_and_priority.1] The ATC Flight Plan is submitted by the Aircraft Operator and distributed by the IFPS.
[M1.origin_and_priority.2] All involved ATC units receive the flight plan, including departure and destination aerodromes.
### Timing

[M1.timing.1] Normally this takes place 3 hours before EOBT, however it may be later.
[M1.timing.2] In some cases a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) has been submitted, covering daily or weekly flights.
### Data Quality

[M1.data_quality.1] The ATC Flight Plan corresponds to the airport slot programme.
### Effect

[M1.effect.1] One aircraft turn-round normally includes an arriving and a departing flight, meaning that it will have two related flight plans.
[M1.effect.2] For coordinated airports, the outbound flight is already known.
[M1.effect.3] The flight plan may be used to update certain information such as type of aircraft.
[M1.effect.4] For long distance flights, the ELDT may differ from the airport slot.
[M1.effect.5] For non coordinated airports, the flight plan is used to initiate the outbound flight.
[M1.effect.6] The flight is ready not later than 15 minutes after the planned EOBT.
[M1.effect.7] The DPI process commences the correct messaging with Network Operations (if implemented - see attachment 2 for details).
### Procedures

[M1.procedures.1] To check consistency between ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data and then confirm the flight to the Network Operations and allow further local processing of the flight.
[M1.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify the consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent.
[M1.procedures.3] The AO must provide correct information before this first E-DPI message, in order to feed Network Operations with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration, and first destination data, as early in time as possible.
[M1.procedures.4] The E-DPI message should not be sent if no or inconsistent information is provided.
[M1.procedures.5] This process is triggered by:
[M1.procedures.6] - The first activation of the ATC Flight Plan (earliest EOBT-3 hr), or
[M1.procedures.7] - New or late submissions of the ATC Flight Plan, after cancellation or revised EOBT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M1.operational_status.1] INITIATED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M1.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT and EIBT updated for an arrival EOBT and ETOT updated for a departure The DPI process commences (if implemented - see section 3.7.3 for details).
## Milestone 2: EOBT - 2 hr

### Definition

[M2.definition.1] At EOBT-2 hr most flights will be known in the Airport CDM Platform including if they are regulated or not.
[M2.definition.2] All regulated flights receive a CTOT from Network Operations.
### Origin and priority

[M2.origin_and_priority.1] The CTOT is issued by the Network Operations and is sent to relevant ATS units as well as the departure aerodrome.
[M2.origin_and_priority.2] CTOT flights usually have a priority over unregulated flights.
### Timing

[M2.timing.1] If the flight is regulated, a CTOT is issued at EOBT-2h.
### Data Quality

[M2.data_quality.1] Not applicable.
### Effect

[M2.effect.1] For inbound flights, ELDT is updated based on information provided by the FUM messages, taking into account the actual progress of the flight.
### Procedures

[M2.procedures.1] To check (before or after take off from outstation) whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan and to inform Network Operations about the updated take off time estimate, using a T-DPI Message.
[M2.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimated off block time at EOBT-2 hrs.
[M2.procedures.3] At EOBT-2 hrs Network Operations is informed through the first T-DPI message.
[M2.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT, if later than EOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.5] In the case of manual input of TOBT, this estimate will override the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.6] This procedure is triggered by
[M2.procedures.7] - a time stamp, at EOBT - 2h.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M2.operational_status.1] N. A.
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M2.action_on_cdm.1] ETOT/TTOT/CTOT Mark appropriate fields as REGULATED
## Milestone 3: Take Off from Outstation

### Definition

[M3.definition.1] The ATOT from the outstation (ADEP)
### Origin and priority

[M3.origin_and_priority.1] The outstation provides ATOT to the Network Operations and Aircraft Operator.
### Timing

[M3.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M3.data_quality.1] The accuracy of ATOT is +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M3.effect.1] If the departure airport is more than 3hrs flying time from the destination airport the ATOT is received from either the Network Operations FUM or via the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler.
[M3.effect.2] Using the ATOT an ELDT can be calculated by using the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL.
[M3.effect.3] If the flight is within 3hrs flying time of the destination airport the Network Operations monitors progress of the flight using the ETFMS and send a Flight Update Message (FUM) that provides updates of the flight's progress.
### Procedures

[M3.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH estimated landing time after take off from outstation are consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan, and when needed inform the Network Operations about the updated take off time estimates using a T-DPI-t Message.
[M3.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan at take off from outstation.
[M3.procedures.3] A TTOT tolerance of 5 minutes is respected before Network Operations is informed of the updated TTOT.
[M3.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.5] In case EOBT is later than EIBT+MTTT, TTOT equals EOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.6] In the case where TOBT is available this prediction will overrule the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.7] This process is triggered by
[M3.procedures.8] - the take off from outstation.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M3.operational_status.1] AIRBORNE
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M3.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 4: Local Radar Update

### Definition

[M4.definition.1] The flight enters the FIR (Flight Information Region) or the local airspace of the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M4.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from the Area Control Centre (ACC) or Approach Control Unit that is associated with an airport.
[M4.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system is able to detect a flight based upon the assigned SSR code when the flight crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary.
### Timing

[M4.timing.1] Dependent upon the position of the airport in relation to the FIR boundary.
### Data Quality

[M4.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M4.effect.1] Update of the ELDT can trigger a new TOBT to be entered by the AO/GH, or calculated automatically by the Airport CDM Platform.
[M4.effect.2] The accuracy of ELDT is particularly important at this stage since downstream decisions are taken, such as stand /gate / aircraft changes, preparation of arrival sequence, preparation of ground handling operations, decisions for connecting passengers.
[M4.effect.3] Uncertainty and ELDT non-accuracy at this stage significantly increase risks for bad and last minute decisions and internal disruptions.
[M4.effect.4] The objective to decrease the number of stand and gate changes in the last 30 minutes requires high accuracy regarding departure and arrival times.
[M4.effect.5] Therefore, taking into account the taxi-in time (EXIT), any change to a stand or gate is not preferred after ELDT-30'.
[M4.effect.6] The update of TOBT for the related departing flight takes place following this milestone.
[M4.effect.7] Decisions such as the turn-round period, connecting passengers etc are taken and need to be stable at this event.
[M4.effect.8] An estimated in-block time (EIBT) is computed using the ELDT and the estimated taxi-in time.
### Procedures

[M4.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M4.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M4.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M4.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M4.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M4.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M4.operational_status.1] FIR
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M4.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 5: Final Approach

### Definition

[M5.definition.1] The flight enters the Final Approach phase at the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M5.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from ATC.
[M5.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system detects a flight based upon the assigned SSR code and identifies when the flight crosses either a defined range / position or passes/leaves a predetermined level.
### Timing

[M5.timing.1] Dependent upon local parameters that are defined by ATC.
### Data Quality

[M5.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M5.effect.1] Update of the ELDT to determine a new TOBT.
[M5.effect.2] When a flight reaches this stage it is usually between 2 and 5 minutes from landing (depending on the parameter set by ATC).
[M5.effect.3] This is often the prompt for many partners to start moving resources connected with the flight, such as positioning a parking marshal and ground handling services.
### Procedures

[M5.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M5.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M5.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M5.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M5.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M5.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M5.operational_status.1] FINAL
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M5.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 6: Landed

### Definition

[M6.definition.1] ALDT - Actual Landing Time.
[M6.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft touches down on a runway.
[M6.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATA - Actual Time of Arrival landing, ACARS=ON).
### Origin and priority

[M6.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or by ACARS from equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M6.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M6.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M6.effect.1] The occurrence of ALDT triggers an update of downstream estimates: TOBT and TTOT are updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the defined turn-round period for the departing flight.
[M6.effect.2] The EIBT can be updated according to the ALDT +EXIT.
### Procedures

[M6.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M6.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M6.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M6.procedures.6] - Actual Landing Time: ALDT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M6.operational_status.1] LANDED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M6.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT changes to ALDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated

## Milestone 9: Final Confirmation of the TOBT

### Definition

[M9.definition.1] The time at which the Aircraft Operator or Ground Handler provide their most accurate TOBT taking into account the operational situation.
### Origin and priority

[M9.origin_and_priority.1] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler provides the information.
### Timing

[M9.timing.1] The information is provided t minutes before EOBT (t is a parameter time agreed locally).
### Data Quality

[M9.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M9.effect.1] The aim of the final TOBT is to give a timely, accurate and reliable assessment of the off-block time.
[M9.effect.2] It is recognised that main benefits of sharing the TOBT are expected in case of disruptions (internal or external).
[M9.effect.3] In such cases, the difference between EOBT (shared by ATC, Network Operations and Stand / Gate Management) and TOBT may be important.
[M9.effect.4] An accurate TOBT at [EOBT-t minutes] is a pre-requisite for ATC to establish a push back / pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.5] Emphasis is put on the need for the Aircraft Operator to integrate his own strategy to compute a TOBT related to the flight.
[M9.effect.6] Following the receipt of the TOBT, the ATC system will calculate and provide the Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) based on the predicted traffic load, gate / stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the Holding Position, etc. The flight is introduced into the pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.7] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M9.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M9.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M9.procedures.3] This check should be performed at a predefined time (local parameter) to confirm TOBT prior to TSAT issue and verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimates given the updated TOBT.
[M9.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M9.procedures.5] This Milestone Process is actually constantly applicable in the CDM Platform, as soon as a TOBT is available.
[M9.procedures.6] However the confirmed TOBT prior to TSAT has special status, where AO/GH check the quality of TOBT before TSAT issue.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M9.operational_status.1] IN-BLOCK
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M9.action_on_cdm.1] This process is triggered by - a new TOBT or TTOT update.
[M9.action_on_cdm.2] No need to confirm an existing TOBT if it has been manually modified before.

### Definition

[M10.definition.1] The time ATC issues the Target Start Up Approval Time
### Origin and priority

[M10.origin_and_priority.1] ATC
### Timing

[M10.timing.1] The information is provided t-minutes before EOBT, where t is a parameter agreed locally
### Data Quality

[M10.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M10.effect.1] The flight is stabilised into the pre-departure sequence.
[M10.effect.2] The Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M10.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant partners of the TSAT that has been allocated to the flight.
[M10.procedures.2] The Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.3] Second step: To check whether the number of TOBT updates exceeds a tolerance defined locally, after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.4] First: The TSAT will indicate to the partners the time when the start up approval can be expected.
[M10.procedures.5] Network Operations will be informed with a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.6] No check is performed.
[M10.procedures.7] Second: A check shall be performed to see the number of TOBT updates after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.8] In case the number of TOBT updates exceeds a threshold, then the TOBT input should be processed according to local procedure.
[M10.procedures.9] This process is triggered by
[M10.procedures.10] - A defined time (local parameter) before TOBT
[M10.procedures.11] - TOBT update after TSAT issue

[M11.definition.2] This is not to be confused with the time passengers are pre-called to the gate via flight information display systems (FIDS) or public address systems.
### Origin and priority

[M11.origin_and_priority.1] Automatic from airport system or manual input by Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M11.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M11.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M11.effect.1] When boarding commences it gives the Airport CDM Partners a good indication of whether the TOBT/TSAT will be respected.
### Procedures

[M11.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT).
[M11.procedures.2] Second step: To check whether boarding starts in time to respect TOBT and inform the AO/GH in case TOBT needs to be updated.
[M11.procedures.3] Inform of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) when it occurs.
[M11.procedures.4] At a certain time before TOBT (local variable e.g. corresponding to aircraft type) a check shall be performed to check the boarding status.
[M11.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M11.procedures.6] - a time variable <value> minutes before TOBT.
### Operational Status (changes to)

## Milestone 12: Aircraft Ready

### Definition

[M12.definition.1] The time when all doors are closed, boarding bridge removed, push back vehicle connected, ready to taxi immediately upon reception of TWR instructions (ARDT).
### Origin and priority

[M12.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by the Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M12.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M12.data_quality.1] Data is directly available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M12.effect.1] ATC refines the pre-departure sequence.
[M12.effect.2] The pilot requests start up just before TSAT, following coordination with the Ground Handler.
[M12.effect.3] (Dispatcher / Supervisor / Redcap).
### Procedures

[M12.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Ready Time (ARDT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft is ready for start up / push-back.
[M12.procedures.2] Second step: To inform the AO/GH that TOBT has passed and the Airport CDM Platform has not yet received ARDT or Ready Status (RDY).
[M12.procedures.3] Inform of ARDT or RDY confirming that the flight follows the indicated TOBT.
[M12.procedures.4] At TOBT + tolerance the AO/GH are informed that TOBT has passed and there has not been a ready status message yet.
[M12.procedures.5] This procedure is triggered by
[M12.procedures.6] - an input to the Airport CDM Platform.

## Milestone 15: Off-Block

### Definition

[M15.definition.1] AOBT - Actual Off-Block Time.
[M15.definition.2] The time the aircraft pushes back/vacates the parking position (Equivalent to Airline/Handler ATD - Actual Time of Departure ACARS=OUT).
### Origin and priority

[M15.origin_and_priority.1] ACARS equipped aircraft or automated docking systems or ATC systems (e.g. A-SMGCS) or by manual input.
### Timing

[M15.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M15.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M15.effect.1] TTOT updated considering the EXOT.
### Procedures

[M15.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Off-Block Time (AOBT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has commenced push-back / taxi from parking position.
[M15.procedures.2] Second step: To check if TTOT changes by more than the agreed tolerance and inform Network Operations.
[M15.procedures.3] Inform of AOBT when it occurs.
[M15.procedures.4] AOBT always triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations or in the case of remote holding at a defined time prior to TTOT.
[M15.procedures.5] After a first A-DPI is sent this check shall be performed to check TTOT updates against the TTOT tolerance before Network Operations is informed, with a new A-DPI, of the updated TTOT.
[M15.procedures.6] This process is triggered by AOBT detection.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M15.operational_status.1] OFF-BLOCK
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M15.action_on_cdm.1] AOBT recorded
## Milestone 16: Take Off

### Definition

[M16.definition.1] ATOT - Actual Take Off Time.
[M16.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft takes off from the runway.
[M16.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATD-Actual Time of Departure, ACARS = OFF).
### Origin and priority

[M16.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or from ACARS equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M16.timing.1] The information is directly available as soon as possible after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M16.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M16.effect.1] FSA and MVT messages are sent.
### Procedures

[M16.procedures.1] To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners about the actual take off.
[M16.procedures.2] An airborne message is generated and the flight is removed from the departure sequence.
[M16.procedures.3] This process is triggered by Tower FDPS, A-SMGCS / Radar detection or ACARS.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M16.operational_status.1] DEPARTED / TAKE OFF
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M16.action_on_cdm.1] ATOT recorded

# The model

class AirportCDMPartner (package Stakeholders): An abstract party that takes part in the Airport CDM process, originating or receiving flight information.

class AircraftOperator (package Stakeholders): The airline responsible for a flight, which submits flight plan data and provides TOBT and turn-round information.

class GroundHandler (package Stakeholders): The party performing ground handling operations on behalf of the Aircraft Operator, providing TOBT, ready and boarding information.

class AirTrafficControlUnit (package Stakeholders): An air traffic control unit, including ACC and Approach Control, that manages the aircraft's progress and issues clearances such as TSAT and start-up approval.

class NetworkOperations (package Stakeholders): The Network Management function that issues CTOT, monitors flight progress via ETFMS, and receives DPI and FUM messages.
  operation issueCtot(flight: Flight): Ctot; If the flight is regulated, a CTOT is issued by Network Operations at EOBT-2h and is sent to relevant ATS units as well as the departure aerodrome (milestone 2).

class StandGateManagement (package Stakeholders): The stakeholder responsible for allocating and managing stands and gates for arriving and departing aircraft.
  operation checkStandGateChangeAllowed(eldt: Eldt, exit: Exit): Boolean; Taking into account the taxi-in time (EXIT), any change to a stand or gate is not preferred after ELDT-30' (milestone 4).

class Aircrew (package Stakeholders): The pilot and flight crew who request start-up and coordinate the turn-round and pre-departure sequence.

class Flight (package Flights): A single scheduled arrival or departure operation of an aircraft, tracked through the sixteen milestones with an operational status.
  attribute operationalStatus: OperationalStatus; The current operational status of the flight as changed by each milestone (e.g. INITIATED, AIRBORNE, LANDED, OFF-BLOCK).
  attribute direction: String; Whether the flight is the arriving or departing leg of the aircraft's turn-round (milestone 1).
  attribute regulated: Boolean; Whether the flight has been issued a CTOT and its fields marked REGULATED, giving it priority over unregulated flights (milestone 2).
  attribute readinessDeadlineOffset: Duration; The flight is ready not later than 15 minutes after the planned EOBT (milestone 1).
  attribute eobt: Eobt; The flight's Estimated Off-Block Time, used as the reference point for milestone triggers such as EOBT-3hr and EOBT-2hr (milestones 1,2,3,9,10).
  attribute tobt: Tobt; The flight's Target Off-Block Time, provided and updated by the AO/GH and checked at every downstream milestone (milestones 2-13).
  attribute aobt: Aobt; The flight's Actual Off-Block Time, recorded when the aircraft pushes back (milestone 15).
  attribute etot: Etot; The flight's Estimated Take-Off Time, updated at flight plan activation and EOBT-2hr (milestones 1,2).
  attribute ttot: Ttot; The flight's Target/Calculated Take-Off Time, computed from EIBT+MTTT+EXOT or TOBT+EXOT and reported to Network Operations when it changes beyond tolerance (milestones 2-10,15).
  attribute ctot: Ctot; The flight's Calculated Take-Off Time issued by Network Operations at EOBT-2hr for regulated flights (milestones 2,13).
  attribute eldt: Eldt; The flight's Estimated Landing Time, updated progressively from flight plan activation through final approach (milestones 1-6).
  attribute aldt: Aldt; The flight's Actual Landing Time, recorded on touchdown (milestone 6).
  attribute eibt: Eibt; The flight's Estimated In-Block Time, computed from ELDT or ALDT plus taxi-in time (milestones 1,2,3,4,6,7).
  attribute aibt: Aibt; The flight's Actual In-Block Time, recorded on arrival in-blocks (milestones 7,8).
  attribute atot: Atot; The flight's Actual Take-Off Time, recorded from an outstation or at the home airport (milestones 3,16).
  attribute exot: Exot; The flight's Estimated Taxi-Out Time calculated by the ATC system, used in TTOT calculation (milestones 2,3,9,15).
  attribute exit: Exit; The flight's estimated taxi-in time used with ELDT or ALDT to compute EIBT (milestones 4,6).
  attribute mttt: Mttt; The flight's minimum turn-round time used with EIBT to calculate TTOT (milestones 2,3).
  attribute ettt: Ettt; The flight's estimated turn-round time on which TOBT is calculated after ground handling starts (milestone 8).
  attribute acgt: Acgt; The flight's Actual Commence of Ground Handling Time, equal to AIBT for a normal turn-round (milestones 7,8).
  attribute tsat: Tsat; The flight's Target Start-Up Approval Time issued by ATC, stabilising it in the pre-departure sequence (milestones 9-14).
  attribute asbt: Asbt; The flight's Actual Start Boarding Time, recorded when boarding physically starts (milestone 11).
  attribute ardt: Ardt; The flight's Actual Ready Time, recorded when the aircraft is ready to taxi (milestone 12).
  attribute asrt: Asrt; The flight's Actual Start-up Request Time, recorded when the pilot requests start-up (milestone 13).
  attribute asat: Asat; The flight's Actual Start-up Approval Time, recorded when start-up approval is received (milestone 14).

class TurnRound (package Flights): The aircraft turn-round linking an arriving flight and its related departing flight at an airport.
  attribute turnRoundType: String; Whether the turn-round is a normal turn-round (ACGT commences at AIBT) or a first-of-day/long-term-parked turn-round with a separately recorded ACGT (milestone 8).
  operation calculateDepartingFlightTimings(turnRoundPeriod: Duration): void; TOBT and TTOT are updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the defined or estimated turn-round period for the departing flight (milestones 6, 7, 8).

class Aircraft (package Flights): The physical aircraft operating a flight, whose type and registration are recorded and used in flight plan consistency checks.
  attribute aircraftType: String; The type of aircraft, which may be updated from the ATC Flight Plan (milestone 1).
  attribute registration: String; The aircraft registration, provided by the Aircraft Operator before the first E-DPI message (milestone 1).
  operation updateAircraftType(aircraftType: String): void; The flight plan may be used to update certain information such as type of aircraft (milestone 1).

class FlightPlan (package Flights): The ICAO ATC Flight Plan submitted for a flight, possibly as part of a repetitive flight plan, used to check consistency with slot and airport data.
  attribute repetitive: Boolean; Whether the flight plan is a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) covering daily or weekly flights (milestone 1).
  attribute scheduledOffBlockTime: Sobt; The Scheduled Off-Block Time fed to Network Operations before the first E-DPI message (milestone 1).
  attribute firstDestination: String; The first destination aerodrome data fed to Network Operations before the first E-DPI message (milestone 1).

class AirportSlot (package Flights): The airport slot programme entry against which the ATC Flight Plan's consistency is checked.

class AirportFlightData (package Flights): The local airport data describing a flight, checked for consistency against the ATC Flight Plan and Airport Slot.

class DepartureSequence (package Flights): The pre-departure and departure sequence into which a flight is placed after TOBT confirmation and TSAT issue, and from which it is removed at take-off.
  operation addFlight(flight: Flight): void; The flight is introduced into the pre-departure sequence following receipt of TOBT (milestone 9).
  operation resequenceFlight(flight: Flight): void; In case the start up approval is not granted at TSAT + tolerance, the flight will be re-sequenced (milestone 14).
  operation removeFlight(flight: Flight): void; An airborne message is generated and the flight is removed from the departure sequence (milestone 16).

class TimeQuantity (package Timings): An abstract time value describing a flight's progress, identified by a prefix (Scheduled, Estimated, Target, Actual, Calculated) and an event, with an originator, accuracy and update history.
  attribute value: DateTime; The time value of this time quantity.
  attribute originator: String; The party or system that provided or last updated this value (e.g. AO/GH, ATC, Network Operations, ACARS, radar).
  attribute accuracy: Duration; The stated accuracy or locally agreed tolerance of this time value, where the document specifies one.
  attribute recordedAt: DateTime; The time at which this value was appended to the flight's history; earlier values are kept, not overwritten, so the turn-round can be analysed afterwards.

class Eobt (package Timings): The Estimated Off-Block Time of a flight, used as the reference point for milestone triggers such as EOBT-3hr and EOBT-2hr.

class Sobt (package Timings): The Scheduled Off-Block Time fed to Network Operations before the first E-DPI message.

class Tobt (package Timings): The Target Off-Block Time provided or updated by the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler, checked for consistency against the ATC Flight Plan at every downstream milestone.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; TOBT accuracy at final confirmation is agreed locally (milestone 9).
  attribute updateCount: Integer; The number of times TOBT has been updated since TSAT was issued, checked against a locally defined tolerance (milestone 10).

class Aobt (package Timings): The Actual Off-Block Time recorded when the aircraft pushes back or vacates the parking position.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; AOBT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 15).

class Etot (package Timings): The Estimated Take-Off Time of a flight, updated at flight plan activation and at EOBT-2hr.

class Ttot (package Timings): The Target/Calculated Take-Off Time, computed from EIBT+MTTT+EXOT or TOBT+EXOT and reported to Network Operations when it changes beyond tolerance.
  attribute toleranceThreshold: Duration; The TTOT tolerance (e.g. 5 minutes at take off from outstation) that must be exceeded before Network Operations is informed of an updated TTOT (milestones 3-9,15).
  operation calculateAtEobtMinus2Hr(eibt: Eibt, mttt: Mttt, exot: Exot, eobt: Eobt, tobt: Tobt): DateTime; Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT, if later than EOBT+EXOT. In the case of manual input of TOBT, this estimate will override the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT (milestone 2).
  operation calculateAtTakeOffFromOutstation(eibt: Eibt, mttt: Mttt, exot: Exot, eobt: Eobt, tobt: Tobt): DateTime; Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT. In case EOBT is later than EIBT+MTTT, TTOT equals EOBT+EXOT. In the case where TOBT is available this prediction will overrule the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT (milestone 3).
  operation updateConsideringExot(exot: Exot): DateTime; TTOT updated considering the EXOT (milestone 15).

class Ctot (package Timings): The Calculated Take-Off Time issued by Network Operations to a regulated flight at EOBT-2hr.

class Eldt (package Timings): The Estimated Landing Time of an arriving flight, updated progressively from flight plan activation through final approach.
  operation updateFromFum(fum: FlightUpdateMessage): void; For inbound flights, ELDT is updated based on information provided by the FUM messages, taking into account the actual progress of the flight (milestone 2).
  operation calculateFromAtot(atot: Atot, estimatedElapsedTime: Duration): DateTime; If the departure airport is more than 3hrs flying time from the destination airport, using the ATOT an ELDT can be calculated using the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL (milestone 3).

class Aldt (package Timings): The Actual Landing Time recorded when the aircraft touches down, with an accuracy of plus or minus one minute.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; ALDT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 6).

class Eibt (package Timings): The Estimated In-Block Time of an arriving flight, computed from ELDT or ALDT plus the estimated taxi-in time (EXIT).
  operation calculateFromEldt(eldt: Eldt, exit: Exit): DateTime; An estimated in-block time (EIBT) is computed using the ELDT and the estimated taxi-in time (milestone 4).
  operation updateFromAldt(aldt: Aldt, exit: Exit): DateTime; The EIBT can be updated according to the ALDT+EXIT (milestone 6).

class Aibt (package Timings): The Actual In-Block Time recorded when the aircraft arrives in-blocks, with an accuracy of plus or minus one minute.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; AIBT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 7).

class Atot (package Timings): The Actual Take-Off Time recorded at take-off from an outstation or at the home airport, with an accuracy of plus or minus one minute.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; ATOT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestones 3,16).

class Exot (package Timings): The Estimated Taxi-Out Time calculated by the ATC system from traffic load, gate/stand location, runway in use and holding position, used in TTOT calculation.
  attribute value: Duration; EXOT is a duration, not a point in time, overriding the inherited TimeQuantity value (milestone 9).

class Exit (package Timings): The estimated taxi-in time used together with ELDT or ALDT to compute EIBT.
  attribute value: Duration; EXIT is a duration, not a point in time, overriding the inherited TimeQuantity value (milestones 4,6).

class Mttt (package Timings): The minimum turn-round time used with EIBT to calculate TTOT.
  attribute value: Duration; MTTT is a duration, not a point in time, overriding the inherited TimeQuantity value (milestones 2,3).

class Ettt (package Timings): The estimated turn-round time on which TOBT is calculated after ground handling starts.
  attribute value: Duration; ETTT is a duration, not a point in time, overriding the inherited TimeQuantity value (milestone 8).

class Acgt (package Timings): The Actual Commence of Ground Handling Time, considered equal to AIBT for a normal turn-round.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; ACGT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 8).
  operation deriveFromAibt(aibt: Aibt): DateTime; ACGT is considered to commence at AIBT for a normal turn-round (milestone 7).

class Tsat (package Timings): The Target Start-Up Approval Time issued by ATC, stabilising the flight in the pre-departure sequence.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; TSAT accuracy is agreed locally (milestone 10).

class Asbt (package Timings): The Actual Start Boarding Time, recorded when the gate opens for passengers to physically board.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; ASBT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 11).

class Ardt (package Timings): The Actual Ready Time, recorded when the aircraft is ready to taxi upon reception of TWR instructions.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; ARDT is directly available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 12).

class Asrt (package Timings): The Actual Start-up Request Time, recorded when the pilot requests start-up.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; ASRT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 13).

class Asat (package Timings): The Actual Start-up Approval Time, recorded when the aircraft receives its start-up approval.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; ASAT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 14).

class MilestoneEvent (package Milestones): An abstract representation of one of the sixteen Airport CDM milestones, each with a trigger, timing rule, effect and procedure that updates a flight's status and timings.

class TobtConsistencyCheck (package Milestones): An abstract milestone procedure common to the events following radar detection, landing and ground handling, that checks TOBT consistency with the ATC Flight Plan and notifies Network Operations of TTOT changes beyond tolerance.
  operation checkTobtConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan, to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT (milestones 4,5,6,7,8,9).
  operation checkTtotToleranceAndNotify(flight: Flight): void; Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance; the TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT (milestones 4,5,6,7,8,9).

class FlightPlanActivation (package Milestones): The milestone in which the ATC Flight Plan is submitted and checked for consistency with the Airport Slot and Airport Flight Data before initiating the Airport CDM Platform for the flight.
  attribute earliestTriggerOffset: Duration; The process is first triggered at the earliest EOBT-3 hr (milestone 1).
  operation checkConsistency(flightPlan: FlightPlan, airportSlot: AirportSlot, airportFlightData: AirportFlightData): Boolean; Checks consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent (milestone 1).
  operation confirmFlightToNetworkOperations(flight: Flight): void; Confirms the flight to Network Operations and allows further local processing of the flight (milestone 1).
  operation sendFirstEDpi(flight: Flight): Boolean; The E-DPI message should not be sent if no or inconsistent information is provided; the AO must provide correct information before this message, feeding Network Operations with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration and first destination data as early as possible (milestone 1).
  operation initiateOutboundFlight(flight: Flight): void; For non coordinated airports, the flight plan is used to initiate the outbound flight (milestone 1).
  operation checkFlightReadiness(flight: Flight): Boolean; The flight is ready not later than 15 minutes after the planned EOBT (milestone 1).
  operation updateArrivalTimings(eldt: Eldt, eibt: Eibt): void; ELDT and EIBT updated for an arrival (milestone 1).
  operation updateDepartureTimings(eobt: Eobt, etot: Etot): void; EOBT and ETOT updated for a departure (milestone 1).

class EobtMinus2HourCheck (package Milestones): The milestone at EOBT-2hr in which regulated flights receive a CTOT and estimates are checked against the ATC Flight Plan before informing Network Operations by T-DPI.
  attribute triggerOffset: Duration; This check is triggered by a time stamp at EOBT-2 hr (milestone 2).
  operation checkFlightEstimatesConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan, before or after take off from outstation, to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimated off block time at EOBT-2 hrs (milestone 2).
  operation informNetworkOperationsWithTDpi(flight: Flight): void; At EOBT-2 hrs Network Operations is informed through the first T-DPI message about the updated take off time estimate (milestone 2).
  operation markAsRegulated(flight: Flight): void; ETOT/TTOT/CTOT are updated and appropriate fields are marked as REGULATED (milestone 2).

class TakeOffFromOutstationCheck (package Milestones): The milestone at take-off from an outstation in which estimated landing time consistency is checked and Network Operations informed via T-DPI-t if needed.
  operation checkLandingEstimateConsistency(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH estimated landing time after take off from outstation are consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan, to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan at take off from outstation (milestone 3).
  operation informNetworkOperationsWithTDpiT(flight: Flight): void; When needed, informs Network Operations about the updated take off time estimates using a T-DPI-t Message; a TTOT tolerance of 5 minutes is respected before Network Operations is informed of the updated TTOT (milestone 3).

class LocalRadarUpdate (package Milestones): The milestone in which the flight is detected entering the FIR or local airspace, triggering the TOBT process and update of ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT.

class FinalApproachCheck (package Milestones): The milestone in which the flight enters final approach, triggering the TOBT process and update of ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT.

class LandedCheck (package Milestones): The milestone in which ALDT is recorded, triggering updates to EIBT, TOBT and TTOT based on the turn-round period.

class InBlockCheck (package Milestones): The milestone in which AIBT is recorded, triggering updates to TOBT and TTOT based on the estimated turn-round period.

class GroundHandlingStartedCheck (package Milestones): The milestone in which ground handling commences (ACGT) for first-of-day or long-parked flights, triggering updates to TOBT and TTOT.

class TobtFinalConfirmation (package Milestones): The milestone at which the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler provides the most accurate TOBT before TSAT issue, checked for consistency with the ATC Flight Plan.
  attribute confirmationOffset: Duration; The final TOBT is provided t minutes before EOBT, where t is a locally agreed parameter (milestone 9).
  operation confirmTobtPriorToTsat(flight: Flight): Boolean; The confirmed TOBT prior to TSAT has special status, where AO/GH check the quality of TOBT before TSAT issue; no need to confirm an existing TOBT if it has been manually modified before (milestone 9).

class TsatIssuance (package Milestones): The milestone in which ATC issues TSAT to stabilise the flight in the pre-departure sequence and checks the number of subsequent TOBT updates.
  attribute issuanceOffset: Duration; ATC issues TSAT t-minutes before EOBT, where t is a locally agreed parameter (milestone 10).
  attribute updateCountThreshold: Integer; The locally defined tolerance on the number of TOBT updates allowed after TSAT has been issued before local procedure applies (milestone 10).
  operation issueTsat(flight: Flight): Tsat; ATC issues TSAT t-minutes before EOBT, where t is a parameter agreed locally (milestone 10).
  operation informPartnersOfTsat(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant partners of the TSAT that has been allocated to the flight; the TSAT will indicate to the partners the time when the start up approval can be expected (milestone 10).
  operation sendTDpiS(flight: Flight): DpiMessage; Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights; no check is performed for this step (milestone 10).
  operation checkTobtUpdateCount(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks whether the number of TOBT updates exceeds a tolerance defined locally, after TSAT has been issued; in case the number exceeds a threshold, the TOBT input should be processed according to local procedure (milestone 10).

class BoardingStartCheck (package Milestones): The milestone in which boarding starts (ASBT) and boarding status is checked against TOBT.
  attribute triggerOffset: Duration; The boarding status check is triggered a locally defined number of minutes before TOBT, e.g. corresponding to aircraft type (milestone 11).
  operation informPartnersOfAsbt(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) when it occurs (milestone 11).
  operation checkBoardingStatus(flight: Flight): Boolean; At a certain time before TOBT (local variable e.g. corresponding to aircraft type) a check shall be performed to check the boarding status and inform the AO/GH in case TOBT needs to be updated (milestone 11).

class AircraftReadyCheck (package Milestones): The milestone in which the aircraft becomes ready for start-up (ARDT), refining the pre-departure sequence and alerting if TOBT has passed without a ready status.
  attribute toleranceOffset: Duration; At TOBT plus a locally agreed tolerance, the AO/GH are informed that no ready status has been received (milestone 12).
  operation informPartnersOfArdt(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Ready Time (ARDT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft is ready for start up/push-back (milestone 12).
  operation checkReadyStatusReceived(flight: Flight): Boolean; At TOBT + tolerance the AO/GH are informed that TOBT has passed and there has not been a ready status message yet (milestone 12).

class StartUpRequestCheck (package Milestones): The milestone in which start-up is requested (ASRT), with an alert if the request is not made within the TSAT tolerance window.
  attribute toleranceWindow: Duration; The locally agreed TSAT tolerance window within which start-up must be requested before an alert is raised (milestone 13).
  operation informPartnersOfAsrt(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start up Request Time (ASRT) in the Airport CDM Platform when it occurs (milestone 13).
  operation checkStartUpRequestedInTime(flight: Flight): Boolean; If the start up request is not made by TSAT + tolerance, the AO/GH is informed that no start up has been requested, and should update TOBT (milestone 13).

class StartUpApprovalCheck (package Milestones): The milestone in which ATC grants start-up approval (ASAT), checked against TSAT and re-sequencing the flight if approval is not granted in time.
  attribute toleranceOffset: Duration; If start-up approval is not granted by TSAT plus a locally agreed tolerance, all relevant partners are informed and the flight is re-sequenced (milestone 14).
  operation informPartnersOfAsat(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual start up approval Time (ASAT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has received start up approval/push-back clearance, when it occurs (milestone 14).
  operation checkAsatAgainstTsat(flight: Flight): Boolean; Checks if ASAT is in accordance to TSAT and alerts all relevant Airport CDM Partners when no start up has been granted; in case the start up approval is not granted at TSAT + tolerance, all relevant partners should be informed (milestone 14).

class OffBlockCheck (package Milestones): The milestone in which AOBT is recorded, triggering an A-DPI message and TTOT update checked against tolerance.
  operation informPartnersOfAobt(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Off-Block Time (AOBT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has commenced push-back/taxi from parking position, when it occurs (milestone 15).
  operation sendADpi(flight: Flight): DpiMessage; AOBT always triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations or, in the case of remote holding, at a defined time prior to TTOT (milestone 15).
  operation checkTtotToleranceAndNotify(flight: Flight): void; After a first A-DPI is sent, checks TTOT updates against the TTOT tolerance before Network Operations is informed, with a new A-DPI, of the updated TTOT (milestone 15).

class TakeOffNotification (package Milestones): The milestone in which ATOT is recorded, generating FSA and MVT messages and removing the flight from the departure sequence.
  operation informPartnersOfTakeOff(flight: Flight): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners about the actual take off (milestone 16).
  operation generateAirborneMessage(flight: Flight): AirborneMessage; An airborne message is generated (milestone 16).
  operation sendFsaAndMvt(flight: Flight): void; FSA and MVT messages are sent (milestone 16).

class AirportCDMPlatform (package Systems): The central Airport CDM system that processes flight information, performs the consistency checks and calculations of each milestone, and triggers CDM Operation actions.
  operation initiateForFlight(flight: Flight): void; The Airport CDM Platform is initiated for this flight, and all available information is processed (milestone 1).
  operation commenceDpiProcess(flight: Flight): void; The DPI process commences the correct messaging with Network Operations (if implemented) (milestone 1).
  operation calculateTobtFromEldt(eldt: Eldt): Tobt; Update of the ELDT can trigger a new TOBT to be entered by the AO/GH, or calculated automatically by the Airport CDM Platform (milestone 4).

class AtcSystem (package Systems): The air traffic control technical system (including tower FDPS and A-SMGCS) that detects flight events, calculates EXOT and provides actual times such as ALDT, AIBT and ATOT.
  operation provideActualLandingTime(flight: Flight): Aldt; ALDT is provided by ATC system or by ACARS from equipped aircraft (milestone 6).
  operation provideActualInBlockTime(flight: Flight): Aibt; AIBT is provided by ACARS equipped aircraft or automated docking systems or ATC systems (e.g. A-SMGCS) or by manual input (milestone 7).
  operation calculateExot(trafficLoad: String, gateStandLocation: String, runwayInUse: String, holdingPositionWait: Duration): Exot; Following the receipt of the TOBT, the ATC system will calculate and provide the Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) based on the predicted traffic load, gate/stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the Holding Position (milestone 9).
  operation provideActualOffBlockTime(flight: Flight): Aobt; AOBT is provided by ACARS equipped aircraft or automated docking systems or ATC systems (e.g. A-SMGCS) or by manual input (milestone 15).
  operation provideActualTakeOffTime(flight: Flight): Atot; ATOT is provided by ATC system or from ACARS equipped aircraft (milestone 16).

class RadarSystem (package Systems): The radar system that detects a flight by its assigned SSR code when crossing FIR, TMA or final approach boundaries.
  operation detectFlightBySsr(ssrCode: String): void; The radar system is able to detect a flight based upon the assigned SSR code when the flight crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary (milestone 4).
  operation detectFinalApproach(flight: Flight): void; The radar system detects a flight based upon the assigned SSR code and identifies when the flight crosses either a defined range/position or passes/leaves a predetermined level (milestone 5).
