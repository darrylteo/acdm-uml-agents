# The document: sixteen A-CDM milestones, sentences numbered

## Milestone 1: ATC Flight Plan Activated

### Definition

[M1.definition.1] The ICAO flight plan is submitted to ATC.
[M1.definition.2] The Airport CDM Platform is initiated for this flight, and all available information is processed.
### Origin and priority

[M1.origin_and_priority.1] The ATC Flight Plan is submitted by the Aircraft Operator and distributed by the IFPS.
[M1.origin_and_priority.2] All involved ATC units receive the flight plan, including departure and destination aerodromes.
### Timing

[M1.timing.1] Normally this takes place 3 hours before EOBT, however it may be later.
[M1.timing.2] In some cases a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) has been submitted, covering daily or weekly flights.
### Data Quality

[M1.data_quality.1] The ATC Flight Plan corresponds to the airport slot programme.
### Effect

[M1.effect.1] One aircraft turn-round normally includes an arriving and a departing flight, meaning that it will have two related flight plans.
[M1.effect.2] For coordinated airports, the outbound flight is already known.
[M1.effect.3] The flight plan may be used to update certain information such as type of aircraft.
[M1.effect.4] For long distance flights, the ELDT may differ from the airport slot.
[M1.effect.5] For non coordinated airports, the flight plan is used to initiate the outbound flight.
[M1.effect.6] The flight is ready not later than 15 minutes after the planned EOBT.
[M1.effect.7] The DPI process commences the correct messaging with Network Operations (if implemented - see attachment 2 for details).
### Procedures

[M1.procedures.1] To check consistency between ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data and then confirm the flight to the Network Operations and allow further local processing of the flight.
[M1.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify the consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent.
[M1.procedures.3] The AO must provide correct information before this first E-DPI message, in order to feed Network Operations with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration, and first destination data, as early in time as possible.
[M1.procedures.4] The E-DPI message should not be sent if no or inconsistent information is provided.
[M1.procedures.5] This process is triggered by:
[M1.procedures.6] - The first activation of the ATC Flight Plan (earliest EOBT-3 hr), or
[M1.procedures.7] - New or late submissions of the ATC Flight Plan, after cancellation or revised EOBT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M1.operational_status.1] INITIATED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M1.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT and EIBT updated for an arrival EOBT and ETOT updated for a departure The DPI process commences (if implemented - see section 3.7.3 for details).
## Milestone 2: EOBT - 2 hr

### Definition

[M2.definition.1] At EOBT-2 hr most flights will be known in the Airport CDM Platform including if they are regulated or not.
[M2.definition.2] All regulated flights receive a CTOT from Network Operations.
### Origin and priority

[M2.origin_and_priority.1] The CTOT is issued by the Network Operations and is sent to relevant ATS units as well as the departure aerodrome.
[M2.origin_and_priority.2] CTOT flights usually have a priority over unregulated flights.
### Timing

[M2.timing.1] If the flight is regulated, a CTOT is issued at EOBT-2h.
### Data Quality

[M2.data_quality.1] Not applicable.
### Effect

[M2.effect.1] For inbound flights, ELDT is updated based on information provided by the FUM messages, taking into account the actual progress of the flight.
### Procedures

[M2.procedures.1] To check (before or after take off from outstation) whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan and to inform Network Operations about the updated take off time estimate, using a T-DPI Message.
[M2.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimated off block time at EOBT-2 hrs.
[M2.procedures.3] At EOBT-2 hrs Network Operations is informed through the first T-DPI message.
[M2.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT, if later than EOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.5] In the case of manual input of TOBT, this estimate will override the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.6] This procedure is triggered by
[M2.procedures.7] - a time stamp, at EOBT - 2h.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M2.operational_status.1] N. A.
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M2.action_on_cdm.1] ETOT/TTOT/CTOT Mark appropriate fields as REGULATED
## Milestone 3: Take Off from Outstation

### Definition

[M3.definition.1] The ATOT from the outstation (ADEP)
### Origin and priority

[M3.origin_and_priority.1] The outstation provides ATOT to the Network Operations and Aircraft Operator.
### Timing

[M3.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M3.data_quality.1] The accuracy of ATOT is +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M3.effect.1] If the departure airport is more than 3hrs flying time from the destination airport the ATOT is received from either the Network Operations FUM or via the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler.
[M3.effect.2] Using the ATOT an ELDT can be calculated by using the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL.
[M3.effect.3] If the flight is within 3hrs flying time of the destination airport the Network Operations monitors progress of the flight using the ETFMS and send a Flight Update Message (FUM) that provides updates of the flight's progress.
### Procedures

[M3.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH estimated landing time after take off from outstation are consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan, and when needed inform the Network Operations about the updated take off time estimates using a T-DPI-t Message.
[M3.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan at take off from outstation.
[M3.procedures.3] A TTOT tolerance of 5 minutes is respected before Network Operations is informed of the updated TTOT.
[M3.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.5] In case EOBT is later than EIBT+MTTT, TTOT equals EOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.6] In the case where TOBT is available this prediction will overrule the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.7] This process is triggered by
[M3.procedures.8] - the take off from outstation.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M3.operational_status.1] AIRBORNE
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M3.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 4: Local Radar Update

### Definition

[M4.definition.1] The flight enters the FIR (Flight Information Region) or the local airspace of the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M4.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from the Area Control Centre (ACC) or Approach Control Unit that is associated with an airport.
[M4.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system is able to detect a flight based upon the assigned SSR code when the flight crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary.
### Timing

[M4.timing.1] Dependent upon the position of the airport in relation to the FIR boundary.
### Data Quality

[M4.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M4.effect.1] Update of the ELDT can trigger a new TOBT to be entered by the AO/GH, or calculated automatically by the Airport CDM Platform.
[M4.effect.2] The accuracy of ELDT is particularly important at this stage since downstream decisions are taken, such as stand /gate / aircraft changes, preparation of arrival sequence, preparation of ground handling operations, decisions for connecting passengers.
[M4.effect.3] Uncertainty and ELDT non-accuracy at this stage significantly increase risks for bad and last minute decisions and internal disruptions.
[M4.effect.4] The objective to decrease the number of stand and gate changes in the last 30 minutes requires high accuracy regarding departure and arrival times.
[M4.effect.5] Therefore, taking into account the taxi-in time (EXIT), any change to a stand or gate is not preferred after ELDT-30'.
[M4.effect.6] The update of TOBT for the related departing flight takes place following this milestone.
[M4.effect.7] Decisions such as the turn-round period, connecting passengers etc are taken and need to be stable at this event.
[M4.effect.8] An estimated in-block time (EIBT) is computed using the ELDT and the estimated taxi-in time.
### Procedures

[M4.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M4.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M4.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M4.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M4.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M4.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M4.operational_status.1] FIR
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M4.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 5: Final Approach

### Definition

[M5.definition.1] The flight enters the Final Approach phase at the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M5.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from ATC.
[M5.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system detects a flight based upon the assigned SSR code and identifies when the flight crosses either a defined range / position or passes/leaves a predetermined level.
### Timing

[M5.timing.1] Dependent upon local parameters that are defined by ATC.
### Data Quality

[M5.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M5.effect.1] Update of the ELDT to determine a new TOBT.
[M5.effect.2] When a flight reaches this stage it is usually between 2 and 5 minutes from landing (depending on the parameter set by ATC).
[M5.effect.3] This is often the prompt for many partners to start moving resources connected with the flight, such as positioning a parking marshal and ground handling services.
### Procedures

[M5.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M5.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M5.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M5.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M5.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M5.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M5.operational_status.1] FINAL
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M5.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 6: Landed

### Definition

[M6.definition.1] ALDT - Actual Landing Time.
[M6.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft touches down on a runway.
[M6.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATA - Actual Time of Arrival landing, ACARS=ON).
### Origin and priority

[M6.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or by ACARS from equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M6.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M6.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M6.effect.1] The occurrence of ALDT triggers an update of downstream estimates: TOBT and TTOT are updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the defined turn-round period for the departing flight.
[M6.effect.2] The EIBT can be updated according to the ALDT +EXIT.
### Procedures

[M6.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M6.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M6.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M6.procedures.6] - Actual Landing Time: ALDT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M6.operational_status.1] LANDED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M6.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT changes to ALDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated

## Milestone 9: Final Confirmation of the TOBT

### Definition

[M9.definition.1] The time at which the Aircraft Operator or Ground Handler provide their most accurate TOBT taking into account the operational situation.
### Origin and priority

[M9.origin_and_priority.1] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler provides the information.
### Timing

[M9.timing.1] The information is provided t minutes before EOBT (t is a parameter time agreed locally).
### Data Quality

[M9.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M9.effect.1] The aim of the final TOBT is to give a timely, accurate and reliable assessment of the off-block time.
[M9.effect.2] It is recognised that main benefits of sharing the TOBT are expected in case of disruptions (internal or external).
[M9.effect.3] In such cases, the difference between EOBT (shared by ATC, Network Operations and Stand / Gate Management) and TOBT may be important.
[M9.effect.4] An accurate TOBT at [EOBT-t minutes] is a pre-requisite for ATC to establish a push back / pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.5] Emphasis is put on the need for the Aircraft Operator to integrate his own strategy to compute a TOBT related to the flight.
[M9.effect.6] Following the receipt of the TOBT, the ATC system will calculate and provide the Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) based on the predicted traffic load, gate / stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the Holding Position, etc. The flight is introduced into the pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.7] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M9.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M9.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M9.procedures.3] This check should be performed at a predefined time (local parameter) to confirm TOBT prior to TSAT issue and verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimates given the updated TOBT.
[M9.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M9.procedures.5] This Milestone Process is actually constantly applicable in the CDM Platform, as soon as a TOBT is available.
[M9.procedures.6] However the confirmed TOBT prior to TSAT has special status, where AO/GH check the quality of TOBT before TSAT issue.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M9.operational_status.1] IN-BLOCK
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M9.action_on_cdm.1] This process is triggered by - a new TOBT or TTOT update.
[M9.action_on_cdm.2] No need to confirm an existing TOBT if it has been manually modified before.

### Definition

[M10.definition.1] The time ATC issues the Target Start Up Approval Time
### Origin and priority

[M10.origin_and_priority.1] ATC
### Timing

[M10.timing.1] The information is provided t-minutes before EOBT, where t is a parameter agreed locally
### Data Quality

[M10.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M10.effect.1] The flight is stabilised into the pre-departure sequence.
[M10.effect.2] The Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M10.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant partners of the TSAT that has been allocated to the flight.
[M10.procedures.2] The Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.3] Second step: To check whether the number of TOBT updates exceeds a tolerance defined locally, after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.4] First: The TSAT will indicate to the partners the time when the start up approval can be expected.
[M10.procedures.5] Network Operations will be informed with a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.6] No check is performed.
[M10.procedures.7] Second: A check shall be performed to see the number of TOBT updates after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.8] In case the number of TOBT updates exceeds a threshold, then the TOBT input should be processed according to local procedure.
[M10.procedures.9] This process is triggered by
[M10.procedures.10] - A defined time (local parameter) before TOBT
[M10.procedures.11] - TOBT update after TSAT issue

[M11.definition.2] This is not to be confused with the time passengers are pre-called to the gate via flight information display systems (FIDS) or public address systems.
### Origin and priority

[M11.origin_and_priority.1] Automatic from airport system or manual input by Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M11.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M11.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M11.effect.1] When boarding commences it gives the Airport CDM Partners a good indication of whether the TOBT/TSAT will be respected.
### Procedures

[M11.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT).
[M11.procedures.2] Second step: To check whether boarding starts in time to respect TOBT and inform the AO/GH in case TOBT needs to be updated.
[M11.procedures.3] Inform of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) when it occurs.
[M11.procedures.4] At a certain time before TOBT (local variable e.g. corresponding to aircraft type) a check shall be performed to check the boarding status.
[M11.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M11.procedures.6] - a time variable <value> minutes before TOBT.
### Operational Status (changes to)

## Milestone 12: Aircraft Ready

### Definition

[M12.definition.1] The time when all doors are closed, boarding bridge removed, push back vehicle connected, ready to taxi immediately upon reception of TWR instructions (ARDT).
### Origin and priority

[M12.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by the Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M12.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M12.data_quality.1] Data is directly available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M12.effect.1] ATC refines the pre-departure sequence.
[M12.effect.2] The pilot requests start up just before TSAT, following coordination with the Ground Handler.
[M12.effect.3] (Dispatcher / Supervisor / Redcap).
### Procedures

[M12.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Ready Time (ARDT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft is ready for start up / push-back.
[M12.procedures.2] Second step: To inform the AO/GH that TOBT has passed and the Airport CDM Platform has not yet received ARDT or Ready Status (RDY).
[M12.procedures.3] Inform of ARDT or RDY confirming that the flight follows the indicated TOBT.
[M12.procedures.4] At TOBT + tolerance the AO/GH are informed that TOBT has passed and there has not been a ready status message yet.
[M12.procedures.5] This procedure is triggered by
[M12.procedures.6] - an input to the Airport CDM Platform.

## Milestone 15: Off-Block

### Definition

[M15.definition.1] AOBT - Actual Off-Block Time.
[M15.definition.2] The time the aircraft pushes back/vacates the parking position (Equivalent to Airline/Handler ATD - Actual Time of Departure ACARS=OUT).
### Origin and priority

[M15.origin_and_priority.1] ACARS equipped aircraft or automated docking systems or ATC systems (e.g. A-SMGCS) or by manual input.
### Timing

[M15.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M15.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M15.effect.1] TTOT updated considering the EXOT.
### Procedures

[M15.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Off-Block Time (AOBT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has commenced push-back / taxi from parking position.
[M15.procedures.2] Second step: To check if TTOT changes by more than the agreed tolerance and inform Network Operations.
[M15.procedures.3] Inform of AOBT when it occurs.
[M15.procedures.4] AOBT always triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations or in the case of remote holding at a defined time prior to TTOT.
[M15.procedures.5] After a first A-DPI is sent this check shall be performed to check TTOT updates against the TTOT tolerance before Network Operations is informed, with a new A-DPI, of the updated TTOT.
[M15.procedures.6] This process is triggered by AOBT detection.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M15.operational_status.1] OFF-BLOCK
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M15.action_on_cdm.1] AOBT recorded
## Milestone 16: Take Off

### Definition

[M16.definition.1] ATOT - Actual Take Off Time.
[M16.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft takes off from the runway.
[M16.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATD-Actual Time of Departure, ACARS = OFF).
### Origin and priority

[M16.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or from ACARS equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M16.timing.1] The information is directly available as soon as possible after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M16.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M16.effect.1] FSA and MVT messages are sent.
### Procedures

[M16.procedures.1] To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners about the actual take off.
[M16.procedures.2] An airborne message is generated and the flight is removed from the departure sequence.
[M16.procedures.3] This process is triggered by Tower FDPS, A-SMGCS / Radar detection or ACARS.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M16.operational_status.1] DEPARTED / TAKE OFF
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M16.action_on_cdm.1] ATOT recorded

# The model

class AircraftOperator (package Stakeholders): The airline or operator responsible for a flight, submitting the ATC flight plan and providing TOBT and related estimates.
  operation provideFlightPlanData(sobt: DateTime, aircraftRegistration: String, firstDestination: String): void; The AO must provide correct information before the first E-DPI message, in order to feed Network Operations with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration, and first destination data, as early in time as possible.

class GroundHandler (package Stakeholders): The party performing turn-round and ground handling operations on behalf of, or alongside, the Aircraft Operator.

class AirTrafficControlUnit (package Stakeholders): Any ATC unit (Area Control Centre, Approach Control, Tower, or ATS unit) that receives the flight plan, detects the flight, or issues approvals.

class NetworkOperations (package Stakeholders): The network management function that issues CTOT, receives DPI messages, and monitors flight progress via ETFMS/FUM.
  operation issueCtot(flight: Flight, eobt: DateTime): DateTime; The CTOT is issued by the Network Operations and sent to relevant ATS units as well as the departure aerodrome; if the flight is regulated, a CTOT is issued at EOBT-2h.

class Ifps (package Stakeholders): The Integrated Initial Flight Plan Processing System that distributes the submitted ATC flight plan to all involved ATC units.
  operation distributeFlightPlan(flightPlan: FlightPlan): void; The ATC Flight Plan is submitted by the Aircraft Operator and distributed by the IFPS; all involved ATC units receive the flight plan, including departure and destination aerodromes.

class FlightCrew (package Stakeholders): The pilot and aircrew who request start-up, coordinate boarding and turn-round management, and receive TSAT confirmation.

class StandGateManagement (package Stakeholders): The stakeholder responsible for stand and gate allocation, sharing EOBT and reacting to stand/gate change decisions.

class Flight (package Flights): A single scheduled arrival or departure operation of an aircraft, carrying an operational status that changes at each milestone.
  attribute operationalStatus: FlightOperationalStatus; The flight's current operational status, changed at each milestone from INITIATED through DEPARTED/TAKE OFF.
  attribute regulated: Boolean; Whether the flight is regulated and has received a CTOT; appropriate fields are marked as REGULATED at EOBT-2hr (milestone 2), and regulated status affects whether a T-DPI-s is sent (milestone 10) and whether ATC must assure the flight respects its CTOT (milestone 13).

class TurnRound (package Flights): The aircraft turn-round linking an arriving flight and its related departing flight, including the defined turn-round period.
  attribute turnRoundPeriod: Duration; The defined or estimated turn-round period for the departing flight, used to calculate TOBT after ALDT, AIBT, or ACGT (milestones 6, 7, 8).

class Aircraft (package Flights): The physical aircraft operating a flight, identified by type and registration, which may be updated from the flight plan.
  attribute registration: String; The aircraft registration, provided by the AO before the first E-DPI message to feed Network Operations with consistent data.
  attribute type: String; The type of aircraft, which the ATC Flight Plan may be used to update.

class FlightPlan (package Flights): The ICAO ATC flight plan submitted for a flight, possibly as part of a repetitive flight plan (RFPL), describing departure and destination aerodromes.
  attribute isRepetitive: Boolean; Whether the flight plan is a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) submitted to cover daily or weekly flights.
  attribute estimatedElapsedTime: Duration; The Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL, used with ATOT to calculate ELDT when the departure airport is more than 3 hours flying time from the destination (milestone 3).

class AirportSlot (package Flights): The airport slot allocated to a flight at a coordinated airport, against which the ATC flight plan and airport flight data are checked for consistency.

class Aerodrome (package Flights): An airport, whether the departure, destination, or outstation aerodrome, involved in a flight's origin or destination.

class OffBlockTime (package Timings): A time quantity for the off-block event (SOBT, EOBT, TOBT, or AOBT), recorded with its prefix, origin, and time of update; every successive value is kept.
  attribute prefix: TimePrefix; Whether this off-block time is the scheduled (SOBT), estimated (EOBT), target (TOBT), or actual (AOBT) value.
  attribute value: DateTime; The date and time of the off-block event for this prefix.
  attribute originator: String; The party or system that provided this value, such as the AO/GH, ATC, or an automated docking/ACARS system.
  attribute timeOfUpdate: DateTime; The time at which this value was recorded, so successive values can be kept and analysed afterwards.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; The accuracy of the value; AOBT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 15).

class LandingTime (package Timings): A time quantity for the landing event (ELDT or ALDT), recorded with its prefix, origin, and time of update.
  attribute prefix: TimePrefix; Whether this landing time is estimated (ELDT) or actual (ALDT).
  attribute value: DateTime; The date and time of the landing event for this prefix.
  attribute originator: String; The party or system that provided this value, such as ATC, ACARS, or Network Operations FUM.
  attribute timeOfUpdate: DateTime; The time at which this value was recorded, appended rather than overwriting the prior value.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; The accuracy of the value; ALDT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 6).
  operation updateFromFum(fum: FlightUpdateMessage): void; For inbound flights, ELDT is updated based on information provided by the FUM messages, taking into account the actual progress of the flight.
  operation calculateFromAtot(atot: DateTime, estimatedElapsedTime: Duration): DateTime; If the departure airport is more than 3hrs flying time from the destination airport, an ELDT can be calculated by using the ATOT and the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL.

class InBlockTime (package Timings): A time quantity for the in-block event (EIBT or AIBT), recorded with its prefix, origin, and time of update.
  attribute prefix: TimePrefix; Whether this in-block time is estimated (EIBT) or actual (AIBT).
  attribute value: DateTime; The date and time of the in-block event for this prefix.
  attribute originator: String; The party or system that provided this value, such as ACARS, an automated docking system, ATC systems, or manual input.
  attribute timeOfUpdate: DateTime; The time at which this value was recorded, appended rather than overwriting the prior value.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; The accuracy of the value; AIBT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 7).
  operation calculateFromEldt(eldt: DateTime, exit: Duration): DateTime; An estimated in-block time (EIBT) is computed using the ELDT and the estimated taxi-in time.
  operation calculateFromAldt(aldt: DateTime, exit: Duration): DateTime; The EIBT can be updated according to the ALDT + EXIT.

class TakeOffTime (package Timings): A time quantity for the take-off event (ETOT, TTOT, CTOT, or ATOT), recorded with its prefix, origin, and time of update.
  attribute prefix: TimePrefix; Whether this take-off time is estimated (ETOT), target (TTOT), calculated (CTOT), or actual (ATOT).
  attribute value: DateTime; The date and time of the take-off event for this prefix.
  attribute originator: String; The party or system that provided this value, such as ATC, ACARS, or Network Operations.
  attribute timeOfUpdate: DateTime; The time at which this value was recorded, appended rather than overwriting the prior value.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; The accuracy of the value; ATOT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestones 3, 16).
  attribute tolerance: Duration; The TTOT tolerance respected before Network Operations is informed of an updated TTOT; a tolerance of 5 minutes is used after take-off from outstation (milestone 3).

class StartUpApprovalTime (package Timings): A time quantity for the start-up approval event (TSAT or ASAT), recorded with its prefix, origin, and time of update.
  attribute prefix: TimePrefix; Whether this value is the target (TSAT) or actual (ASAT) start-up approval time.
  attribute value: DateTime; The date and time of the start-up approval event for this prefix.
  attribute originator: String; The party that provided this value, namely ATC.
  attribute timeOfUpdate: DateTime; The time at which this value was recorded, appended rather than overwriting the prior value.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; The accuracy of the value; ASAT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 14).
  attribute tolerance: Duration; The locally agreed TSAT tolerance window within which start-up must be requested and approved (milestones 9-14).

class StartUpRequestTime (package Timings): The actual time at which start-up is requested (ASRT), recorded with its origin and time of update.
  attribute value: DateTime; The date and time at which start-up is requested (ASRT).
  attribute originator: String; The party that provided this value, namely ATC based on the pilot's request.
  attribute timeOfUpdate: DateTime; The time at which this value was recorded.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; The accuracy of the value; ASRT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 13).
  attribute toleranceWindow: Duration; The locally agreed TSAT tolerance window inside which the start-up request must occur, after which AO/GH are alerted (milestone 13).

class ReadyTime (package Timings): The actual time at which the aircraft is ready for start-up/push-back (ARDT), recorded with its origin and time of update.
  attribute value: DateTime; The date and time at which the aircraft is ready for start-up/push-back (ARDT).
  attribute originator: String; The party that provided this value, namely the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler.
  attribute timeOfUpdate: DateTime; The time at which this value was recorded.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; The accuracy of the value; ARDT is directly available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 12).
  attribute tolerance: Duration; The tolerance after TOBT at which AO/GH are informed that no ARDT or ready status has been received (milestone 12).

class BoardingTime (package Timings): The actual start boarding time (ASBT) at which the gate opens for passengers to board.
  attribute value: DateTime; The date and time at which boarding starts (ASBT).
  attribute originator: String; The party or system that provided this value, namely the automatic airport system or manual input by AO/GH.
  attribute timeOfUpdate: DateTime; The time at which this value was recorded.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; The accuracy of the value; ASBT is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 11).

class GroundHandlingTime (package Timings): The actual commence of ground handling time (ACGT), applicable to first-of-day or long-parked flights.
  attribute value: DateTime; The date and time at which ground handling operations commence (ACGT).
  attribute originator: String; The party that provided this value, namely the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler.
  attribute timeOfUpdate: DateTime; The time at which this value was recorded.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; The accuracy of the value; ACGT data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 8).

class TaxiTime (package Timings): An estimated taxi duration, either taxi-out (EXOT) or taxi-in (EXIT), used in the calculation of other time quantities.
  attribute type: String; Whether this is the estimated taxi-out time (EXOT) or estimated taxi-in time (EXIT).
  attribute duration: Duration; The estimated duration of the taxi phase, used in the calculation of TTOT or EIBT.
  attribute calculationBasis: String; For EXOT, the basis of calculation: predicted traffic load, gate/stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the holding position (milestone 9).

class MinimumTurnRoundTime (package Timings): The minimum turn-round time (MTTT) defined for an aircraft type, used as a calculation basis for TTOT.
  attribute duration: Duration; The minimum turn-round time (MTTT) defined for an aircraft type, used as a calculation basis for TTOT (milestones 2, 3).

class FlightPlanActivationMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 1: checks consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, airport slot, and airport flight data, and confirms the flight to Network Operations before the first E-DPI.
  attribute triggerOffset: Duration; The nominal timing of this milestone, normally 3 hours before EOBT, though it may occur later (milestone 1).
  operation checkConsistency(atcFlightPlan: FlightPlan, airportSlot: AirportSlot, aircraft: Aircraft): Boolean; Checks consistency between ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data; shall be performed to verify this consistency before the first E-DPI is sent.
  operation confirmFlightToNetworkOperations(flight: Flight): void; Confirms the flight to the Network Operations and allows further local processing of the flight.
  operation triggerOnActivationOrResubmission(eobt: DateTime, isNewOrLateSubmission: Boolean): void; Process is triggered by the first activation of the ATC Flight Plan (earliest EOBT-3 hr), or new or late submissions of the ATC Flight Plan, after cancellation or revised EOBT.
  operation updateArrivalOrDepartureTimings(flight: Flight): void; ELDT and EIBT updated for an arrival; EOBT and ETOT updated for a departure.

class TtotCalculationMilestone (package Milestones): The common TTOT calculation rule shared by the EOBT-2hr check and the take-off-from-outstation check: TTOT is based on EIBT+MTTT+EXOT (or EOBT+EXOT if later), overridden by TOBT+EXOT once TOBT is available.
  operation calculateTtot(eibt: DateTime, mttt: Duration, exot: Duration, eobt: DateTime, tobt: DateTime): DateTime; Calculation basis for the TTOT takes into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT, compared against EOBT+EXOT (the later of the two applies); in the case of manual input of TOBT, or once TOBT is available, this prediction overrides the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT (milestones 2 and 3).

class RegulationCheckMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 2 (EOBT-2hr): checks AO/GH flight estimates against the ATC Flight Plan, determines CTOT for regulated flights, and informs Network Operations via a T-DPI message.
  attribute triggerOffset: Duration; The fixed timing of this milestone, occurring at EOBT-2 hours (milestone 2).
  operation checkFlightEstimatesConsistency(estimates: String, atcFlightPlan: FlightPlan): Boolean; Checks (before or after take off from outstation) whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan; shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimated off block time at EOBT-2 hrs.
  operation informNetworkOperationsViaTDpi(flight: Flight): void; At EOBT-2 hrs Network Operations is informed through the first T-DPI message about the updated take off time estimate.
  operation markFieldsAsRegulated(flight: Flight): void; Marks appropriate fields as REGULATED for a flight that has received a CTOT, setting the flight's regulated attribute.

class TakeOffFromOutstationMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 3: checks estimated landing times after take-off from the outstation against the outbound ATC Flight Plan and informs Network Operations via a T-DPI-t message.
  operation checkLandingEstimatesConsistency(estimatedLandingTime: DateTime, outboundFlightPlan: FlightPlan): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH estimated landing time after take off from outstation is consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan; shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan at take off from outstation.
  operation informNetworkOperationsViaTDpiT(flight: Flight): void; When needed, informs the Network Operations about the updated take off time estimates using a T-DPI-t Message; a TTOT tolerance of 5 minutes is respected before Network Operations is informed of the updated TTOT.

class TobtCheckMilestone (package Milestones): The common TOBT-feasibility check and TTOT-tolerance notification performed at each milestone from Local Radar Update through Final Confirmation of the TOBT: verify that TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan and inform Network Operations when TTOT changes by more than the agreed tolerance.
  operation checkTobtConsistency(tobt: DateTime, atcFlightPlan: FlightPlan): Boolean; Checks whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan; shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT (milestones 4-9).
  operation informNetworkOperationsOfTtotChange(newTtot: DateTime, tolerance: Duration): void; Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance (milestones 4-9).

class LocalRadarUpdateMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 4: commences the TOBT process on radar detection of the flight in the FIR, checking TOBT feasibility and TTOT tolerance, and constrains stand/gate changes near ELDT.
  operation commenceTobtProcess(flight: Flight): void; Commences the TOBT process; triggered by the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
  operation checkStandGateChangeAllowed(eldt: DateTime, exit: Duration): Boolean; Taking into account the taxi-in time (EXIT), any change to a stand or gate is not preferred after ELDT-30'.

class FinalApproachMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 5: commences the TOBT process on radar detection of the flight on final approach.
  operation commenceTobtProcess(flight: Flight): void; Commences the TOBT process; triggered by the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.

class LandedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 6: on ALDT, updates downstream time estimates for the departing flight based on the turn-round period.
  operation triggerDownstreamUpdatesFromAldt(aldt: DateTime, turnRoundPeriod: Duration): void; The occurrence of ALDT triggers an update of downstream estimates: TOBT and TTOT are updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the defined turn-round period for the departing flight.

class InBlockMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 7: on AIBT, updates downstream time estimates for the departing flight based on the turn-round period.
  operation triggerDownstreamUpdatesFromAibt(aibt: DateTime, turnRoundPeriod: Duration): void; The occurrence of AIBT should trigger an update of downstream estimates: TOBT and TTOT are updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the estimated turn-round period for the departing flight.

class GroundHandlingStartedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 8: on ACGT, for first-of-day or long-parked flights, updates the TOBT for the departing flight based on the turn-round period.
  operation triggerTobtUpdateFromAcgt(acgt: DateTime, turnRoundPeriod: Duration): void; The occurrence of ACGT triggers an update of downstream estimates: TOBT is updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the estimated turn-round period for the departing flight.

class FinalTobtConfirmationMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 9: checks that the AO/GH's most accurate TOBT, provided t minutes before EOBT, is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan prior to TSAT issue.
  attribute localParameterT: Duration; The locally agreed parameter t, the number of minutes before EOBT at which the AO/GH must provide the final confirmed TOBT (milestone 9).
  operation confirmTobt(tobt: DateTime, wasManuallyModified: Boolean): Boolean; Triggered by a new TOBT or TTOT update; no need to confirm an existing TOBT if it has been manually modified before.

class TsatIssuedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 10: informs partners of the TSAT allocated to the flight and checks whether TOBT updates after TSAT issue exceed a locally defined tolerance.
  attribute localParameterT: Duration; The locally agreed parameter t, the number of minutes before EOBT/TOBT at which TSAT is issued (milestone 10).
  operation informPartnersOfTsat(tsat: DateTime): void; Informs all relevant partners of the TSAT that has been allocated to the flight.
  operation informNetworkOperationsViaTDpiS(flight: Flight): void; The Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
  operation checkTobtUpdateCountAfterTsat(updateCount: Integer, threshold: Integer): Boolean; A check shall be performed to see the number of TOBT updates after TSAT has been issued. In case the number of TOBT updates exceeds a threshold, then the TOBT input should be processed according to local procedure.

class BoardingStartsMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 11: informs partners of the Actual Start Boarding Time and checks whether boarding starts in time to respect TOBT.
  attribute triggerOffset: Duration; The locally defined time variable, in minutes before TOBT, at which the boarding status check is triggered (milestone 11).
  operation informPartnersOfAsbt(asbt: DateTime): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) when it occurs.
  operation checkBoardingStatus(asbt: DateTime, tobt: DateTime): Boolean; At a certain time before TOBT (local variable e.g. corresponding to aircraft type) a check shall be performed to check the boarding status, and inform the AO/GH in case TOBT needs to be updated.

class AircraftReadyMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 12: informs partners of the Actual Ready Time and alerts AO/GH if TOBT has passed without an ARDT or ready status message.
  operation informPartnersOfArdt(ardt: DateTime): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Ready Time (ARDT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft is ready for start up / push-back.
  operation checkReadyStatusReceived(tobt: DateTime, tolerance: Duration): Boolean; At TOBT + tolerance the AO/GH are informed that TOBT has passed and there has not been a ready status message yet.

class StartUpRequestedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 13: informs partners of the Actual Start-Up Request Time and alerts partners if no start-up request is made within the TSAT tolerance window.
  operation informPartnersOfAsrt(asrt: DateTime): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start up Request Time (ASRT) in the Airport CDM Platform.
  operation checkStartUpRequestedWithinTolerance(tsat: DateTime, tolerance: Duration): Boolean; If the start up request is not made by TSAT + tolerance, the AO/GH is informed that no start up has been requested, and should update TOBT.

class StartUpApprovedMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 14: informs partners of the Actual Start-Up Approval Time and checks and alerts if approval is not aligned with or granted by TSAT tolerance.
  operation informPartnersOfAsat(asat: DateTime): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual start up approval Time (ASAT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has received start up approval / push-back clearance.
  operation checkAsatAgainstTsat(asat: DateTime, tsat: DateTime, tolerance: Duration): Boolean; Checks if ASAT is in accordance to TSAT and alerts all relevant Airport CDM Partners when no start up has been granted; in case the start up approval is not granted at TSAT + tolerance, all relevant partners should be informed.
  operation resequenceFlight(flight: Flight): void; The flight will be re-sequenced when start up approval is not granted at TSAT + tolerance.

class OffBlockMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 15: informs partners of the Actual Off-Block Time, updates TTOT considering EXOT, and triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations.
  operation informPartnersOfAobt(aobt: DateTime): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Off-Block Time (AOBT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has commenced push-back / taxi from parking position.
  operation updateTtotConsideringExot(exot: Duration): DateTime; TTOT updated considering the EXOT.
  operation sendADpi(flight: Flight, isRemoteHolding: Boolean): void; AOBT always triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations or, in the case of remote holding, at a defined time prior to TTOT.
  operation checkTtotToleranceAndSendADpi(newTtot: DateTime, tolerance: Duration): void; After a first A-DPI is sent this check shall be performed to check TTOT updates against the TTOT tolerance before Network Operations is informed, with a new A-DPI, of the updated TTOT.

class TakeOffMilestone (package Milestones): Milestone 16: informs partners of the actual take-off, generates an airborne message, sends FSA and MVT messages, and removes the flight from the departure sequence.
  operation informPartnersOfTakeOff(atot: DateTime): void; Informs all relevant Airport CDM Partners about the actual take off.
  operation generateAirborneMessageAndRemoveFromSequence(flight: Flight): void; An airborne message is generated and the flight is removed from the departure sequence.
  operation sendFsaAndMvt(flight: Flight): void; FSA and MVT messages are sent.

class AirportCdmPlatform (package Systems): The Airport CDM Platform that is initiated per flight, processes all available flight information, computes updated times, and triggers the DPI process and partner notifications.
  operation calculateTobtAutomatically(eldt: DateTime): DateTime; Update of the ELDT can trigger a new TOBT to be entered by the AO/GH, or calculated automatically by the Airport CDM Platform.
  operation initiateForFlight(flight: Flight): void; The Airport CDM Platform is initiated for this flight, and all available information is processed.

class AtcSystem (package Systems): The ATC system that provides radar/system-derived times (ALDT, TSAT context) and calculates EXOT based on traffic load, gate/stand location, runway, and holding position.
  operation calculateExot(tobt: DateTime, trafficLoad: String, gateStandLocation: String, runwayInUse: String, holdingPositionWait: Duration): Duration; Following the receipt of the TOBT, the ATC system will calculate and provide the Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) based on the predicted traffic load, gate/stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the Holding Position.

class RadarSystem (package Systems): The radar system that detects a flight by its assigned SSR code when it crosses a FIR/ATC boundary, a defined range/position, or a predetermined level.
  operation detectFlight(ssrCode: String, boundaryOrPositionOrLevel: String): void; The radar system is able to detect a flight based upon the assigned SSR code when the flight crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary (milestone 4), or crosses either a defined range/position or passes/leaves a predetermined level (milestone 5).

class EtfmsSystem (package Systems): The Network Operations system (ETFMS) that monitors a flight's progress within 3 hours flying time of the destination and issues Flight Update Messages.
  operation monitorProgressAndSendFum(flight: Flight): FlightUpdateMessage; If the flight is within 3hrs flying time of the destination airport the Network Operations monitors progress of the flight using the ETFMS and sends a Flight Update Message (FUM) that provides updates of the flight's progress.

class AcarsSystem (package Systems): The ACARS system fitted to equipped aircraft that reports actual times such as landing, in-block, off-block, and take-off.

class AutomatedDockingSystem (package Systems): An automated docking or ground surveillance system (e.g. A-SMGCS) that detects in-block, off-block, or take-off events.

class PassengerInformationSystem (package Systems): The airport system, flight information display system (FIDS), or public address system used to inform passengers of boarding.

class DpiMessage (package Messages): A Departure Planning Information message (E-DPI, T-DPI, T-DPI-t, T-DPI-s, or A-DPI) sent to Network Operations to convey updated flight time estimates.
  attribute type: DpiMessageType; Which kind of DPI message this is: E-DPI, T-DPI, T-DPI-t, T-DPI-s, or A-DPI.
  operation canBeSent(informationProvided: Boolean, isConsistent: Boolean): Boolean; The E-DPI message should not be sent if no or inconsistent information is provided.

class FlightUpdateMessage (package Messages): The FUM sent by Network Operations providing updates of a flight's progress used to update ELDT.

class ReadyStatusMessage (package Messages): The RDY message confirming that the aircraft is ready and follows the indicated TOBT.

class DepartureClearanceMessage (package Messages): The DCL message or voice communication by which flight crew requests start-up.

class TakeOffNotificationMessage (package Messages): A notification message sent at take-off (FSA, MVT, or airborne message) informing partners and Network Operations of the actual take-off.
  attribute type: TakeOffNotificationType; Which kind of take-off notification this is: FSA, MVT, or airborne message.
