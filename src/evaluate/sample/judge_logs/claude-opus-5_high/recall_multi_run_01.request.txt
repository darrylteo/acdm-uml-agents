# The document: sixteen A-CDM milestones, sentences numbered

## Milestone 1: ATC Flight Plan Activated

### Definition

[M1.definition.1] The ICAO flight plan is submitted to ATC.
[M1.definition.2] The Airport CDM Platform is initiated for this flight, and all available information is processed.
### Origin and priority

[M1.origin_and_priority.1] The ATC Flight Plan is submitted by the Aircraft Operator and distributed by the IFPS.
[M1.origin_and_priority.2] All involved ATC units receive the flight plan, including departure and destination aerodromes.
### Timing

[M1.timing.1] Normally this takes place 3 hours before EOBT, however it may be later.
[M1.timing.2] In some cases a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) has been submitted, covering daily or weekly flights.
### Data Quality

[M1.data_quality.1] The ATC Flight Plan corresponds to the airport slot programme.
### Effect

[M1.effect.1] One aircraft turn-round normally includes an arriving and a departing flight, meaning that it will have two related flight plans.
[M1.effect.2] For coordinated airports, the outbound flight is already known.
[M1.effect.3] The flight plan may be used to update certain information such as type of aircraft.
[M1.effect.4] For long distance flights, the ELDT may differ from the airport slot.
[M1.effect.5] For non coordinated airports, the flight plan is used to initiate the outbound flight.
[M1.effect.6] The flight is ready not later than 15 minutes after the planned EOBT.
[M1.effect.7] The DPI process commences the correct messaging with Network Operations (if implemented - see attachment 2 for details).
### Procedures

[M1.procedures.1] To check consistency between ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data and then confirm the flight to the Network Operations and allow further local processing of the flight.
[M1.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify the consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent.
[M1.procedures.3] The AO must provide correct information before this first E-DPI message, in order to feed Network Operations with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration, and first destination data, as early in time as possible.
[M1.procedures.4] The E-DPI message should not be sent if no or inconsistent information is provided.
[M1.procedures.5] This process is triggered by:
[M1.procedures.6] - The first activation of the ATC Flight Plan (earliest EOBT-3 hr), or
[M1.procedures.7] - New or late submissions of the ATC Flight Plan, after cancellation or revised EOBT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M1.operational_status.1] INITIATED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M1.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT and EIBT updated for an arrival EOBT and ETOT updated for a departure The DPI process commences (if implemented - see section 3.7.3 for details).
## Milestone 2: EOBT - 2 hr

### Definition

[M2.definition.1] At EOBT-2 hr most flights will be known in the Airport CDM Platform including if they are regulated or not.
[M2.definition.2] All regulated flights receive a CTOT from Network Operations.
### Origin and priority

[M2.origin_and_priority.1] The CTOT is issued by the Network Operations and is sent to relevant ATS units as well as the departure aerodrome.
[M2.origin_and_priority.2] CTOT flights usually have a priority over unregulated flights.
### Timing

[M2.timing.1] If the flight is regulated, a CTOT is issued at EOBT-2h.
### Data Quality

[M2.data_quality.1] Not applicable.
### Effect

[M2.effect.1] For inbound flights, ELDT is updated based on information provided by the FUM messages, taking into account the actual progress of the flight.
### Procedures

[M2.procedures.1] To check (before or after take off from outstation) whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan and to inform Network Operations about the updated take off time estimate, using a T-DPI Message.
[M2.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimated off block time at EOBT-2 hrs.
[M2.procedures.3] At EOBT-2 hrs Network Operations is informed through the first T-DPI message.
[M2.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT, if later than EOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.5] In the case of manual input of TOBT, this estimate will override the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M2.procedures.6] This procedure is triggered by
[M2.procedures.7] - a time stamp, at EOBT - 2h.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M2.operational_status.1] N. A.
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M2.action_on_cdm.1] ETOT/TTOT/CTOT Mark appropriate fields as REGULATED
## Milestone 3: Take Off from Outstation

### Definition

[M3.definition.1] The ATOT from the outstation (ADEP)
### Origin and priority

[M3.origin_and_priority.1] The outstation provides ATOT to the Network Operations and Aircraft Operator.
### Timing

[M3.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M3.data_quality.1] The accuracy of ATOT is +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M3.effect.1] If the departure airport is more than 3hrs flying time from the destination airport the ATOT is received from either the Network Operations FUM or via the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler.
[M3.effect.2] Using the ATOT an ELDT can be calculated by using the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL.
[M3.effect.3] If the flight is within 3hrs flying time of the destination airport the Network Operations monitors progress of the flight using the ETFMS and send a Flight Update Message (FUM) that provides updates of the flight's progress.
### Procedures

[M3.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH estimated landing time after take off from outstation are consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan, and when needed inform the Network Operations about the updated take off time estimates using a T-DPI-t Message.
[M3.procedures.2] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan at take off from outstation.
[M3.procedures.3] A TTOT tolerance of 5 minutes is respected before Network Operations is informed of the updated TTOT.
[M3.procedures.4] Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.5] In case EOBT is later than EIBT+MTTT, TTOT equals EOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.6] In the case where TOBT is available this prediction will overrule the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT.
[M3.procedures.7] This process is triggered by
[M3.procedures.8] - the take off from outstation.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M3.operational_status.1] AIRBORNE
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M3.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 4: Local Radar Update

### Definition

[M4.definition.1] The flight enters the FIR (Flight Information Region) or the local airspace of the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M4.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from the Area Control Centre (ACC) or Approach Control Unit that is associated with an airport.
[M4.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system is able to detect a flight based upon the assigned SSR code when the flight crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary.
### Timing

[M4.timing.1] Dependent upon the position of the airport in relation to the FIR boundary.
### Data Quality

[M4.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M4.effect.1] Update of the ELDT can trigger a new TOBT to be entered by the AO/GH, or calculated automatically by the Airport CDM Platform.
[M4.effect.2] The accuracy of ELDT is particularly important at this stage since downstream decisions are taken, such as stand /gate / aircraft changes, preparation of arrival sequence, preparation of ground handling operations, decisions for connecting passengers.
[M4.effect.3] Uncertainty and ELDT non-accuracy at this stage significantly increase risks for bad and last minute decisions and internal disruptions.
[M4.effect.4] The objective to decrease the number of stand and gate changes in the last 30 minutes requires high accuracy regarding departure and arrival times.
[M4.effect.5] Therefore, taking into account the taxi-in time (EXIT), any change to a stand or gate is not preferred after ELDT-30'.
[M4.effect.6] The update of TOBT for the related departing flight takes place following this milestone.
[M4.effect.7] Decisions such as the turn-round period, connecting passengers etc are taken and need to be stable at this event.
[M4.effect.8] An estimated in-block time (EIBT) is computed using the ELDT and the estimated taxi-in time.
### Procedures

[M4.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M4.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M4.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M4.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M4.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M4.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M4.operational_status.1] FIR
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M4.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 5: Final Approach

### Definition

[M5.definition.1] The flight enters the Final Approach phase at the destination airport.
### Origin and priority

[M5.origin_and_priority.1] This information is normally available from ATC.
[M5.origin_and_priority.2] The radar system detects a flight based upon the assigned SSR code and identifies when the flight crosses either a defined range / position or passes/leaves a predetermined level.
### Timing

[M5.timing.1] Dependent upon local parameters that are defined by ATC.
### Data Quality

[M5.data_quality.1] Must be equal to the accuracy of the ATC system.
### Effect

[M5.effect.1] Update of the ELDT to determine a new TOBT.
[M5.effect.2] When a flight reaches this stage it is usually between 2 and 5 minutes from landing (depending on the parameter set by ATC).
[M5.effect.3] This is often the prompt for many partners to start moving resources connected with the flight, such as positioning a parking marshal and ground handling services.
### Procedures

[M5.procedures.1] To commence the TOBT process and check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M5.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M5.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the up-dated TOBT.
[M5.procedures.4] The TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed of updated TTOT.
[M5.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M5.procedures.6] - the detection of the flight by radar in either FIR, TMA, or on Final Approach.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M5.operational_status.1] FINAL
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M5.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated
## Milestone 6: Landed

### Definition

[M6.definition.1] ALDT - Actual Landing Time.
[M6.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft touches down on a runway.
[M6.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATA - Actual Time of Arrival landing, ACARS=ON).
### Origin and priority

[M6.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or by ACARS from equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M6.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M6.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M6.effect.1] The occurrence of ALDT triggers an update of downstream estimates: TOBT and TTOT are updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the defined turn-round period for the departing flight.
[M6.effect.2] The EIBT can be updated according to the ALDT +EXIT.
### Procedures

[M6.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M6.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
[M6.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M6.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M6.procedures.6] - Actual Landing Time: ALDT
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M6.operational_status.1] LANDED
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M6.action_on_cdm.1] ELDT changes to ALDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT updated

## Milestone 9: Final Confirmation of the TOBT

### Definition

[M9.definition.1] The time at which the Aircraft Operator or Ground Handler provide their most accurate TOBT taking into account the operational situation.
### Origin and priority

[M9.origin_and_priority.1] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler provides the information.
### Timing

[M9.timing.1] The information is provided t minutes before EOBT (t is a parameter time agreed locally).
### Data Quality

[M9.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M9.effect.1] The aim of the final TOBT is to give a timely, accurate and reliable assessment of the off-block time.
[M9.effect.2] It is recognised that main benefits of sharing the TOBT are expected in case of disruptions (internal or external).
[M9.effect.3] In such cases, the difference between EOBT (shared by ATC, Network Operations and Stand / Gate Management) and TOBT may be important.
[M9.effect.4] An accurate TOBT at [EOBT-t minutes] is a pre-requisite for ATC to establish a push back / pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.5] Emphasis is put on the need for the Aircraft Operator to integrate his own strategy to compute a TOBT related to the flight.
[M9.effect.6] Following the receipt of the TOBT, the ATC system will calculate and provide the Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) based on the predicted traffic load, gate / stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the Holding Position, etc. The flight is introduced into the pre-departure sequence.
[M9.effect.7] The Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M9.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M9.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M9.procedures.3] This check should be performed at a predefined time (local parameter) to confirm TOBT prior to TSAT issue and verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimates given the updated TOBT.
[M9.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M9.procedures.5] This Milestone Process is actually constantly applicable in the CDM Platform, as soon as a TOBT is available.
[M9.procedures.6] However the confirmed TOBT prior to TSAT has special status, where AO/GH check the quality of TOBT before TSAT issue.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M9.operational_status.1] IN-BLOCK
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M9.action_on_cdm.1] This process is triggered by - a new TOBT or TTOT update.
[M9.action_on_cdm.2] No need to confirm an existing TOBT if it has been manually modified before.

### Definition

[M10.definition.1] The time ATC issues the Target Start Up Approval Time
### Origin and priority

[M10.origin_and_priority.1] ATC
### Timing

[M10.timing.1] The information is provided t-minutes before EOBT, where t is a parameter agreed locally
### Data Quality

[M10.data_quality.1] Accuracy is agreed locally.
### Effect

[M10.effect.1] The flight is stabilised into the pre-departure sequence.
[M10.effect.2] The Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler, in coordination with the aircrew, can manage the turn-round process accordingly.
### Procedures

[M10.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant partners of the TSAT that has been allocated to the flight.
[M10.procedures.2] The Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.3] Second step: To check whether the number of TOBT updates exceeds a tolerance defined locally, after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.4] First: The TSAT will indicate to the partners the time when the start up approval can be expected.
[M10.procedures.5] Network Operations will be informed with a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights.
[M10.procedures.6] No check is performed.
[M10.procedures.7] Second: A check shall be performed to see the number of TOBT updates after TSAT has been issued.
[M10.procedures.8] In case the number of TOBT updates exceeds a threshold, then the TOBT input should be processed according to local procedure.
[M10.procedures.9] This process is triggered by
[M10.procedures.10] - A defined time (local parameter) before TOBT
[M10.procedures.11] - TOBT update after TSAT issue

[M11.definition.2] This is not to be confused with the time passengers are pre-called to the gate via flight information display systems (FIDS) or public address systems.
### Origin and priority

[M11.origin_and_priority.1] Automatic from airport system or manual input by Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M11.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M11.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M11.effect.1] When boarding commences it gives the Airport CDM Partners a good indication of whether the TOBT/TSAT will be respected.
### Procedures

[M11.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT).
[M11.procedures.2] Second step: To check whether boarding starts in time to respect TOBT and inform the AO/GH in case TOBT needs to be updated.
[M11.procedures.3] Inform of Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) when it occurs.
[M11.procedures.4] At a certain time before TOBT (local variable e.g. corresponding to aircraft type) a check shall be performed to check the boarding status.
[M11.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M11.procedures.6] - a time variable <value> minutes before TOBT.
### Operational Status (changes to)

## Milestone 12: Aircraft Ready

### Definition

[M12.definition.1] The time when all doors are closed, boarding bridge removed, push back vehicle connected, ready to taxi immediately upon reception of TWR instructions (ARDT).
### Origin and priority

[M12.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by the Aircraft Operator/ Ground Handler.
### Timing

[M12.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M12.data_quality.1] Data is directly available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M12.effect.1] ATC refines the pre-departure sequence.
[M12.effect.2] The pilot requests start up just before TSAT, following coordination with the Ground Handler.
[M12.effect.3] (Dispatcher / Supervisor / Redcap).
### Procedures

[M12.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Ready Time (ARDT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft is ready for start up / push-back.
[M12.procedures.2] Second step: To inform the AO/GH that TOBT has passed and the Airport CDM Platform has not yet received ARDT or Ready Status (RDY).
[M12.procedures.3] Inform of ARDT or RDY confirming that the flight follows the indicated TOBT.
[M12.procedures.4] At TOBT + tolerance the AO/GH are informed that TOBT has passed and there has not been a ready status message yet.
[M12.procedures.5] This procedure is triggered by
[M12.procedures.6] - an input to the Airport CDM Platform.

## Milestone 15: Off-Block

### Definition

[M15.definition.1] AOBT - Actual Off-Block Time.
[M15.definition.2] The time the aircraft pushes back/vacates the parking position (Equivalent to Airline/Handler ATD - Actual Time of Departure ACARS=OUT).
### Origin and priority

[M15.origin_and_priority.1] ACARS equipped aircraft or automated docking systems or ATC systems (e.g. A-SMGCS) or by manual input.
### Timing

[M15.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M15.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M15.effect.1] TTOT updated considering the EXOT.
### Procedures

[M15.procedures.1] First step: To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners of Actual Off-Block Time (AOBT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has commenced push-back / taxi from parking position.
[M15.procedures.2] Second step: To check if TTOT changes by more than the agreed tolerance and inform Network Operations.
[M15.procedures.3] Inform of AOBT when it occurs.
[M15.procedures.4] AOBT always triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations or in the case of remote holding at a defined time prior to TTOT.
[M15.procedures.5] After a first A-DPI is sent this check shall be performed to check TTOT updates against the TTOT tolerance before Network Operations is informed, with a new A-DPI, of the updated TTOT.
[M15.procedures.6] This process is triggered by AOBT detection.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M15.operational_status.1] OFF-BLOCK
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M15.action_on_cdm.1] AOBT recorded
## Milestone 16: Take Off

### Definition

[M16.definition.1] ATOT - Actual Take Off Time.
[M16.definition.2] This is the time that an aircraft takes off from the runway.
[M16.definition.3] (Equivalent to ATC ATD-Actual Time of Departure, ACARS = OFF).
### Origin and priority

[M16.origin_and_priority.1] Provided by ATC system or from ACARS equipped aircraft.
### Timing

[M16.timing.1] The information is directly available as soon as possible after occurrence of the milestone.
### Data Quality

[M16.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.
### Effect

[M16.effect.1] FSA and MVT messages are sent.
### Procedures

[M16.procedures.1] To inform all relevant Airport CDM Partners about the actual take off.
[M16.procedures.2] An airborne message is generated and the flight is removed from the departure sequence.
[M16.procedures.3] This process is triggered by Tower FDPS, A-SMGCS / Radar detection or ACARS.
### Operational Status (changes to)

[M16.operational_status.1] DEPARTED / TAKE OFF
### Action on CDM Operation (ACISP)

[M16.action_on_cdm.1] ATOT recorded

# The model

class CDMPartner (package Stakeholders): A generalization of the parties that take part in the Airport CDM process and originate or receive its information.
  attribute name: String; The identifying name of the stakeholder that originates or receives information in the Airport CDM process.

class AircraftOperator (package Stakeholders): The airline or its representative that submits the ATC Flight Plan, provides TOBT and other estimates, and manages the turn-round.
  operation submitFlightPlan(flightPlan: FlightPlan): void; The ATC Flight Plan is submitted by the Aircraft Operator, normally 3 hours before EOBT although it may be later, or as a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) covering daily or weekly flights (milestone 1).
  operation provideFinalTOBT(flight: Flight, tobt: TimeQuantity, offsetMinutes: Integer): void; The Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler provides their most accurate TOBT taking into account the operational situation, t minutes before EOBT where t is a locally agreed parameter (milestone 9).

class GroundHandler (package Stakeholders): The party that performs ground handling operations and, together with the Aircraft Operator, provides turn-round related times such as ACGT, ARDT and TOBT.
  operation recordACGT(flight: Flight, acgt: TimeQuantity): void; The Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler provides the Actual Commence of Ground Handling time (ACGT), available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 8).
  operation recordARDT(flight: Flight, ardt: TimeQuantity): void; The Ground Handler provides the Actual Ready Time (ARDT) when all doors are closed, boarding bridge removed and push back vehicle connected, ready to taxi immediately upon reception of TWR instructions (milestone 12).
  operation recordASBT(flight: Flight, asbt: TimeQuantity): void; ASBT is provided automatically from the airport system or by manual input by the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler when the gate opens for passengers to physically start boarding (milestone 11).

class AirTrafficControlUnit (package Stakeholders): The ATC unit (tower, approach, or area control centre) that detects flight progress, issues TSAT/ASAT and manages the pre-departure sequence.
  operation issueTSAT(flight: Flight, offsetMinutes: Integer): TimeQuantity; ATC issues the Target Start Up Approval Time (TSAT) t-minutes before EOBT, where t is a parameter agreed locally (milestone 10).
  operation calculateEXOT(flight: Flight): DurationEstimate; Following receipt of the TOBT, the ATC system calculates and provides the Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) based on the predicted traffic load, gate/stand location, runway in use, and waiting period at the Holding Position (milestone 9).
  operation recordASRT(flight: Flight): TimeQuantity; ATC records the Actual Start up Request Time (ASRT), based on pilot request, available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 13).
  operation confirmTSATToPilot(flight: Flight): void; ATC confirms TSAT to the pilot in order to maintain the aircraft in the pre-departure sequence (milestone 13).
  operation recordASAT(flight: Flight): TimeQuantity; ATC provides the Actual start up Approval Time (ASAT) when the aircraft receives its start up approval, available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestone 14).

class NetworkOperations (package Stakeholders): The Network Manager function that issues CTOT, monitors flight progress and receives DPI messages to coordinate the flight with the wider ATM network.
  operation issueCTOT(flight: Flight): TimeQuantity; The CTOT is issued by the Network Operations at EOBT-2h if the flight is regulated, and is sent to relevant ATS units as well as the departure aerodrome (milestone 2).
  operation monitorFlightProgress(flight: Flight): void; If the flight is within 3hrs flying time of the destination airport, Network Operations monitors progress of the flight using the ETFMS (milestone 3).
  operation sendFlightUpdateMessage(flight: Flight): FlightUpdateMessage; Network Operations sends a Flight Update Message (FUM) that provides updates of the flight's progress, used to update ELDT (milestones 1, 3).

class FlightCrew (package Stakeholders): The pilot and cockpit crew who request start-up and push-back and coordinate with the Ground Handler around TSAT.

class StandGateManagement (package Stakeholders): The stakeholder responsible for stand and gate allocation who shares EOBT information with ATC and Network Operations.

class Flight (package Flights): A single arriving or departing operation of an aircraft, tracked through the sixteen milestones and carrying an operational status.
  attribute operationalStatus: FlightOperationalStatus; The current operational status of the flight, updated as each milestone occurs (e.g. INITIATED, AIRBORNE, LANDED, OFF_BLOCK).
  attribute isRegulated: Boolean; Whether the flight has received a CTOT from Network Operations and its fields are marked REGULATED (milestone 2).
  attribute offBlockTime: TimeQuantity; The successive scheduled, estimated, target and actual off-block time values (SOBT, EOBT, TOBT, AOBT) recorded for the flight.
  attribute inBlockTime: TimeQuantity; The successive estimated and actual in-block time values (EIBT, AIBT) recorded for the flight.
  attribute landingTime: TimeQuantity; The successive estimated and actual landing time values (ELDT, ALDT) recorded for the flight.
  attribute takeOffTime: TimeQuantity; The successive estimated, target, calculated and actual take-off time values (ETOT, TTOT, CTOT, ATOT) recorded for the flight.
  attribute startUpApprovalTime: TimeQuantity; The target and actual start-up approval time values (TSAT, ASAT) recorded for the flight.
  attribute boardingTime: TimeQuantity; The actual start boarding time (ASBT) recorded for the flight, accurate to +/-1 minute (milestone 11).
  attribute readyTime: TimeQuantity; The actual ready time (ARDT) recorded for the flight, accurate to +/-1 minute (milestone 12).
  attribute startUpRequestTime: TimeQuantity; The actual start-up request time (ASRT) recorded for the flight, accurate to +/-1 minute (milestone 13).
  attribute groundHandlingStartTime: TimeQuantity; The actual commencement of ground handling time (ACGT) recorded for the flight, accurate to +/-1 minute (milestone 8).
  attribute taxiOutTime: DurationEstimate; The Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT) calculated by ATC based on predicted traffic load, gate/stand location, runway in use and holding-position waiting period (milestone 9).
  attribute taxiInTime: DurationEstimate; The estimated taxi-in time (EXIT) used together with ELDT to compute EIBT (milestones 4, 6).
  attribute minimumTurnRoundTime: DurationEstimate; The Minimum Turn-round Time (MTTT) used with EIBT and EXOT as a basis for calculating TTOT (milestones 2, 3).
  attribute estimatedTurnRoundTime: DurationEstimate; The Estimated Turn-round Time (ETTT) used to update TOBT after ground handling starts (milestone 8).
  attribute parkingPosition: ParkingPosition; The stand or gate currently assigned to the flight, changes to which are avoided after ELDT-30 minutes (milestone 4).
  operation updateOperationalStatus(newStatus: FlightOperationalStatus): void; The flight's operational status changes to the value defined by each milestone as it occurs, e.g. INITIATED (milestone 1), AIRBORNE (milestone 3), FIR (milestone 4), FINAL (milestone 5), LANDED (milestone 6), IN-BLOCK (milestones 7, 8, 9), SEQUENCED (milestone 10), BOARDING (milestone 11), READY (milestone 12), OFF-BLOCK (milestone 15), DEPARTED/TAKE OFF (milestone 16).
  operation calculateEIBT(eldt: TimeQuantity, taxiInTime: DurationEstimate): TimeQuantity; An estimated in-block time (EIBT) is computed using the ELDT and the estimated taxi-in time (milestone 4); the EIBT can be updated according to the ALDT+EXIT (milestone 6).

class TurnRound (package Flights): The aircraft turn-round linking an arriving flight and its related departing flight for the purpose of estimating and analysing turn-round times.
  attribute period: DurationEstimate; The defined or estimated turn-round period (MTTT/ETTT) on which the departing flight's TOBT and TTOT updates are based (milestones 6, 7, 8).
  operation calculateTOBTFromTurnRoundPeriod(referenceTime: TimeQuantity, period: DurationEstimate): TimeQuantity; TOBT for the departing flight is updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the defined or estimated turn-round period, following ALDT (milestone 6), AIBT (milestone 7) or ACGT (milestone 8).

class Aircraft (package Flights): The physical aircraft operating a flight, identified by type and registration, which the ATC Flight Plan may be used to update.
  attribute registration: String; The aircraft registration that must be provided consistently before the first E-DPI message is sent (milestone 1).
  attribute aircraftType: String; The type of aircraft, which the ATC Flight Plan may be used to update (milestone 1).

class FlightPlan (package Flights): The ICAO/ATC Flight Plan submitted by the Aircraft Operator and distributed by IFPS, including repetitive flight plans (RFPL), against which flight estimates are checked for consistency.
  attribute isRepetitive: Boolean; Whether the submitted flight plan is a repetitive flight plan (RFPL) covering daily or weekly flights (milestone 1).
  attribute destination: String; The first destination aerodrome data that must be consistent and correct before the first E-DPI message is sent (milestone 1).
  attribute estimatedElapsedTime: Duration; The Estimated Elapsed Time stated on the flight plan, used together with ATOT to calculate ELDT (milestone 3).

class AirportSlot (package Flights): The airport slot programme entry that the ATC Flight Plan must correspond to for consistency checking at coordinated airports.
  attribute assignedTime: DateTime; The time assigned by the airport slot programme, with which the ATC Flight Plan must be consistent; for long-distance flights the ELDT may differ from this slot time (milestone 1).

class ParkingPosition (package Flights): The stand or gate assigned to a flight, changes to which are discouraged after ELDT-30 minutes.
  attribute identifier: String; The stand or gate identifier assigned to the flight.

class TimeQuantity (package Timings): A named time value describing a flight's progress toward a milestone event, distinguished by a prefix (Scheduled, Estimated, Target, Actual, Calculated) and an event (e.g. Off-Block, In-Block, Landing, Take-Off, Start-Up Approval, Boarding, Ready, Start-Up Request, Ground Handling Start); every successive value is appended with its originator and time of update rather than overwritten.
  attribute prefix: TimePrefix; Whether this value is a Scheduled, Estimated, Target, Actual or Calculated time for its event.
  attribute event: TimeEvent; The milestone event this time quantity describes, e.g. Off-Block, In-Block, Landing or Take-Off.
  attribute value: DateTime; The date and time of the estimated, target, calculated or actual occurrence.
  attribute accuracy: Duration; The stated accuracy of this value, typically +/-1 minute for actual times recorded directly at occurrence (e.g. ALDT, AIBT, AOBT, ATOT, ASBT, ARDT, ASRT, ASAT).
  attribute originator: CDMPartner; The stakeholder or system that provided or last updated this value.
  attribute recordedAt: DateTime; The time this value was appended to the flight's record, so that a turn-round can be analysed afterwards without overwriting prior values.

class DurationEstimate (package Timings): A calculated time interval such as the Estimated Taxi-Out Time (EXOT), Estimated Taxi-In Time (EXIT), Minimum Turn-round Time (MTTT) or Estimated Turn-round Time (ETTT), used as input to compute event-based time quantities.
  attribute durationType: DurationType; The kind of calculated interval this estimate represents (Taxi-Out Time, Taxi-In Time, Minimum Turn-round Time or Estimated Turn-round Time).
  attribute value: Duration; The length of the estimated interval.

class Milestone (package Milestones): A generalization of the sixteen defined events in the CDM turn-round process, each with its trigger, timing rule, effect and procedure applied over the flight's entity classes.
  attribute number: Integer; The milestone number (1 to 16) identifying this event in the CDM turn-round process.
  attribute resultingStatus: FlightOperationalStatus; The operational status the flight changes to when this milestone occurs.
  attribute ttotTolerance: Duration; The locally agreed tolerance for TTOT change (e.g. 5 minutes at take-off from outstation) beyond which Network Operations must be re-informed.

class TOBTConsistencyMilestone (package Milestones): An abstract generalization of the milestones (Local Radar Update, Final Approach, Landed, In-Block, Ground Handling Started, Final Confirmation of the TOBT) that each check TOBT consistency against the ATC Flight Plan and notify Network Operations when TTOT changes beyond the agreed tolerance.
  operation checkTOBTConsistency(flight: Flight, flightPlan: FlightPlan): Boolean; A check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT, checking whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan (milestones 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9).
  operation notifyNetworkOperationsIfTTOTExceedsTolerance(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance (milestones 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9).

class MilestoneFlightPlanActivation (package Milestones): Milestone 1: submission and activation of the ATC Flight Plan, initiating the Airport CDM Platform for the flight and checking consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI.
  attribute activationOffset: Duration; The normal timing of this milestone, 3 hours before EOBT, though it may occur later.
  attribute readinessOffset: Duration; The maximum time after the planned EOBT, 15 minutes, by which the flight must be ready.
  operation checkFlightPlanConsistency(flight: Flight, flightPlan: FlightPlan, airportSlot: AirportSlot): Boolean; A check shall be performed to verify the consistency between the ATC Flight Plan, Airport Slot and Airport flight data before the first E-DPI is sent (milestone 1).
  operation confirmFlightToNetworkOperations(flight: Flight): void; The process confirms the flight to the Network Operations and allows further local processing of the flight, feeding Network Operations with consistent SOBT, aircraft registration and first destination data as early as possible (milestone 1).
  operation sendEDPI(flight: Flight): void; The E-DPI message should not be sent if no or inconsistent information is provided by the AO before this first E-DPI message (milestone 1).

class MilestoneEOBTMinus2Hours (package Milestones): Milestone 2: at EOBT-2 hours, regulated flights receive a CTOT and estimates are checked against the ATC Flight Plan before informing Network Operations via the first T-DPI.
  attribute offsetBeforeEOBT: Duration; The fixed timing of this milestone, 2 hours before EOBT, at which a CTOT is issued for regulated flights.
  operation checkEstimatesConsistency(flight: Flight, flightPlan: FlightPlan): Boolean; A check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan estimated off block time at EOBT-2 hrs, checking whether AO/GH flight estimates are consistent with the ATC Flight Plan (milestone 2).
  operation informNetworkOperationsViaTDPI(flight: Flight): void; At EOBT-2 hrs Network Operations is informed through the first T-DPI message of the updated take off time estimate (milestone 2).
  operation calculateTTOT(eibt: TimeQuantity, mttt: DurationEstimate, exot: DurationEstimate, eobt: TimeQuantity, tobt: TimeQuantity): TimeQuantity; Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT, if later than EOBT+EXOT. In the case of manual input of TOBT, this estimate overrides the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT (milestone 2).

class MilestoneTakeOffFromOutstation (package Milestones): Milestone 3: the ATOT from the outstation is used to check estimated landing time consistency with the outbound ATC Flight Plan and, if the TTOT tolerance is exceeded, inform Network Operations via a T-DPI-t.
  operation checkLandingEstimateConsistency(flight: Flight, outboundFlightPlan: FlightPlan): Boolean; A check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan at take off from outstation, checking whether the AO/GH estimated landing time is consistent with the outbound ATC Flight Plan (milestone 3).
  operation notifyNetworkOperationsIfTTOTExceedsTolerance(flight: Flight, toleranceMinutes: Integer): void; A TTOT tolerance of 5 minutes is respected before Network Operations is informed of the updated TTOT using a T-DPI-t Message (milestone 3).
  operation calculateTTOT(eibt: TimeQuantity, mttt: DurationEstimate, exot: DurationEstimate, eobt: TimeQuantity, tobt: TimeQuantity): TimeQuantity; Calculation basis for the TTOT shall take into account EIBT+MTTT+EXOT. In case EOBT is later than EIBT+MTTT, TTOT equals EOBT+EXOT. Where TOBT is available this prediction overrules the EIBT+MTTT estimate, hence TTOT equals TOBT+EXOT (milestone 3).
  operation calculateELDTFromATOT(atot: TimeQuantity, estimatedElapsedTime: Duration): TimeQuantity; Using the ATOT an ELDT can be calculated by using the Estimated Elapsed Time on the FPL, if the departure airport is more than 3hrs flying time from the destination airport (milestone 3).

class MilestoneLocalRadarUpdate (package Milestones): Milestone 4: detection of the flight entering the FIR or local airspace, commencing the TOBT process and checking TOBT feasibility against the ATC Flight Plan.

class MilestoneFinalApproach (package Milestones): Milestone 5: detection of the flight entering Final Approach, commencing the TOBT process and checking TOBT feasibility against the ATC Flight Plan.

class MilestoneLanded (package Milestones): Milestone 6: the actual landing time (ALDT) triggers updates to TOBT and TTOT based on the defined turn-round period and checks TOBT feasibility against the ATC Flight Plan.

class MilestoneInBlock (package Milestones): Milestone 7: the actual in-block time (AIBT) triggers updates to TOBT and TTOT based on the estimated turn-round period and checks TOBT feasibility against the ATC Flight Plan.

class MilestoneGroundHandlingStarted (package Milestones): Milestone 8: the actual commencement of ground handling (ACGT) triggers a TOBT update based on the estimated turn-round period and checks TOBT feasibility against the ATC Flight Plan.

class MilestoneFinalTOBTConfirmation (package Milestones): Milestone 9: the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler provides their most accurate TOBT before EOBT, which is checked against the ATC Flight Plan prior to TSAT issue.
  attribute confirmationOffset: Duration; The locally agreed parameter t, the number of minutes before EOBT at which the AO/GH provides their most accurate final TOBT.

class MilestoneTSATIssued (package Milestones): Milestone 10: ATC issues the TSAT, informs partners and Network Operations, and checks the number of TOBT updates after TSAT against a local tolerance.
  attribute issueOffset: Duration; The locally agreed parameter t, the number of minutes before EOBT/TOBT at which ATC issues the TSAT.
  attribute tobtUpdateThreshold: Integer; The locally defined threshold on the number of TOBT updates permitted after TSAT has been issued before local procedure is applied.
  operation informPartnersOfTSAT(flight: Flight): void; All relevant partners are informed of the TSAT that has been allocated to the flight; Network Operations is informed by a T-DPI-s for non regulated flights (milestone 10).
  operation checkTOBTUpdatesAfterTSAT(flight: Flight, threshold: Integer): Boolean; A check shall be performed to see the number of TOBT updates after TSAT has been issued against a tolerance defined locally; in case the number of TOBT updates exceeds this threshold, the TOBT input should be processed according to local procedure (milestone 10).

class MilestoneBoardingStarts (package Milestones): Milestone 11: boarding of passengers begins; partners are informed of ASBT and boarding status is checked against TOBT.
  attribute boardingCheckOffset: Duration; The local variable time before TOBT (e.g. based on aircraft type) at which boarding status is checked.
  operation informPartnersOfASBT(flight: Flight, asbt: TimeQuantity): void; All relevant Airport CDM Partners are informed of the Actual Start Boarding Time (ASBT) when it occurs (milestone 11).
  operation checkBoardingStatus(flight: Flight): Boolean; At a certain time before TOBT (local variable e.g. corresponding to aircraft type) a check shall be performed to check the boarding status, checking whether boarding starts in time to respect TOBT, and to inform the AO/GH in case TOBT needs to be updated (milestone 11).

class MilestoneAircraftReady (package Milestones): Milestone 12: the aircraft is ready for start-up/push-back (ARDT); partners are informed and, past TOBT plus tolerance without a ready status, the Aircraft Operator/Ground Handler is alerted.
  attribute readyTolerance: Duration; The tolerance added to TOBT after which the AO/GH is informed that no ARDT or Ready Status has been received.
  operation informPartnersOfARDT(flight: Flight, ardt: TimeQuantity): void; All relevant Airport CDM Partners are informed of Actual Ready Time (ARDT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft is ready for start up/push-back (milestone 12).
  operation checkReadyStatusReceived(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): Boolean; At TOBT + tolerance the AO/GH are informed that TOBT has passed and there has not been a ready status message (ARDT or RDY) yet (milestone 12).

class MilestoneStartUpRequested (package Milestones): Milestone 13: ATC records the actual start-up request time (ASRT) and alerts partners if no request is made within the TSAT tolerance window.
  attribute requestTolerance: Duration; The locally agreed TSAT tolerance window within which a start-up request must be made before partners are alerted.
  operation informPartnersOfASRT(flight: Flight, asrt: TimeQuantity): void; All relevant Airport CDM Partners are informed of Actual Start up Request Time (ASRT) in the Airport CDM Platform when it occurs (milestone 13).
  operation alertIfNoStartUpRequested(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; If the start up request is not made by TSAT + tolerance, all relevant Airport CDM Partners are alerted and the AO/GH is informed that no start up has been requested and should update TOBT (milestone 13).

class MilestoneStartUpApproved (package Milestones): Milestone 14: ATC records the actual start-up approval time (ASAT), checks it against TSAT, and alerts and re-sequences the flight if approval is not granted within tolerance.
  attribute approvalTolerance: Duration; The tolerance added to TSAT after which, if start-up approval has not been granted, all partners are informed and the flight is re-sequenced.
  operation informPartnersOfASAT(flight: Flight, asat: TimeQuantity): void; All relevant Airport CDM Partners are informed of Actual start up approval Time (ASAT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has received start up approval/push-back clearance when it occurs (milestone 14).
  operation checkASATAgainstTSAT(flight: Flight): Boolean; A check shall be performed to see if ASAT is in accordance with TSAT (milestone 14).
  operation alertAndResequenceIfNoApproval(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; In case the start up approval is not granted at TSAT + tolerance, all relevant partners are informed and the flight will be re-sequenced (milestone 14).

class MilestoneOffBlock (package Milestones): Milestone 15: the actual off-block time (AOBT) is recorded, TTOT is updated considering EXOT, and an A-DPI is sent to Network Operations, with further A-DPIs if TTOT changes beyond tolerance.
  operation informPartnersOfAOBT(flight: Flight, aobt: TimeQuantity): void; All relevant Airport CDM Partners are informed of Actual Off-Block Time (AOBT) in the Airport CDM Platform and that the aircraft has commenced push-back/taxi from parking position, when it occurs (milestone 15).
  operation sendADPI(flight: Flight): void; AOBT always triggers an A-DPI message to Network Operations, or in the case of remote holding at a defined time prior to TTOT (milestone 15).
  operation checkTTOTAfterAOBT(flight: Flight, tolerance: Duration): void; After a first A-DPI is sent this check shall be performed to check TTOT updates against the TTOT tolerance before Network Operations is informed, with a new A-DPI, of the updated TTOT (milestone 15).

class MilestoneTakeOff (package Milestones): Milestone 16: the actual take-off time (ATOT) is recorded, FSA and MVT messages are sent, and the flight is removed from the departure sequence.
  operation informPartnersOfTakeOff(flight: Flight): void; All relevant Airport CDM Partners are informed about the actual take off (milestone 16).
  operation generateAirborneMessage(flight: Flight): void; An airborne message is generated and FSA and MVT messages are sent (milestone 16).
  operation removeFromDepartureSequence(flight: Flight): void; The flight is removed from the departure sequence following take off (milestone 16).

class AirportCDMPlatform (package Systems): The Airport CDM Platform that is initiated for each flight, holds all available flight information, and performs the ACISP checks, updates and notifications defined for each milestone.
  operation initiateForFlight(flight: Flight): void; The Airport CDM Platform is initiated for this flight, and all available information is processed (milestone 1).
  operation updateEstimatesOnActivation(flight: Flight, isArrival: Boolean): void; For an arrival, ELDT and EIBT are updated; for a departure, EOBT and ETOT are updated (milestone 1).
  operation markRegulated(flight: Flight): void; ETOT/TTOT/CTOT are updated and appropriate fields are marked as REGULATED (milestone 2).
  operation updateEstimatesAfterOutstationTakeOff(flight: Flight): void; ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT are updated following take off from outstation (milestone 3).
  operation updateEstimatesAfterRadarUpdate(flight: Flight): void; ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT are updated following local radar detection of the flight (milestone 4).
  operation updateEstimatesAfterFinalApproach(flight: Flight): void; ELDT, EIBT, TOBT and TTOT are updated following detection of final approach (milestone 5).
  operation updateEstimatesAfterLanding(flight: Flight, aldt: TimeQuantity): void; ELDT changes to ALDT, and EIBT, TOBT and TTOT are updated (milestone 6).
  operation updateEstimatesAfterInBlock(flight: Flight, aibt: TimeQuantity): void; EIBT changes to AIBT, and TOBT and TTOT are updated (milestone 7).
  operation updateEstimatesAfterGroundHandlingStart(flight: Flight): void; ETTT/TOBT and TTOT are updated (milestone 8).
  operation updateTTOTAfterTSAT(flight: Flight): void; TTOT is updated following TSAT issue (milestone 10).
  operation recordAOBT(flight: Flight, aobt: TimeQuantity): void; AOBT is recorded in the Airport CDM Platform (milestone 15).
  operation recordATOT(flight: Flight, atot: TimeQuantity): void; ATOT is recorded in the Airport CDM Platform (milestone 16).

class IFPS (package Systems): The Integrated Initial Flight Plan Processing System that distributes the submitted ATC Flight Plan to all involved ATC units.
  operation distributeFlightPlan(flightPlan: FlightPlan, atcUnits: List<AirTrafficControlUnit>): void; The ATC Flight Plan is submitted by the Aircraft Operator and distributed by the IFPS; all involved ATC units receive the flight plan, including departure and destination aerodromes (milestone 1).

class ATCSystem (package Systems): The ATC/radar system (including A-SMGCS and Tower FDPS) that detects flight position and provides milestone times such as ELDT, ALDT, AIBT, AOBT and ATOT.
  operation detectMilestoneEvent(flight: Flight, event: TimeEvent): TimeQuantity; The ATC system (including A-SMGCS and Tower FDPS) or radar detects a flight based upon the assigned SSR code when it crosses a defined FIR/ATC boundary, range/position, or level, providing milestone times with an accuracy equal to that of the ATC system (milestones 4, 5, 6, 9, 15, 16).

class ACARS (package Systems): The aircraft reporting system that provides actual times such as ALDT, AIBT, AOBT and ATOT from equipped aircraft.
  operation provideActualTime(flight: Flight, event: TimeEvent): TimeQuantity; ACARS equipped aircraft provide actual times such as ALDT (ACARS=ON), AIBT (ACARS=IN), AOBT (ACARS=OUT) and ATOT (ACARS=OFF), available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute (milestones 6, 7, 15, 16).

class ETFMS (package Systems): The system used by Network Operations to monitor flight progress and issue Flight Update Messages when a flight is close to its destination.

class DPIMessage (package Messages): A Departure Planning Information message (E-DPI, T-DPI, T-DPI-t, T-DPI-s, or A-DPI) exchanged between the Airport CDM Platform and Network Operations to convey flight and take-off time estimates.
  attribute type: DPIType; The kind of Departure Planning Information message being sent (E-DPI, T-DPI, T-DPI-t, T-DPI-s or A-DPI).
  attribute scheduledOffBlockTime: DateTime; The SOBT conveyed in the E-DPI message, required to be consistent before the first E-DPI is sent (milestone 1).
  attribute aircraftRegistration: String; The aircraft registration conveyed in the E-DPI message, required to be consistent before the first E-DPI is sent (milestone 1).
  attribute destination: String; The first destination data conveyed in the E-DPI message, required to be consistent before the first E-DPI is sent (milestone 1).
  attribute takeOffTimeEstimate: DateTime; The updated take-off time estimate (ETOT/TTOT) conveyed by a T-DPI, T-DPI-t or T-DPI-s message.

class FlightUpdateMessage (package Messages): The FUM sent by Network Operations providing updates of an inbound flight's progress and estimated landing time.
