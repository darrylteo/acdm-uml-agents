# Milestone 8: Ground Handling Started

The section, split into sentences. The field name precedes each field; the id precedes each sentence.


## Definition

[M8.definition.1] Commence of Ground Handling Operations (ACGT).
[M8.definition.2] Note: this milestone is specific to flights that are the first operation of the day or that have been long term parked.
[M8.definition.3] For flights that are on a normal turn-round ACGT is considered to commence at AIBT.

## Origin and priority

[M8.origin_and_priority.1] Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler will provide the information.

## Timing

[M8.timing.1] The information is directly available after occurrence of the milestone.

## Data Quality

[M8.data_quality.1] Data is available with an accuracy of +/- 1 minute.

## Effect

[M8.effect.1] The occurrence of ACGT triggers an update of downstream estimates: TOBT is updated automatically or inserted manually by the Aircraft Operator / Ground Handler, calculated on the basis of the estimated turn-round period for the departing flight.

## Procedures

[M8.procedures.1] To check whether the AO/GH TOBT is consistent with the ATC Flight Plan.
[M8.procedures.2] Network Operations is informed when the TTOT changes by more than the agreed TTOT tolerance.
[M8.procedures.3] This check shall be performed to verify feasibility of the ATC Flight Plan given the updated TOBT or ATC Flight Plan.
[M8.procedures.4] A TTOT tolerance is respected before Network Operations is informed on updated TTOT.
[M8.procedures.5] This process is triggered by
[M8.procedures.6] - Actual Commence of Ground Handling: ACGT

## Operational Status

[M8.operational_status.1] IN-BLOCK

## Action on CDM Operation

[M8.action_on_cdm.1] ETTT/TOBT, TTOT updated
